## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

- **UK Skilled Worker 2026 threshold**: base £41,700 / going rate. Any D.E. Shaw London SWE / Quant role far exceeds.
- **CoS**: A-rated sponsor licence, historically stable in issuing CoS (but total numbers small = quota actually not the issue, bar is).
- **Internship visa path**: UK school Student visa OK; non-UK school PhD candidate, company can help sponsor Tier 5 Government Authorized Exchange or Standard Visitor PPE.
- **Graduate Route (PSW)**: UK school graduates 2-year PSW, then convert to Skilled Worker.
- **Special**: D.E. Shaw "Research" (biocomputing + drug discovery, NYC) and hedge fund entity (D.E. Shaw & Co.) are separate entities, confirm entity before applying. London is all HF entity.
- **Reference check strict**: visa approval rate high, but earlier ref check extremely picky, departing reference quality is critical.

## 3. Interview Process

| Round | Content | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 1. CV / Application Review | Recruiter screen + school + quant background | 1-2 weeks |
| 2. OA (Online Assessment) | HackerRank / Codility, 4 LeetCode medium-hard + probability/statistics concept questions | 90-120 min |
| 3. Phone Interview | 1 engineer, data structures / algorithms / project deep-dive | 60 min |
| 4. Virtual Interviews | 2-4 rounds, including coding (Python/C++/Java) + math/probability depth + system design + behavioral | half day-full day |
| 5. Case Study / Code Sample | Some roles require writing sample or code sample submission | varies |
| 6. Reference Calls | D.E. Shaw proactively contacts your provided references | 1-2 weeks |
| 7. Offer | Recruiter communicates final | - |

Average total time ~14 days (Indeed/Glassdoor data) but actual 4-8 week drag is common. OA report: "4 LeetCode medium-hards" [Source (search result): https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/gfg-academy/de-shaw-recruitment-process/].

## 6. Application Channels & Tips

- **Official application**: [deshaw.com/careers](https://www.deshaw.com/careers) — London roles clearly marked "(London)"; intern goes through [campus.deshaw.com](https://campus.deshaw.com/internships).
- **Campus recruiting deadline alert**: official page clearly states "selection is completed early in Q1 of the internship year, recommend applying by end of prior calendar year". Summer 2027 application window Sept-Dec 2026.
- **Referral**: D.E. Shaw heavily depends on network + school target list (Oxford/Cambridge/Imperial/UCL UK schools, MIT/Princeton/CMU US schools). Cold apply has lowest pass rate, school careers fair / target school events highest.
- **CV focus**:
  - Top school + high GPA (mandatory, almost no exceptions)
  - Strong math / probability background (Putnam, IMO, Kaggle Top)
  - C++ / Python performance optimization experience plus
  - PhD candidate priority Quant Analyst / Systematic Research path
- **Same-tier alternatives**: Citadel, Citadel Securities, Jane Street, HRT, Jump, Two Sigma — these are top-tier simultaneously interviewed sets. Squarepoint / Marshall Wace also commonly cross-applied in London.

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **Bar extremely high**: London hiring is small + math/probability depth extremely strong, top EU school + Putnam / IMO level experience may still fail.
- **Slow and opaque process**: official says 14 days average, actual many report 4-8 weeks + multiple ref checks.
- **Code sample / writing sample requirement**: some senior roles require, prepare a polished GitHub repo.
- **No formal grad cohort**: unlike G-Research / Man Group with structured Programme, directly enter team, learning curve steep.
- **AI tool ban** (similar to Two Sigma policy, interview is real-person supervised).
- **Glassdoor review**: 4.1/5, highly rated colleagues quality + comp, complain about work intensity + opaque bonus decisions.
- **International student friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐ — sponsorship stable but bar extremely high, small hiring volume is key bottleneck. PhD candidates significantly higher pass rate than undergrad.
- **Don't confuse**: D.E. Shaw Research (drug discovery, NYC only) ≠ D.E. Shaw & Co. (HF, London+NYC). Confirm HF entity before applying.
