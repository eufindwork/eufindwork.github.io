## 1. Basic Information

- **European office**: Amsterdam (EMEA HQ, ~400 staff) — mainly product + GTM teams; **engineering bulk has shifted to San Francisco / Austin (US)**
- **Role types**: SWE (frontend / backend / platform / full-stack), Data Engineer, ML Engineer, Product Designer, PM
- **Hiring cycle**: rolling, no fixed cohort
- **Important deadlines**: None
- **IND Recognised Sponsor**: Yes (Miro historically internationalized hiring, China/Russia/India new grad proportion high) [Source: https://workwithvisa.com/companies/miro]
- **Layer risk**: 2023 Feb (119 people reduced) + 2024 Oct (275 people global) 2 rounds of layoffs, Amsterdam affected [Source: https://miro.com/blog/a-message-from-our-ceo/], [Source: https://www.teamblind.com/post/Miro-laid-off-275-people-globally-today-YY34hnJS]

## Intern Path

> ⚠️ **Almost no public intern channel** — Miro careers currently has no intern listing; occasionally has design intern / data intern, mostly through NL universities (UvA / TU Delft) cooperation.

- **Role naming**: not fixed; occasionally Product Design Intern / Data Intern
- **Duration**: 4-6 months
- **Application window + deadline**: no public window
- **Salary (stipend gross/month)**: no public data (Miro overall salary high, intern estimate €1000-1500/month)
- **Eligibility**: NL student (default)
- **Sponsor policy (intern)**: doesn't handle work permit
- **Process**: spontaneous application + go through simplified SWE process
- **Conversion to FT rate**: no data

## NG (New Grad / Junior Full-time) Path

- **Role naming**: **SWE I** (entry-level IC), occasionally "Junior Software Engineer"; no independent cohort
- **Independent cohort program**: **No**
- **Starting salary (base + bonus + equity)** (Levels.fyi Greater Amsterdam):
  - **SWE I**: **total €133K** (base €115K + stock €18K + bonus €0) [Source (verified opened): https://www.levels.fyi/companies/miro/salaries/software-engineer/locations/greater-amsterdam-area]
  - **SWE II**: total €119K (base €107K + stock €11.9K + bonus €187)
  - Senior SWE: total €149K (base €139K + stock €10.1K)
  - **median Amsterdam**: €128K (40 data points) [Source: https://techpays.com/europe/netherlands/miro]
  - **Equity**: 4-year vesting, 25% annually (RSU, pre-IPO valuation locked, exit depends on IPO)
- **Sponsor policy (FT)**: NL Kennismigrant completely no obstacles. SWE I base €115K = €9,580/month, **well above** <30 HSM threshold €4,357.
- **Application window**: year-round rolling
- **Process differences vs intern**: intern barely exists; NG SWE I goes through standard 4-5 round process

## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **Supports non-EU students**: Yes (IND Recognised Sponsor)
- **Intern vs FT sponsor differences**:
  - Intern: doesn't handle
  - FT: handles Kennismigrant + relocation
- **Known sponsorship cases**: 
  - workwithvisa.com lists Miro
  - Miro historical Perm (Russia) office many Russian engineers relocated to Amsterdam (after 2022)
- **2026 thresholds**: NL Kennismigrant <30 €4,357 / 30+ €5,942 / **NL master grad reduced €2,801**
- **30% ruling**: SWE I base €115K fully meets condition, overseas hiring + residence ≥150km can apply. **From 2024 5-year ladder reduction** (30%→27%→20%→15%)

## 6. Application Channels and Recommendations

- **Official application**: https://miro.com/careers/
- **Referral channels**: Many former Perm office engineers in Amsterdam, LinkedIn referral; Russian-speaking community tight
- **Resume/background requirements**:
  - **Strong frontend** (React, TypeScript, Canvas / WebGL) **or** strong backend (Java / Kotlin, distributed systems)
  - SaaS / collaboration tools experience bonus
  - **Russian not required**, English fluent
- **Recommendations**:
  - **Carefully assess layoff risk** — company continues restructuring, 2024 Oct already laid off 275 people, 2026 internal still rumors of fear of next round
  - SWE I €133K total is one of Amsterdam tech top tier pay (on par with Stripe / Datadog), but with risk premium
  - **Engineering bulk already moved to SF / Austin**, Amsterdam mainly product + GTM + some engineering — pay attention to JD writing "EMEA team" vs "global team"

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

- **Glassdoor Amsterdam**: rating mixed (overall 3.6/5, dipped to 3.2 during layoff period)
- **Pros**: high pay, internationalized, EMEA HQ decision power
- **Cons (serious)**:
  - **Continuous layoff shadow** — "fearful of additional layoffs (the first round of layoffs were treated extremely poorly) and now looking for new jobs" [Source (verified opened): https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Miro-Amsterdam-Reviews-EI_IE2106300.0,4_IL.5,14_IM1112.htm]
  - "first round of layoffs treated extremely poorly"
  - Amsterdam reviews mention "bias towards people/friends who are from Perm (Russia) during layoffs and promotions"
  - **Engineering decision power shifted to US**, Amsterdam team influence declining
- **Salary vs Adyen/Booking**: SWE I €133K total has already significantly exceeded Booking Compass Grad (€74K), comparable to Datadog / Stripe Amsterdam
- **Equity**: pre-IPO, 4-yr vest 25%/yr; IPO timeline unclear

## 8. Specific Assessment for UvA Students (NL student visa) ⭐

- **Intern feasibility**: **Low** — no public intern channel; even if you can get stage, company hiring slow during layoff period. Student visa technically covers, but value is limited
- **FT feasibility**: **Zero friction (technically) / Medium (risk)** — UvA master graduates go through zoekjaar then apply SWE I, base €115K well above reduced HSM €2,801, also significantly above standard HSM. Company is IND sponsor, mature process. **But layoff risk needs assessment**
- **Expected friction**:
  - Intern: high (no channel)
  - FT: low (technically zero friction, but weigh layoff risk)
- **UvA advantage**:
  - Miro Amsterdam HQ at Herengracht (canal district), **UvA campus walk / bike 10-15 minutes**
  - UvA CS alumni in Miro have some numbers
- **Recommended application time window**:
  - rolling, year-round
  - **Recommend as high-base backup**, not primary target — prioritize Adyen / Booking / Mollie / JetBrains stable companies
