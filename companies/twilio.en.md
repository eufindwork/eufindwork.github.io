## 1. Basic Information

- **European offices**: Dublin (primary engineering, ~600 employees), London (sales / some eng), Madrid (after acquiring Zipwhip), Tallinn
- **Role types**:
  - SWE (Python / Go / Java backend + Voice/Messaging APIs + SendGrid email infra)
  - Data Eng / ML / Data Scientist
  - SRE / Cloud Platform
  - Product / Customer Engineering (large portion)
  - **AI/Conversational pivot** (Twilio Engage, Flex AI focus) — 2026 important direction
- **Hiring cycle**: rolling, sporadic cohort grad / intern (depends on Talent budget)
- **Important deadlines**: see JD case by case, **no official public grad program**

## Intern Track

- **Role names**: **Software Engineering Intern** / **Engineering Intern** (Dublin) — **roles rare**, 2025-2026 cycle seen mostly in US (Denver, SF), Dublin/London sporadic
- **Duration**: 10-12 weeks Summer (Jun-Aug)
- **Application window + deadline**: 2026 Summer application window 2026-02 → 2026-04 (rolling); 2027 Summer application 2027-01 → 2027-03
- **Salary (stipend gross/mo)**:
  - **Dublin intern**: €3,000-3,500/mo (based on Twilio US SWE intern **$47/hr ≈ $8K/mo** conversion + Dublin tech intern market [Source (verified opened): https://www.levels.fyi/internships/Twilio/Software-Engineer-Intern/])
  - **London intern**: £3,000-3,800/mo (Glassdoor 4 SWE intern samples 25th-75th $64K-$113K/yr → £3K-£4K/month converted)
- **Eligibility (education, local residency)**:
  - Enrolled BSc / MSc CS
  - Priority IE/UK university partnerships, international student applications accepted (Twilio historical sponsor)
- **Sponsor policy (intern)**:
  - **Dublin < 90 days**: Atypical Working Scheme, permit-exempt
  - **Dublin ≥ 90 days**: requires GEP
  - **London intern**: UK Skilled Worker intern few, Twilio historically doesn't sponsor intern in UK
- **Process**: OA → recruiter → 1 tech (coding + system) → final panel
- **Conversion rate**: Twilio New Grad Track mainly from intern conversion, conversion ~50-60% (community estimate)

## NG (New Grad / Junior Full-Time) Track

- **Role names**:
  - **Software Engineering New Graduate Track** — direct placement to product team (Programmable Voice / SendGrid email / Flex contact center)
  - **Software Engineer L1 / L2** — some teams hire entry-level
- **Independent cohort program**: ⚠️ has New Grad Track but not annual regular cohort, depends on Talent budget
- **Starting salary (base + bonus + equity)**:
  - **Dublin IC2 (NG)**: median TC **€100K** (Twilio Dublin range starting point) [Levels.fyi Twilio Dublin (verified opened): https://www.levels.fyi/companies/twilio/salaries/software-engineer/locations/greater-dublin-area]
  - **Dublin IC3 (mid)**: median TC **€144K**
  - **Dublin overall**: €100K - €226K (IC2-IC5)
  - **Ireland**: same Dublin range, median €100K [Levels.fyi Twilio Ireland (verified opened): https://www.levels.fyi/companies/twilio/salaries/software-engineer/locations/ireland]
  - IC2 breakdown: base ~€75K + €10K bonus + €15K RSU/year ≈ €100K TC
  - + RSU (US listed, $TWLO 2024-2026 large fluctuation, equity value varies a lot)
- **Sponsor policy (FT)**: ✅ explicit sponsor — IE CSEP active [Nextleveljobs IE Visa Sponsorship (verified opened): https://nextleveljobs.eu/blog/visa-sponsorship/ie]
- **Application window**: rolling, year-round open (depending on hiring freeze status)
- **Process differences vs intern**: NG adds system design + 1 culture / bar raiser round

## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **Supports non-EU students**: ✅ Yes (FT Dublin); intern depends on duration
- **Intern vs FT sponsor differences**:
  - **Intern < 90 days Dublin**: permit-exempt
  - **Intern ≥ 90 days**: GEP, Twilio rarely sponsors intern
  - **FT**: CSEP, 2-4 weeks, Twilio covers
  - **London FT**: UK Skilled Worker, Twilio London rarely sponsors (sales-led, eng team shrunk)
- **Known sponsorship cases**: Twilio Dublin many international engineers, but **after 2024-2026 multiple rounds of layoffs hiring trending cautious**; 2025-2026 Dublin new roles mostly senior+
- **2026 thresholds**:
  - **Ireland CSEP**: €40,904/year (starting 2026-03) — IC2 base €75K+ far exceeds
  - **UK Skilled Worker**: £41,700 (NEC £49,400) — London IC2 base estimated £75K+ meets

## 3. Interview Process

| Stage | Content | Duration |
|------|------|------|
| 1. OA | HackerRank: 2 LC medium (Python/Java/Go) + 1 system design lite | 90 min |
| 2. Recruiter call | TA, motivation, visa, comp expectation | 30 min |
| 3. Tech 1 - Coding | Live coding + API design (RESTful, idempotency, rate limiting) | 60 min |
| 4. Tech 2 - System Design | Design messaging service / SMS routing / SendGrid-like email pipeline | 60 min |
| 5. Hire manager + Bar raiser | Twilio "9 Things": Be Inclusive, Build Trust, etc. | 45 min |

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

- **2022-2026 multiple rounds of layoffs**: 2022 Q4 layoff 11%, 2023 layoff 17% (1500+ people), 2024 restructured closed SF HQ, **2026 further layoff Communications + AI departments** [KORE1 Twilio Layoffs 2026 (search-confirmed): https://www.kore1.com/twilio-layoffs-2026/]
- **Segment department**: 2020 acquired $3.2B, 2024 wind-down some teams, seniors largely flowed to Resend / Apollo. **SendGrid department relatively stable**, still building.
- **Dublin office status**: still EMEA engineering primary, but hiring cautious; 2025-2026 new roles lean senior
- **Stock fluctuation**: $TWLO 2022 $300 → 2024 $50, 2026 ~$80-110, RSU value unstable; salary negotiation focus on base + bonus
- **AI pivot**: Twilio 2026 heavily betting on AI conversational + Flex AI, **new roles lean AI / ML / agent**, traditional messaging eng slots reduced
- **WLB**: Glassdoor overall 3.5/5 (Dublin), comments: "great mission early, lost focus 2023+"; on-call rotation heavy (24/7 messaging infra)
- **Remote flexibility**: Twilio was "remote-first" before, 2024 after some teams require hybrid, Dublin expected 3 day/week

## 8. For UvA Students (NL Student Visa) — Specific Assessment ⭐

### Intern feasibility
- **Summer Intern Dublin (< 90 days)**: ⚠️ medium feasibility — IE Atypical Working Scheme passable, but **Twilio Dublin intern roles themselves rare (2025-2026 mainly in US)**, slots are the real bottleneck
- **London intern**: ❌ high friction — UK Skilled Worker intern rare, Twilio London leans sales/customer eng, intern slots almost none

### FT feasibility
- **Graduate IC2 SWE Dublin**: ⚠️ medium feasibility — IE CSEP passable, IC2 base €75K+ far exceeds €40,904 threshold; **but 2025-2026 entry-level/NG roles very few**, hiring freeze cyclical
- **NL zoekjaar conflict**: Twilio has **no office** in NL, user needs to give up zoekjaar
- **Stock risk**: $TWLO upside limited, equity cannot serve as main comp expectation

### Estimated friction
- Intern: **medium-high** (policy OK but slots scarce)
- FT: **medium** (policy OK, but 2026 hiring cautious, slots are bottleneck)

### Recommendations
- **Not as primary path** — Twilio 2024-2026 restructuring causes grad/intern slots thin, low cost-effectiveness
- **If must try**: (1) Summer Intern Dublin continuously monitor Greenhouse, **apply immediately when 2027-01 application window opens** (rolling); (2) graduate FT look at SendGrid department (email infra relatively stable), skip Communications/AI departments (layoff risk)
- **Alternatives** (higher priority):
  - **HubSpot Dublin** — same IE CSEP sponsor, many more intern/grad slots, more stable company
  - **Workday Dublin** — also Trusted Partner, structured Graduate Program
  - **Stripe Dublin** — already in repo, same fintech quality
- **Timeline**: if must be Twilio, focus apply 2026 Q4 / 2027 Q1, prepare fallback options
