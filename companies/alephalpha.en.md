## 3. Interview Process

> Glassdoor aggregate: difficulty 3/5, 44% positive — medium difficulty, relatively fast process (timely feedback)

- **Total rounds**: ~3-4 rounds (PhD Intern) / 4-5 rounds (full-time)
- **Each round format** (based on Glassdoor Heidelberg 2024-10 SWE interview + industry NLP/LLM company standard):
  1. **Recruiter Screening** (60 min): role + visa + research interest alignment
  2. **Team Introduction Call** (30-45 min): meet potential team member, casual introduction + Q&A
  3. **Technical Interview** (90 min): candidate introduces past work / projects + each part followed by technical depth questions ("first interview is already half technical" — Glassdoor quote) [Source: https://eujobs.co/career-guides/aleph-alpha-gmbh-career-guide]
  4. **Research Deep Dive / Coding** (60-90 min):
     - PhD Intern: existing research project deep dive + discussion of alignment with Aleph Alpha team mission
     - Full-time: Python + PyTorch implementation question (transformer / training loop / sampling / RAG)
  5. **Culture / Leadership Chat** (45 min): mission alignment (sovereign EU AI, multilingual LLM values)
- **Question type distribution**:
  - PhD Intern: Research / paper deep dive (50%) / programming implementation (30%) / domain (LLM training / multilingual) (20%)
  - Full-time SWE: Coding + PyTorch (40%) / system design (LLM inference / RAG / model evaluation) (30%) / Domain (NLP / LLM) (20%) / BQ (10%)
- **Difficulty assessment**: Medium - Medium-Hard; LeetCode hard proportion low, heavy on ML / NLP concept depth
- **OA exists**: no standardized OA; occasional take-home (e.g. fine-tune a small model + report)
- **Process speed**: Glassdoor feedback "fast and reactive with feedback after each stage" — among DE tech companies, on the faster side

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

### Process level
- **"First interview already half technical"** — don't be lulled by recruiter call label, prepare to discuss past work technical details
- **Industrial PhD application cycle long**: 3-6 months conversation + topic alignment + university advisor matching, not routine fast hire
- **Referral nearly mandatory**: direct cold apply pass rate low, find internal referral for significant boost

### Culture / business risk
- **Strategic transformation + 2024-2025 layoff**: company shifted from pure base model (competing with OpenAI/Anthropic) to Enterprise GenAI platform + sovereign AI, some Research team shrunk; research current hiring status before applying
- **Sovereign AI positioning**: main customers German government / banks / large manufacturers, business pace slower than pure research lab but commercial stability strong
- **Heidelberg geographic location**: college town, high international level but not Berlin/Munich big city, infrastructure suitable for long-term living
- **Multilingual model vs English SOTA**: Pharia series multilingual benchmark strong but English head-to-head with GPT-4 / Claude still has gap

### Known "pitfalls"
1. **Nextleveljobs sponsor list not included**: indirect signal company sponsor pace not as Trade Republic / Celonis public; confirm via email before applying
2. **Levels.fyi data very few (n=3-5)**: lack benchmark for salary negotiation
3. **PhD Intern must be 3rd year+**: 1st/2nd year PhD don't waste time
4. **Strategic transformation period**: team / product direction may adjust after onboarding, mental preparation
5. **German friendly**: working environment English-primary, but German B1+ has significant advantage in GTM / Enterprise sales roles

### International student friendliness
- **EU national + ML / NLP PhD**: extremely friendly
- **PhD top conference first-author + multilingual NLP**: friendly, Industrial PhD is rare channel
- **Chinese-national master / new grad**: medium friendly — cases indirect but channel exists
- **Chinese-national PhD / Industrial PhD candidate**: high potential — directly apply, referral boost
