## Intern Path

- **Role naming**: **Intern** or **Working Student** — occasional, usually 4-6 months; Pleo **has no formal intern programme**, hiring focuses on mid-level
- **Length**: 4-6 months (CPH mostly thesis intern)
- **Application window + deadline**: rolling, depends on team needs
- **Salary (stipend gross/month)**: Denmark intern usually DKK 18,000-25,000/month ≈ **€2,400-3,400/month** (based on Glassdoor Copenhagen tech intern range — Pleo specific cases few, pending web re-verification)
- **Eligibility (education, local residency requirement)**: enrolled MSc preferred, mostly DTU / CBS / KU local students thesis collaboration
- **Sponsor policy (intern)**: **does not proactively sponsor intern visa** — Pay Limit Scheme requires €69.6K annual salary, intern doesn't meet; Denmark alternative: **Researcher visa** (academic project) or student internship via original student visa
- **Process**: apply → 1 recruiter call → 1 hire manager → 1 tech (small take-home)
- **Conversion to FT rate**: data sparse, not publicly disclosed (case estimate 30-50%)

> Pleo intern public info still extremely scarce (2026-06 retry: pleo.io/en/careers has no separate intern section, Glassdoor Pleo Copenhagen intern salary page data missing); this section based on Glassdoor + Reddit + Denmark tech intern prevailing levels inference, before applying directly email Pleo HR (talent@pleo.io) to confirm.

## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **Supports non-EU students**: ✅ yes (FT), intern generally not
- **Intern vs FT sponsor differences**:
  - **Intern**: does not sponsor, international students need to bring DK student status or via Researcher visa
  - **FT**: Pay Limit Scheme, Pleo covers full costs, 2-3 months to issue
- **Known sponsorship cases**: Relocate.me Backend SWE JD explicit, Reddit r/Denmark has Pleo international student cases [verified opened: https://relocate.me/denmark/copenhagen/pleo/backend-software-engineer-1089]
- **2026 thresholds**:
  - **Denmark Pay Limit Scheme**: **DKK 519,224/year (€69.6K, 2026)** — Pleo Junior SWE starting salary just meets
  - **Fast-Track Scheme**: company needs certified (Pleo is), 30-day processing
  - **Positive List**: software engineer in list, threshold drops to DKK 380,000 ≈ €51K — but Pleo uses Pay Limit more commonly
  - **Beneficial Researcher tax scheme**: first 7 years 32.84% tax rate (vs ordinary ~55%), threshold ~DKK 75K/month (Pleo Senior+ to meet)

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

- **2023 layoff**: Pleo 2023 Q2 layoff ~15% (115 people), 2024 H2 another round; 2025 hiring recovered but leans senior. **Entry-level roles very scarce**, this is user's biggest obstacle.
- **Down round**: 2024 internal valuation lowered ~30% (vs $4.7B 2021); equity upside expectation reduced
- **Glassdoor rating**: 3.9/5 (CPH), complaints: scope ambiguity + cross-timezone collaboration (with London/Madrid teams)
- **Take-home length**: Anteus 2-5 days workload, international student competition needs all-in effort
- **Pay Limit risk**: if negotiated to DKK 510K (below €69.6K threshold), Pay Limit not approved; must negotiate to ≥ DKK 520K
- **CPH cost of living**: rent 1bd ~DKK 12K/month (€1,610), after-tax take-home significantly reduced (tax rate ~50%)

## 8. For UvA Students (NL student visa) — Specific Assessment ⭐

### Intern viability
- **Copenhagen intern**: ❌ high friction — Pleo almost doesn't open intern roles (hiring focuses mid+), even if opened doesn't sponsor intern visa.
- **Workaround**: via thesis project — UvA + DTU/CBS partner thesis, user short-term to CPH; but Pleo cases extremely few

### FT viability
- **Post-graduation Junior SWE Copenhagen**: ⚠️ medium viable — obstacle is **Pleo entry-level roles scarce**, not a sponsor issue. Once getting offer, Pay Limit Scheme works (Junior starting salary €72K+ meets threshold).
- **NL zoekjaar conflict**: Pleo has **no office in NL**, user needs to give up zoekjaar to go via Denmark Pay Limit. Pay Limit gives 4-year residence + work permit, spouse can work.
- **Tax**: Denmark tax ~50% (vs NL ~38% with 30% ruling), take-home actually possibly lower than equivalent NL base; but base ~20% higher offsets most

### Expected friction
- Intern: **high** (Pleo doesn't hire intern + doesn't sponsor)
- FT: **medium** (Pay Limit process smooth, but entry-level role scarcity is real bottleneck)

### Suggestions
- **Don't recommend Pleo as main path** — entry-level opportunities few, user better waiting until post-graduation with 1-2 years experience
- **If must try**: (1) frequently browse Pleo Lever for Junior tag (frontend has more entry-level than backend); (2) Refer through DTU / CBS alumni network; (3) prepare Kotlin/Spring project portfolio
- **Alternatives**: similar field prioritize **N26 (Berlin, EU Blue Card)** or **Adyen (AMS, NL zero friction)** — overlapping product with Pleo, but much more user-friendly
- **Timeline**: if Pleo-only, continuously monitor 6 months pre-graduation, Pay Limit Scheme 4-8 weeks to issue
