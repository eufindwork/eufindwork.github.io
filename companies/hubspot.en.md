- **Role types**: SWE (Java/Kotlin backend + React/TS frontend + iOS/Android), Product Mgmt, UX Design, Data Eng, Data Scientist, Sales / Marketing / Customer Success (large share)
- **Hiring cycle**: Emerging Talent has clear cohort timing
  - **Spring Co-op**: Jan-Apr 2026 start, apply previous Fall (Sep-Nov)
  - **Summer Internship**: May/Jun-Aug start, apply same year Spring (Jan-Mar)
  - **Industrial Placement** (12 mo, Ireland-only): Aug start, apply previous Fall
- **Important deadlines**: Spring 2026 closed; Summer 2026 application window 2026-01 → 2026-03 (historical pattern); Spring 2027 applications open 2026-09

## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

- **Whether non-EU students supported**: Yes (FT, IE Trusted Partner status); intern depends on duration
- **Intern vs FT sponsor differences**:
  - **Intern < 90 days (Summer)**: Atypical Working Scheme, no permit needed, HubSpot only assists with agreement
  - **Intern ≥ 90 days (Co-op / Placement)**: requires General Employment Permit, HubSpot rarely sponsors non-IE university students
  - **FT**: CSEP, IE Trusted Partner 2-4 week fast track, Stamp 1 → 2 yrs renewal → Stamp 4 PR clear path
- **Known sponsorship cases**: nextleveljobs lists HubSpot as IE active sponsor; LinkedIn shows multiple India/China international student Dublin FT cases
- **2026 thresholds**:
  - **Ireland CSEP**: **€40,904/year (from 2026-03)** — Dublin Associate SWE €60K+ far exceeds
  - Spouse can work (Stamp 1 spouse automatic Stamp 1G)
  - After 21 months can apply for Stamp 4 (5-year PR path)

## 3. Interview Process

| Stage | Content | Duration |
|------|------|------|
| 1. OA / Coding challenge | HireVue video questions + 2 LC medium (Java/Python optional) | 90 min |
| 2. Recruiter call | TA, motivation, visa status | 30 min |
| 3. Tech 1 - Coding | 1-hour pair with engineer, real-world refactor (not LC algorithm) | 60 min |
| 4. Tech 2 - System Design | design marketing/CRM related system (e.g. email scheduling, lead scoring) | 60 min |
| 5. Hire manager + Culture | "HEART" values: Humble, Empathetic, Adaptable, Remarkable, Transparent | 45 min |

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **Cambridge MA is HQ**: key technical decisions in Boston, Dublin/Berlin are satellites (though large); some teams have cross-timezone collaboration fatigue
- **Marketing/Sales-driven culture**: HubSpot is CRM/marketing SaaS, eng team ~15%, engineering role proportion isn't large
- **Layoffs**: 2024 Q1 layoff ~7% (~500 headcount), 2025 stable; eng minimally affected
- **HEART culture screening**: HubSpot heavily values culture fit, "remarkable" / "transparent" are interviewer scoring items — prepare behavioral well
- **Industrial Placement international student restrictions**: 12 mo placement prefers IE university students (TCD/UCD/DCU etc.) currently enrolled, international universities need to confirm
- **Dublin office scale advantage**: ~1000+ employees, active Chinese/Indian colleague communities
- **Glassdoor overall 4.3/5** (Dublin), positive reviews

## 8. Specific Assessment for UvA Students (NL student visa) ⭐

### Intern Feasibility
- **Summer Intern Dublin (May-Aug, < 90 days 12 weeks boundary)**: Medium-high feasibility — IE Atypical Working Scheme allows no permit within 90 days, UvA students coming to Dublin in summer **don't need sponsor**, only short-stay visa + HubSpot agreement. **Most realistic path for user**
- **Spring Co-op (6 mo) Dublin**: ⚠️ High friction — exceeds 90 days, requires General Employment Permit, HubSpot rarely sponsors non-IE university students
- **Industrial Placement (12 mo)**: Almost not feasible — strongly prefers IE university partnerships
- **Berlin intern**: ⚠️ Same as Personio section — requires enrollment at DE university

### FT Feasibility
- **Post-graduation Associate SWE Dublin**: Highly feasible — IE CSEP 4-8 weeks, HubSpot Trusted Partner fast processing; €60K+ starting salary >> €40,904 threshold
- **Post-graduation Associate SWE Berlin**: Feasible — EU Blue Card €45,934 threshold, HubSpot operates this often
- **NL zoekjaar conflict**: HubSpot has **no office in NL**, user needs to give up zoekjaar; but IE CSEP / Stamp 4 PR 5-year path is more lenient than NL Kennismigrant 5-year PR

### Estimated friction
- Summer Intern Dublin: **low** (Atypical Working Scheme works)
- Spring Co-op / Industrial Placement: **high**
- FT Dublin: **low** (Trusted Partner)
- FT Berlin: **low-medium**

### Recommendations
- **First choice**: **Summer 2027 Intern Dublin** (applications open 2027-01, 12 weeks via Atypical Working Scheme) → convert to FT
  - Timeline: prepare resume + LeetCode from 2026-08; submit application first week of 2027-01 (rolling)
- **Second choice**: post-graduation apply for Associate SWE Dublin (rolling, IE CSEP sponsor)
- **Avoid**: Spring Co-op / Industrial Placement, friction not worth it
- **Alternative**: Berlin office also possible, but EU Blue Card is more complex than IE CSEP, Dublin is the better path
