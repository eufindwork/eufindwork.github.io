## Intern 路径

- **岗位命名**:
  - **Applied Science Internship 2026** [来源 (实际打开过): https://careers.wolt.com/en/jobs/1/7552472] — 生产级 AI/ML 系统
  - **Product Management Internship 2026** [来源 (实际打开过): https://www.aaltobt.fi/wolt-product-management-internship-2026/]
  - Software Engineering Internship 也定期开放
- **时长**: **6 mo**, 2026-05 启动
- **申请窗口 + deadline**: 2026 Applied Science assignment deadline 已过 (2026-02-08); 下一轮预计 2026 秋开放 2027 cohort
- **薪资 (stipend gross/月)**: **€3,300/月** (Applied Science 2026 confirmed)
- **资格 (学历, 当地居留要求)**:
  - MSc / 末学期 BSc CS / Data Science / ML
  - **必须已在 Finland (优 Capital Region) + 持有效工签**
- **Sponsor 政策 (intern)**: ❌ **明文不 sponsor** —
  > "Wolt is unfortunately unable to offer relocation or visa support for internships, so you should be eligible to work in Finland for the duration of the program. However, if you're offered to stay with us on a permanent basis after the internship, they'll take care of your work visa if that's needed." [来源 (实际打开过): https://careers.wolt.com/en/jobs/1/7552472]
- **流程**: take-home assignment → recruiter → hiring manager → technical → team fit
- **转 FT 率**: 高 — 官方明确转 FT 时 sponsor visa; 是 6 mo intern 的核心 retention hook

## 8. 对 UvA 学生 (NL student visa) 的具体评估 ⭐

- **核心 trade-off**: 已在 NL 持学生签 → 选 Wolt 意味着 **放弃 NL zoekjaar (€2,801 reduced HSM)**, 换 Finland Blue Card / specialist permit
- **Intern 可行性**: ❌ **零** — Wolt 明文不 sponsor intern + 需 FI 已有工签; **跳过 intern, 直接走 FT**
- **FT 可行性**:
  - **Helsinki SWE entry → 高** — Wolt €99K 远超 Finland Blue Card €47,244 阈值; FT sponsor 明文 + DoorDash mobility 流程
  - **Stockholm / Berlin office → 不要投 R&D** (这两个 office 主要 ops, tech 大头在 Helsinki)
- **预计摩擦**:
  - Intern → 高到不可行
  - FT → 低 (sponsor 明文 + 起薪宽松 cover 阈值)
- **UvA 优势失效**: 这家不在 NL, 无 zoekjaar 福利
- **建议**:
  - **若你不打算搬 Helsinki — Wolt 不值得投 (intern 不行, FT 必须人到岗)**
  - **若你愿意去 Helsinki — Wolt 是 Finland tech 第一梯队, 起薪 €99K 是 Helsinki 顶级, FT sponsor 政策明确, 推荐投**
  - Intern 路径完全跳过, 直接申请 entry-level SWE / Applied Scientist FT 岗
