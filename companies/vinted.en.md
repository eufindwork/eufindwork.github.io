- **Hiring cycle**: rolling (Senior) + **Engineering Academy / Data Academy / Marketing Tech Academy annual cohort**

## Intern Path

- **Role name**:
  - **Vinted Engineering Academy** — nominally "Engineering Academy" starts as FT contract, but first 2 months intensive training nature similar to intern (paid starting at €2,233/month)
  - Occasionally Web Engineer Internship (within Academy system) [Source: https://startup.jobs/web-engineer-internship-vinted-academy-vinted-1978767]
- **Duration**: Engineering Academy: **6 months** (2 months technical induction + 4 months on team)
- **Application window + deadline**: usually opens Jan-Mar, June start (Vilnius)
- **Salary (stipend gross/month)**: **€2,233/month gross (junior engineer starting salary)** — this is Academy starting salary, not traditional stipend [Source: https://vinted.engineering/2023/02/22/vinted-engineering-academy-2023/]
- **Eligibility**:
  - Programming foundation (any language) + strong learning will
  - **CS / engineering degree preferred but not required**
  - **Vilnius onboarding (default)**
- **Sponsor policy (intern → Academy)**: **Yes** — Vinted relocation page explicitly supports visa: non-EU candidates from outside Schengen get **€6,000 (solo) / €10,000 (family)** relocation subsidy [Source (verified opened): https://careers.vinted.com/relocate-to-lithuania]
- **Process**: Application → CV review → technical OA → 1-2 rounds technical interview → culture interview → offer
- **Conversion rate to FT**: **100%** (Academy = FT contract, completion means staying as junior engineer)

## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

- **Does it support non-EU students**: **Yes (strong)** — Vinted is LT's main international employer, 100+ already relocated
- **Intern vs FT sponsor differences**:
  - Vinted Academy = FT contract, uses LT work visa
  - Regular IC = LT work visa / EU Blue Card
  - Amsterdam: Kennismigrant (but NL role scarce)
- **Known sponsorship cases**:
  - Official relocation page: €6K (solo) / €10K (family) for non-EU, €3K / €5K for EU [Source (verified opened): https://careers.vinted.com/relocate-to-lithuania]
  - "select positions cover first 2 months temporary housing + kindergarten/school search"
  - **Limited-time €30K bonus** for select positions (senior roles)
- **2026 thresholds**:
  - **LT**: National Visa / EU Blue Card LT threshold ≈ €2,178/month gross (1.2x avg, 2025 data), Academy €2,233 just satisfies
  - **NL** (if applying Amsterdam): Kennismigrant <30 €4,357 / 30+ €5,942 / reduced €2,801
- **Lithuanian language**: not required (Vinted internal work language English)

## 8. For UvA Students (NL student visa) — Specific Assessment ⭐

- **Intern feasibility**: **Low-medium** — Vinted Amsterdam office extremely small (1 role open, Data Science), eng intern channel not in NL. **If wanting to do Vinted intern, must accept Vilnius relocate** — but hard to commit to LT during UvA studies
- **FT feasibility**: **Medium** (complex) — path depends on preference:
  - **Path A (Vilnius relocate)**: After graduation apply Engineering Academy / Junior SWE, Vinted handles LT work visa + €6K relocation subsidy. **Cons**: leaves NL, zoekjaar wasted, NL PR progress reset
  - **Path B (Amsterdam)**: Wait for Amsterdam eng role to open (rare), low probability. Data Scientist occasional — if user is ML / DS direction can consider
- **Estimated friction**:
  - Intern: high (location mismatch)
  - FT: medium-high (need to choose location path)
- **UvA advantage**:
  - If choosing LT path: UvA CS degree competitive at Vinted
  - LT alumni at Vinted mainly local, weak UvA external network
- **Suggested application time window**:
  - Engineering Academy 2027 cohort: **2026-12 – 2027-03** watch for opening
  - **Suggest Vinted as backup / springboard**, not user's main target (because deviates from NL long-term path)
