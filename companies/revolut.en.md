# Revolut (London) Internship Intel

> **Language**: [中文](revolut.md) | English
>
> Revolut is the UK's highest-valued fintech unicorn (~$45B), and obtained the full UK Banking Licence in 2024. Known for its "high-intensity culture", its intern and full-time bars are among the most strict in the London market. **Long interview process, high pressure, includes IQ tests, with polarized Glassdoor culture reviews (Toxic label prominent)** — research before applying.

## 3. Application Requirements & Visa

- **Education**: Penultimate year, master's also accepted; top school recruiting prefers Oxbridge / IC / UCL / Warwick / top overseas
- **Core hard requirement**: 1+ relevant internship (fintech/bank/defense/healthcare) → officially stated, candidates without relevant internship rarely accepted
- **Visa**: Revolut Ltd is Skilled Worker licensed employer, interns mainly use Student Visa; after grad conversion sponsor Skilled Worker
- **Salary (Glassdoor)**:
  - Intern: average £23,250 / year pro-rated, range £16.9k - £29.8k
  - Grad SWE: ~£60-70k Total Comp
  - Internship is *paid* (recent years onwards, occasional early unpaid initial stages reported)

## 4. Interview Process (5-6 stages)

```
1. Recruiter Screen (15-30 min)
   → background, motivation, why Revolut

2. HackerRank OA (60-90 min)
   → 2-4 questions DSA + SQL + some IQ / math puzzles

3. Technical Live Coding (45-60 min)
   → own IDE, implement a small service / 2-3 algorithm questions

4. System Design (for Senior; intern typically merged into LC + SOLID)
   → rate limiter, event tracking, chat system

5. Team Fit / Bar Raiser (Senior leader)
   → culture alignment, long-term value, leadership check
```

Total time **3-6 weeks**, Pass rate per most public samples ~**30-40%**.

## 6. Culture & Reputation — **Key Warnings**

### Recurring Negative Signals on Glassdoor / Blind / Reddit

- **"Toxic culture"** recurring — high intensity, long hours (60-70h common), strict PIP (performance improvement)
- **Founder Nik Storonsky's strict style** — weekly reports, extremely quantified goal management, weekend OT default
- **Turnover** — some teams over 50% turnover in 18 months
- **Senior engineer reviews**: "Good pay, but serious problems with culture"
- **ML engineer reviews (relatively positive)**: "Fast-paced, lots of impact"

### Positive Signals

- High pay, strong brand, high CV value
- Big project responsibility, real ownership
- High probability of converting / jumping to IB / FAANG next

> Overall evaluation: **"Shiny CV brand, but need strong physical and mental preparation during work period"**. Suitable for those wanting credential stacking and high-intensity testing, caution for students who prioritize WLB.

## 7. Key Preparation Checklist

1. **HackerRank high-frequency**: LC Medium algorithm (Top 150) + SQL Window Function + 1-2 brain teasers
2. **Java/Kotlin memory model**: GC, JVM, concurrency (ConcurrentHashMap, ThreadPool)
3. **SOLID principles deep dive**: must be able to explain each + real code examples
4. **DB basics**: indexes, transaction isolation levels, PostgreSQL EXPLAIN
5. **"Why Revolut"**: must have specific answer — used product, follow which business line
6. **Behavioral questions**: repeatedly practice "how you complete deliverable under pressure"
