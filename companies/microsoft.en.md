### 1.2 Main intern programs

1. **Microsoft Global University Internship (main program)**: Bachelor's / Master's / MBA / PhD in progress, 12 weeks, summer; global incl. Europe
2. **Microsoft Research Internship (mainly Cambridge UK)**: 12 weeks; Master/PhD preferred, occasional undergrad; "Many of our interns have gone on to get full-time positions here or at other Microsoft locations"
3. **12-month Placement (UK specialty, mainly Reading)**: 1 full year (sandwich year); £20,000/year + 25 days holiday
4. **Microsoft Garage Intern**: in Cambridge MA / Redmond / Vancouver / Dublin / Belgrade / Israel etc.; interdisciplinary prototype projects, **no Cambridge UK or Reading**
5. **Explore Microsoft (freshman/sophomore exclusive)**: **only US / Canada / Mexico (US/CAN/MEX), India has a shortened version**; **Europe basically no Explore program** ⚠️
6. **Discovery Program**: post-high school / pre-freshman exploration program, mainly North America

### 1.3 Key distinction: Explore vs Full SWE Intern

| Program | Explore Microsoft | Microsoft Global University Internship (SWE) |
|------|-------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Target group | 1st/2nd year undergrad (mostly CS beginners) | Any Bachelor / Master / MBA / PhD |
| Duration | 12 weeks (US) / 8 weeks (India) | 12 weeks (global) |
| Work style | Small group (3 people SWE/PM/Designer pairing) on sponsor project | Individual 1 project + 1 mentor |
| Learning focus | Engineering vs PM vs Design path exploration, role rotation | role-specific depth |
| Europe open | **No (US/Canada/Mexico only; India independent program)** | **Yes (London/Cambridge UK/Amsterdam/Munich/Dublin/Paris/Stockholm)** |
| Suitable for | Lower-year students exploring direction | Decided SWE / senior year / already has project experience |

## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

| Country | Internship visa support | Key notes |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Ireland (Dublin)** | ❌ **Doesn't sponsor intern visa** ("must have right to work in Ireland") | Non-EU international students must have own Stamp 1G/2/4 etc. work rights; Teamblind experience post explicit |
| **Netherlands (Amsterdam)** | ❌ **Doesn't sponsor intern visa** ("no visas will be sponsored for interns in the Netherlands programs") | Must have own NL work rights (international students use school internship agreement / 30% ruling not applicable to intern) |
| **UK (London/Cambridge/Reading)** | ⚠️ **Must have right to work in UK**; no explicit sponsorship | UK study students use work rights within Tier 4/Student visa (term-time 20h/wk, vacation 40h/wk); 12-month placement requires special arrangement |
| **Germany (Munich)** | ⚠️ Usually needs own work rights; some research roles may sponsor | International students in Germany use 120/240 day rule; non-EU non-Germany-study students application difficulty high |
| **Global Microsoft Global University Internship** | ✅ Official promotion "visa sponsorship assistance for international students" | But **in practice at European intern level almost no sponsor**; sponsor mainly concentrated in North America + Research programs |

⚠️ **Important warning**: Compared with Amazon, Microsoft in Europe is **significantly stricter** on visa sponsorship for interns. If you are a non-EU student + not studying in EU/UK, applying for Microsoft European internship feasibility << Amazon. Recommend **prioritizing MSR Cambridge (Research Intern)** or applying for Reading 12-month placement when already having legal work rights.

## 3. Interview Process

### 3.1 SWE Intern standard process (global, 2024 data)

```
Apply (official / referral / on-campus)
  → OA on Codility: 2 LC Easy–Medium questions in 90–100 minutes (themes Graph / DP / Hashmap / Queue)
  → Tech Round 1 (45 min): 1 coding question (LC Medium) + project discussion
  → Tech Round 2 + HR (45 min + behavioral): coding + computer fundamentals (OS/OOP/DBMS) + behavioral
  → Offer (usually within 2 days to 1 month notification)
```

### 3.2 Research Intern (MSR Cambridge UK) process

```
Enter shortlist based on CV / papers / professor recommendation
  → Round 1 (Technical Interview): coding + ML framework fundamentals
  → Round 2 (Science Interview): deep technical questions related to that team's research direction
  → Round 3 (Fit Interview): culture/fit conversation with team members
  → Some roles: candidate does 2 video presentations introducing their research papers + Q&A
  → Offer
```

### 3.3 Dublin SWE process (new grad / intern similar)

```
HackerRank OA: 1 easy + 1 medium/hard
  → 1 behavioral round + 3-hour mixed interview (3 interviewers alternating, incl. behavioral + technical + system design)
  → Offer
```

### 3.4 Munich SWE process (Glassdoor 39 samples)

- OA on Codility: 2 LC Medium–Hard
- 2 technical interviews 45 minutes each
- Themes: algorithms (DP, Graph BFS), OOP, occasional system design (intern not required)

### 3.5 Question difficulty summary

| Program | OA difficulty | Onsite difficulty | LP/behavioral ratio |
|------|--------|------------|-----------|
| Microsoft SWE Intern | LC Easy–Medium (Codility) | LC Medium | ~30% (project+behavioral) |
| MSR Cambridge Research Intern | No OA (replaced by CV/paper screening) | Science deep dive, related to specific team | "Fit" takes independent 1 round |
| Microsoft Dublin SWE (NG, borrow from intern) | HackerRank 1E + 1M | LC Medium, OOP, Sys Design (intern usually none) | 1 full behavioral round |
| 12-month Placement (UK Reading) | Limited info; usually 1 application form + competency round | competency-based (UK style), less pure coding | Behavioral takes majority |

### 3.6 Microsoft behavioral topics

Microsoft doesn't have strict standardized "Leadership Principles" system like Amazon, but has **Growth Mindset / Culture** focus:
- "Tell me about a time you learned something completely new"
- "How do you handle disagreement with your manager?"
- "Tell me about a failure and what you learned"
- Project deep dive: projects on resume need to be able to 30-minute deep dive (from motivation to specific decisions)
- "Tell me something not on your resume" — common HR opener

- **UK Placement**: usually Sep - following Jan (matching UK student sandwich year timetable)

### 6.3 Preparation recommendations
1. **Codility training**: Microsoft OA's only platform is Codility, question style slightly different from LC (focus on boundaries + complexity reporting); recommend doing [Codility Lessons](https://app.codility.com/programmers/lessons/)
2. **LC Medium themes**: DP, Graph BFS, Hashmap, Queue, Binary Tree these five categories are Microsoft high-frequency
3. **CS fundamentals**: Unlike Amazon, Microsoft interviews often ask OS/DBMS/OOP fundamentals (array vs linked list, sorting complexity, heap implementation, DBMS keys); **review textbook content**
4. **Project deep dive**: HR often asks "tell me something not on resume" + project detail 30-minute interrogation; prepare 2–3 projects (incl. motivation/technical decisions/failure lessons)
5. **Behavioral**: No Amazon LP framework, but has Growth Mindset focus; prepare "learning new things", "failure lessons", "disagreement with mentor/manager" three major story categories
6. **MSR Cambridge special**: must be familiar with details of **every paper** on CV; contacting team lead in advance is golden path
7. **Visa upfront clarification**: Microsoft in NL/IE/UK explicitly doesn't sponsor intern visa; email recruiter to confirm your work rights before applying

### 6.4 Referral and networking
- Contacting Microsoft EU recruiter (especially ISE / CO+I) on LinkedIn is far more effective than cold applying on official site
- Microsoft has booth at NeurIPS / ICML / CHI, MSR Cambridge researchers often attend
- 12-month placement (Reading) on-campus focus on attending UK university careers fair

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

### 7.1 Common pitfalls
- **Explore Microsoft not open in Europe**: Freshman/sophomore students wanting to apply Microsoft European internship can only squeeze into Global University Internship pool (competing with Masters/PhD), high difficulty
- **Visa restrictions**: Doesn't sponsor intern in IE/NL/UK; most non-EU students are eliminated by default; don't be misled by official "visa assistance" — **at European intern level almost no actual sponsor**
- **MSR Cambridge physical onsite requirement**: entire 12 weeks must physically work from Cambridge UK office; no remote
- **Microsoft Ireland interview reviews polarized**: Some people on Quora say interviewers "read questions don't listen to answers"; Glassdoor comprehensive 50–60% positive
- **Amsterdam intern roles few**: Schiphol office currently focused on senior-level hiring, intern numbers unstable; higher difficulty than Munich / London / Dublin
- **Salary lower than Amazon**: Same city same role net cash usually 30–40% lower; but MS benefits (gym / meal allowance / 25 days holiday / product discount) more complete
- **OA Codility platform trap**: First-time Codility users often fail due to unfamiliarity with submission process / test case timeout, must do a few familiarization runs

### 7.2 Community review highlights
- **MSR Cambridge high conversion rate**: "Many of our interns have gone on to get full-time positions here or at other Microsoft locations" (official)
- **Reading 12-month placement satisfaction high**: real job role, not "intern busywork"; 25 days holiday + £20K + good benefits
- **Munich engineering atmosphere**: Microsoft positions Munich as "workplace of the future"; intern exposed to frontier cloud / AI work
- **Dublin feedback**: process well-organized, Microsoft dispatches taxi from Heuston train station to office
- **Amsterdam ISE**: co-engineering with clients, "Forward Deployed Engineering"; great for those who enjoy client work, may not satisfy those wanting pure R&D

### 7.3 Conversion
- Historical conversion rate not officially announced; MSR Cambridge informal feedback 60–70% (based on professor circle word-of-mouth)
- General SWE intern: 60–80% (depending on team headcount)
- 12-month placement: high ratio converting to graduate offer (originally sandwich year training mode)

## Next-step action recommendations

1. **Prioritize MSR Cambridge**: If you have papers / strong ML background, MSR internship is Microsoft Europe's highest value-for-money track (good salary + high conversion rate + visa relatively friendly)
2. **Watch official site August 1**: Global University Internship 2026 cycle should open August 1, 2026; must apply before October 31
3. **Codility familiarization in advance**: at least do 10 Codility practice problems to familiarize with submission process; focus DP / Graph BFS
4. **CS Fundamentals review**: OS, DBMS, OOP textbooks; this is Microsoft's key examination point distinguishing it from Amazon
5. **Visa self-check**: confirm your work rights before applying for European role; **don't assume Microsoft will sponsor intern visa** (besides MSR almost none sponsored)
6. **UK 12-month placement (Reading)**: If you're a UK university sandwich year student, this is the most stable channel (£20K + real role + 25 days holiday)
7. **Amsterdam direct contact**: Amsterdam intern roles few, contacting ISE / CO+I recruiter via LinkedIn is more effective than official application
8. **Behavioral preparation**: "learning new things" + "failure lessons" + "disagreement with superior" three major story categories, prepare 1–2 STAR stories each; no need to prepare 16 like Amazon
9. **Referral**: Microsoft referral has some boost to OA pass; contact EU regional employees on LinkedIn
10. **Pay attention to NeurIPS / ICML / CHI**: MSR Cambridge researchers networking at these conferences is shortcut to getting referrals
