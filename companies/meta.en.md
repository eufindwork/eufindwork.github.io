### 1.3 Hiring Background and Status (2024-2025, key!)
- **Meta 2022-2023 mass layoffs** (~25,000 people total), European offices (especially London) impacted.
- **2023-2024 EU intern HC drastically cut**: Multiple 2024 data points on Reddit + Blind show Meta London "intern HC cut by more than 50% vs 2022", some candidates were even told after onsite that "HC is gone, team match canceled". 1point3acres has a clear case: [Meta London DS onsite canceled due to HC gone](https://www.1point3acres.com/bbs/thread-855305-1-1.html).
- **2024-2025 status**: Hiring resumed but still smaller than peak, intern priority lower than NG. Meta publicly stated "still hiring" (from Q1 2024), but EU intern HC still tight.
- **2025-2026 cycle**: SWE/PE Intern public positions reappear, but batch is an order of magnitude smaller than US.

Sources: [Medium - 2024 Meta Current State of Hiring](https://chan-keith-96.medium.com/2024-meta-current-state-of-hiring-485b9cc5b475), [Blind - Is Meta Still Hiring](https://www.teamblind.com/post/is-meta-still-hiring-im-interviewing-right-now-and-a-friend-told-me-they-are-freezing-hiring-pvoaludx)

## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

### 2.1 UK (London)
- **Tier 5 GAE (Temporary Work)** — Meta provides short-term internship visa for international students through authorized sponsor.
- **Student visa** holders (international students studying in UK): can intern full-time during summer, no new visa needed.
- **Skilled Worker visa**: Meta is a registered sponsor, intern-to-FT goes through this channel.
- Meta is listed as one of "best companies for UK visa sponsorship" on LeverageEdu and other lists, and **explicitly sponsors intern visa** (advantageous compared to many companies that don't sponsor interns).
- Source: [LeverageEdu - 8 Best Companies UK Visa Sponsorship](https://leverageedu.com/explore/visa/best-companies-for-visa-sponsorship-uk/)

### 2.2 Netherlands (Amsterdam)
- **Orientation Year (Zoekjaar)**: International students graduating in the Netherlands have a 1-year job-seeking visa, internships are easier during this period.
- **Knowledge Migrant (HSM)**: Meta is a registered employer, but interns usually go through short-term work permit.
- **EU students**: free to work, no permit needed.
- Non-EU students applying for Amsterdam SWE intern directly from overseas is very difficult, as Meta Amsterdam doesn't primarily push engineering roles.

### 2.3 General
- Meta prefers candidates who already have EU work rights (reduces visa friction). If you are a non-EU student studying in UK/Netherlands, you have far more advantage than applying from overseas.
- Meta internships don't necessarily offer overseas relocation visa (relocation policy varies by office), confirm at offer stage.

## 3. Interview Process

### 3.1 SWE Intern Process (London)
1. **Resume screening** + recruiter outreach (LinkedIn / on-campus / official application)
2. **Recruiter call** (10-20 min): availability, location preference, signature questions
3. **CodeSignal online assessment** (some cases) — 4 questions, 70 minutes (medium difficulty)
4. **Technical Interview (1 round, 45 min)**: 2 LeetCode questions (medium, Top question bank high-frequency)
   - Some people have an additional round (especially sponsorship case)
5. **Team Matching**: This step gets stuck or fails when HC is tight
6. Offer

**SWE Intern in London is extremely competitive**: JoinTaro data shows success rate **25%** (4 sample cases), fail rate higher than corresponding US roles.

Sources: [JoinTaro - Meta SWE Intern London May 2024](https://www.jointaro.com/interviews/companies/meta/experiences/swe-intern-london-england-may-7-2024-declined-offer-neutral-f7c8281c/), [Exponent - Meta SWE Intern Guide](https://www.tryexponent.com/guides/meta-software-engineer-intern-interview)

### 3.2 Production Engineering (PE) Intern Process (London/Dublin)
1. **Recruiter outreach** (email, Europe PE intern usually combines London + Dublin requisition)
2. **Online Assessment (OA)**: 20 MCQ (Linux basics), 20 minutes. Covers Linux commands, OS concepts, networking
3. **Coding Round (45 min)**: 1-2 LeetCode easy-medium (simpler than SWE), common questions like "Top N words in a file"
4. **Systems Round (45 min)**: Core of PE
   - OS internals (memory, paging, swap)
   - Linux troubleshooting (strace, tcpdump, vmstat, df -h, du)
   - Processes/signals (zombie, SIGTERM/SIGKILL)
   - File systems (inode, soft/hard link, B-tree)
   - Containers (namespaces, cgroups)
   - Networking (traceroute, TCP/IP, IPv6 AAAA)
5. **Behavioral / Team match**
6. Offer

PE intern reviews: "questions easier than SWE, but systems depth required is high, memorizing Linux concepts is not enough, you need to know how to debug".

Sources: [IGotAnOffer - Meta PE Interview](https://igotanoffer.com/blogs/tech/facebook-production-engineer-interview), [GitHub Gist - Meta PE Intern Systems Notes](https://gist.github.com/akshatgit/3d45aa82ce5251b1bc8e173a53050bec), [LeetCode - Meta PE Intern London Dublin 2025](https://leetcode.com/discuss/interview-experience/6380204/Meta-or-Production-Engineer-Intern-or-London-Dublin-or-2025-Reject/)

### 3.3 About E3 vs Intern Level
- **Interns are not leveled** — Meta interns are equal-level, no E3/E4 distinction (E3 = New Grad level).
- Intern → NG conversion is generally onboard as **E3 (London)** or **E4 (US, IC)**. Conversion goes through a separate RLO (Return Loop Offer) process, not automatic.
- Conversion rate was ~70% at 2022 peak, dropped significantly to 40-50% in 2023-2024 (Blind data, no official figure).

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

### 7.1 Common pitfalls
1. **HC closure leading to no team match after onsite**: happened multiple times in 2023-2024. The [Meta London DS onsite canceled](https://www.1point3acres.com/bbs/thread-855305-1-1.html) on 1point3acres is a classic case. Recommend applying for other companies in parallel, don't go all in.
2. **Recruiter communication breakdown**: Meta EU recruiter team has high turnover, often "applied for 1 month no response" or "took 2 months to get result after interview". Recommend proactively follow up every 2 weeks.
3. **PE vs SWE application confusion**: Theoretically can apply both in same cycle but the system actually flags as duplicate. If you lean PE, only apply PE; lean SWE, only apply SWE.
4. **Amsterdam misapplication**: Many people see Meta Amsterdam hiring and apply SWE intern, but Amsterdam is mainly sales/legal/marketing. SWE intern barely opens in Amsterdam.
5. **Conversion not guaranteed**: Even with strong intern perf, NG conversion is not automatic. Need to go through RLO + team match, 2024 conversion rate significantly lower than 2022.
6. **Visa processing**: Meta sponsors, but intern visa (Tier 5 GAE) processing takes about 6-8 weeks, offer-to-start needs 2 months advance start.

### 7.2 Community reviews
- **London Engineering**: "Meta London work intensity is high, team velocity is fast, IG/WhatsApp/Ads infra are doing world-class engineering". Intern feedback mentor quality high, but culture is more aggressive than Google.
- **PE Intern reputation**: PE is "hidden good path for system engineers" — learn lots of production Linux/SRE experience, and PE → SRE FT has higher conversion rate than SWE → SWE FT.
- **2023-2024 EU intern experience**: Blind / 1point3acres feedback "organizational chaos", reorg + layoff affecting intern experience, some team mentors got laid off during internship.
- **Amsterdam**: Engineering almost nonexistent, interns mainly in EMEA business/policy team, experience completely different from tech intern.
