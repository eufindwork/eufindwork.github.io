## 3. 面试流程

- **总轮数**: 4 轮, ~4 周 (部分 candidate up to 2 个月) [来源 (实际打开过): https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Unbabel-Lisbon-Interview-Questions-EI_IE868834.0,7_IL.8,14_IM1121.htm]
- **每轮形式**:
  1. HR screen (recruiter knowledgeable about engineering team, English)
  2. **Take-home option A**: 3-hour onsite with 3 interviewers
     **Take-home option B**: 1-week programming challenge + 两次 45-min onsite interviews (推荐 B, 准备空间大)
  3. Technical interview with engineer (60 min, 讨论 challenge + 经验)
  4. Final / team fit
- **题型分布**: Take-home / coding 50% / Technical discussion 25% / BQ 15% / Domain (NLP / translation) 10% (NLP/ML 岗加)
- **难度评估**: **2.6/5 (Glassdoor)** — 比 GAFAM/Datadog 简单, 实战导向
- **OA 是否存在**: 是 (take-home challenge) 或 onsite 二选一

## 7. 踩坑与社区评价

- **Take-home 1 周** vs 3-hr onsite — 选 1 周 take-home 准备空间大但耗时, 选 onsite 压力大但快
- Unbabel **业务 pivot 风险**: 2024 经历裁员 (focus customer service AI vs general translation), 公司 trajectory 仍在重建
- Lisbon 薪资远低于法国 / 德国, 但 NHR 税收 + 生活成本低 + 气候是补偿
- Engineering Medium / blog 不活跃 (vs OutSystems engineering), 技术 brand 偏弱
- 国际生友好度**非常高** — explicit visa sponsor + relocation, English-only, 国籍多元
