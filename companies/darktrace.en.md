## Intern Path

- **Role naming**: "Summer Internship Programme" (8 weeks, with CyberFirst NCSC partnership) + various SWE / Analyst interns
  - SWE Intern (C++): Cambridge HQ
  - Cyber Analyst Intern: Cambridge / London
  - Tech Author Intern: Cambridge
  - CyberFirst Industrial Placement (NCSC-funded, **only for UK nationals and students with UK work right** — non-UK students usually don't qualify) [Source (verified opened): https://www.darktrace.com/news/darktrace-launches-uk-internship-programme-with-cyberfirst-to-boost-cyber-skills-0]
- **Length**: 8 weeks summer (CyberFirst path) or 10-12 weeks regular intern
- **Application window**: October open, rolling, 1-2 month rounds; some placements (12 month) open in September
- **Salary (stipend gross/month GBP)**: ~£2,200-£2,800/month (intern), 12-month placement ~£27-30K p.a.
- **Qualifications**: Undergrad / master's in progress, no major restrictions but CS / Math / Physics preferred; CyberFirst path requires UK national / settled status
- **Sponsor policy (intern)**: UK Student visa holders (studying in UK) allowed full-time summer work — can participate. **NL student visa (UvA) cannot directly intern in UK** — UK company must open Tier 5 GAE or Skilled Worker, but interns almost never. CyberFirst explicitly **only accepts UK-resident students**.
- **Process**: HR call → online assessment (sometimes) → 1-2 technical rounds → offer; total 2-3 weeks
- **Conversion to FT rate**: About 30-40% (high-performing interns get grad return offer, but cohort shrank after Thoma Bravo took over)

## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **2026 UK thresholds**: Skilled Worker £41,700, NEC £33,400, SOC 2134 Software Developer going rate ~£49,400
- **Darktrace Limited** holds A-rated Skilled Worker sponsor license [Source (verified opened): https://huntukvisasponsors.com] (as Cambridge veteran employer / ex-LSE company, sponsor path mature)
- **Delisting impact**: 2024-10 Thoma Bravo privatization, all previous RSU cashed out, new grads no longer get listed stock, switched to performance bonus + private phantom equity (5+ year vest). Impact on international students: **TC number on paper shrinks** (no RSU hedge), but base slightly raised to compensate
- **NL student visa (UvA) path — not applicable**:
  - UvA students can't intern in UK cross-border on paid basis (need UK company to open work permit, almost never happens)
  - Darktrace **The Hague office** exists (NL) — mainly sales / customer success roles, no R&D positions, not suitable for SWE
- **GBM path**: Rare (Darktrace is UK HQ, no intra-company transfer)

## 8. Specific Assessment for UvA Students (NL student visa) ⭐

- **Core trade-off**: User already holds NL student visa, choosing UK means giving up zoekjaar (1-year post-graduation job search) + reduced HSM (€2,801/month) benefits, in exchange for UK Skilled Worker (£41,700 threshold)
- **Intern feasibility**: **Very low**
  - CyberFirst explicitly excludes non-UK students
  - Regular 8-week intern doesn't open Tier 5 / SW sponsor (UK intern extremely rare case)
  - The Hague NL office has no SWE intern, only sales support
- **FT feasibility**: **Medium-low**
  - Cambridge grad SWE base £40-45K — stuck around SW £41,700 threshold, some offers won't get sponsor (limited annual CoS quota)
  - London £45-50K safely above threshold
  - GradSignal rated 2026 top 20 sponsor, has historically opened sponsor but grad quota <20 per year
  - **Juno / GradSignal rating**: GradSignal "consistent sponsor"; huntukvisasponsors marked active
- **Alternatives**:
  - NL: Darktrace The Hague has no SWE — not feasible
  - Use zoekjaar in NL to find NL cybersec (Fox-IT, NCSC.nl, ESET NL) more smoothly
- **Expected friction**: **Medium-high**
- **Recommendations**:
  - If insisting on Darktrace, **directly apply London Grad SWE** (skip Cambridge), starting salary clears £41,700 threshold
  - **Explicitly write visa status when applying**, so recruiter can judge early whether to give CoS
  - Backup: Same-period Snyk London (higher base, clearer sponsor policy)
