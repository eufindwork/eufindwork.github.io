## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

**Core conclusion: Most SW Dev intern roles explicitly require "must be enrolled at a Dutch university" — nearly seals off non-EU students not studying in NL.**

| Dimension | Policy |
|------|------|
| Signify is IND recognised sponsor | **Yes** (Signify Netherlands B.V. in IND public register, can take Highly Skilled Migrant route) |
| Intern offers visa sponsorship | **Not explicit in role descriptions**, but Philips Hue SW Dev intern directly requires "registered at a Dutch University" → in practice equivalent to "only accepting students already in NL" |
| Erasmus / tripartite agreement route | Non-EU students studying in NL can use "school + Signify + student" tripartite internship agreement, in this case **no separate work permit needed** |
| Non-EU students not studying in NL | In theory can use "non-EU intern work permit" (requires internship at HBO/WO level + student has ≥50% Dutch minimum wage financial proof + internship agreement); Labour Market Test rarely passes, practically no public cases |

**Feasible paths for Chinese international students**:
1. **Already in Netherlands (TU/e, TU Delft, UvA, etc.)**: most direct, via school career portal + tripartite agreement
2. **Already in another EU country**: Signify doesn't strictly require Dutch uni, but SW Dev type highly localized, pass rate lower than local students
3. **Not in EU**: nearly only possibility is waiting for Signify office in your home country to open intern (India Bangalore has IT internship; China Shanghai has non-SW internship)

## 3. Interview Process

### Standard intern process (based on multiple Glassdoor samples)
```
HR initial screen (10-15 min, occasionally 30 min)
   ↓
1 round direct supervisor / hiring manager interview (tech + behavioral mix)
   ↓
[Occasionally] Case study (week to prepare, e.g. "Design outdoor lighting plan for Philips Hue + competitor analysis")
   ↓
Offer
```

### Question Type Distribution
| Question Type | Proportion | Notes |
|------|------|------|
| Behavioral | **dominant** | "Tell me about a time..."; team fit, motivation, why Signify, why Eindhoven |
| Technical (SW Dev intern) | medium | "More technical depth than expected" — Flutter/Dart experience, C++/Rust, app development process, debug approach |
| Case study | occasional | Hue / Marketing intern often has; week of prep, on-site 30 min presentation |
| LeetCode algorithm | rare | Signify doesn't lead with algorithms; but **full-time** sometimes has coding online assessment reported by PrepInsta, intern almost doesn't need |
| System design | none | Intern not tested |

### Difficulty
- Glassdoor intern self-rated difficulty **2.9 / 5** (low-medium)
- Positive experience rate: **84%**
- Average cycle: **20 days** (intern); 4 weeks to offer

### OA
- **Most intern roles have no OA**
- Some PrepInsta sources mention full-time SW recruitment drive has coding online assessment, **not applicable to intern**

> Sources: glassdoor.com/Interview/Signify-Intern..., glassdoor.com/Interview/Signify-Internship...

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

| Dimension | Review |
|------|------|
| Process pace | Fast (4 weeks to offer) — top tier in European efficiency |
| OA deterrent | Almost no OA, won't be filtered out |
| International student friendliness | **Strong "Dutch university" gating is biggest pitfall**; students already studying in NL very friendly (Eindhoven is one of most international Dutch cities, ~50% TU/e students are international) |
| Stipend | **Clearly lower than pure software companies** (€500-€700 vs ASML/Booking's €1,800-€2,500) — Signify follows traditional European intern stipend for industrial / lighting companies, don't measure with Booking/Adyen standard |
| Culture | Glassdoor intern evaluation 4.6 work-life balance, 4.5 D&I, 4.6 culture, 3.7 career — i.e. "comfortable but slow promotion" |
| Mentor & project structure | Some interns reported "thrown in to learn on the go, no structured project" — double-edged; self-driven students benefit, those expecting hand-holding will be disappointed |
| Conversion path | "Open to converting interns to full-time"; but R&D / lighting-related full-time roles themselves limited in Eindhoven, software / IoT / Hue team conversion opportunities better |
| Industry outlook | Smart lighting competition fierce (with LG/Samsung/Xiaomi etc.), Signify global market share first but growth slowing; "Good company with bad prospects" is some employees' evaluation |

### Known "pitfalls"
- **Stipend illusion**: Glassdoor auto-shown $25-$45/hr is polluted by Signify Health (US) data, actual European intern is €500-€800/month gross
- **6 months commitment**: Most roles reject short-term (3 months) intern, students with mismatched semester schedule need to coordinate
- **"Must be enrolled at NL university" seals cross-border applications** — don't pin hopes on exception, prioritize Eindhoven (TU/e), Delft (TU Delft), Amsterdam (UvA/VU) and other NL institution exchange / dual degree
- **Company recent volatility (2026 update)**: Signify 2023-2026 experienced multiple cost cuts; **on 2026-01-30 announced latest round of global layoffs of 900 people + €180M cost-cutting program**, cumulative layoffs since 2023 ~6,200 people; CEO defined 2026 as "transitional year", **2026-06-23 Capital Markets Day** to publish strategy review conclusions; software-type intern position 354870 has been taken down; before applying must check latest Glassdoor reviews + LinkedIn Signify Eindhoven employee activity [Source (verified opened): https://www.metaintro.com/blog/signify-job-cuts-2026]
- **"Philips" brand confusion**: Hue still called Philips Hue, but owner is Signify; identify Signify Careers domain when applying
