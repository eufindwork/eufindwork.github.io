## 3. Interview Process

- **Total rounds**: 4-5 rounds, average 24 days [Source (verified opened): https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Photoroom-Interview-Questions-E8011752.htm]
- **Per-round format**:
  1. Recruiter screen (30 min)
  2. Technical coding / take-home
  3. Tech deep-dive with future manager
  4. **Values interviews** (scenario-based, Photoroom values heavy)
  5. **Final onsite at Paris office** (Photoroom covers travel), half to one day, team interaction
- **Question distribution**: Tech 50% / Values 30% / Manager fit 20%
- **Difficulty assessment**: difficulty 3/5 (Glassdoor), positive rate only 30.8% (harsh filter)
- **Whether OA exists**: take-home or live coding either-or, depends on role

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews
