## 8. For UvA Students (NL Student Visa) — Specific Assessment ⭐

- **Core trade-off**: Already on NL student visa → choosing Trivago means **giving up NL zoekjaar (€2,801 reduced HSM)**, switching to DE Blue Card / Skilled Worker
- **Intern feasibility**: ❌ **Low** — Working Student mode requires DE enrollment; full intern rare (sponsor not explicit)
- **FT feasibility**:
  - **Düsseldorf Junior SWE → medium-high** — sponsor explicit + full relocation; **but starting salary €50K just covers DE Blue Card shortage €45,934, needs negotiation**
  - **Data Analyst → high** — 2026-02 JD explicit sponsor case (verified)
  - If negotiate base to €48K+ → standard Blue Card also covered, both paths either condition satisfied are safe
- **Estimated friction**:
  - Intern → high (not feasible)
  - FT → medium (sponsor process stable, but negotiate starting salary + cross-Europe move)
- **UvA advantage lost**: this company isn't in NL, no zoekjaar benefit
- **Düsseldorf location**: 2.5 hr drive from AMS, closer to user's current location than Helsinki/Stockholm, weekend NL trips possible
- **Recommendation**:
  - **If wanting to do travel-tech + want to stay in Europe but move to DE → Trivago is the geographically closest to UvA among the 7, sponsor process explicit**
  - **Core action: negotiate starting salary to €48K+** to ensure Blue Card threshold buffer
  - **If user wants more cutting-edge tech → trivago leans stable OTA business, less technical challenge than Aiven Berlin / Wolt Helsinki**
