## 1. Basic Info

- **Full name**: ASML Holding N.V.
- **HQ**: De Run 6501, Veldhoven (Eindhoven metro area, ~15 min by car from Eindhoven central station)
- **Other NL locations**: mainly Veldhoven, some teams in Eindhoven, Helmond
- **Overseas**: San Diego, San Jose, Wilton (CT), Chandler (AZ), Hillsboro (OR), Linkou (Taiwan), Pyeongtaek (Korea), etc.
- **Business**: lithography systems (DUV / EUV / High-NA EUV), computational lithography software, metrology, service
- **Stock**: ASML (Nasdaq) / ASML.AS (Amsterdam), 2024 market cap ~€280B
- **Internship roles**:
  - **Software (SW) Internship**: desktop / server / embedded C++, Python, machine learning infra
  - **Embedded Software Internship**: board-level C/C++, real-time, RTOS
  - **Electrical Engineering (EE) Internship**: circuit design, signal integrity, power
  - **Computational Lithography Internship**: ML for resist modeling, OPC, mask synthesis (**hot, high bar**)
  - **Physics / Optics Internship**: experimental optical systems, electron optics modeling
  - **Mathematics Internship**: optimization, numerical methods, programming improvement
  - Mechanical / Mechatronics / Process / Control Systems and other engineering internships
- **Internship duration**: **6-9 months** (official recommends minimum 6 months, 9 months ideal, thesis track can reach 12 months)
- **Weekly hours**: 4-5 days (at least 2 days on-site, project-dependent)
- **Application window**: rolling year-round, **May and November are the peak months for new openings** [Source (actually opened): https://www.asml.com/en/careers/students-new-graduates/netherlands/internships], suggest applying at least 3 months in advance (includes 4-week pre-onboarding)

## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **EU/EEA students**: no visa needed, internship straightforward
- **Non-EU students**:
  - Already studying at NL institution → study residence + Nuffic internship agreement (ASML uses this often)
  - Not at NL institution (other European country or Chinese/US institution) → **GVVA (combined permit residence+work for internship)**; ASML is IND recognised sponsor, can arrange, processing ~2-6 weeks
- **Key difference**: ASML is **more willing to arrange visas for long internships (6-9 months)** than SWE internet companies, because ROI is higher (short SWE internship companies don't want to deal with GVVA process)
- **Housing allowance**:
  - **EU students**: €450/month, conditions: original residence ≥80 km from Veldhoven, must move to new address within 30 km of ASML, find housing yourself + provide lease contract
  - **Non-EU students**: official page **not explicit**, multiple 1point3acres reports show non-EU students **usually also receive similar allowance**, but case-by-case confirmation with HR needed
- **30% ruling**: interns **do not qualify**; full-time conversion if meeting highly skilled migrant criteria, 30% ruling applies (ASML is a large highly skilled migrant employer)

## 3. Interview Process

ASML process is **lighter than internet companies**, emphasising fit + project match, fewer LeetCode hard questions.

### Standard process (4-6 weeks, 4 steps)
1. **CV + Motivation Letter submission**
   - **Key**: motivation letter is more important than at SWE companies, ASML highly values "why ASML / why this project"
   - Notification within 7 business days
2. **HireVue video interview** (pre-recorded, async)
   - A few preset questions, 60-120s answer each, mostly motivation/experience/teamwork
   - **Almost no technical questions**
3. **First round 1v1 (Recruiter / HR)** — video, 30 min, motivation + ambition + culture fit
4. **Second technical round (with team / supervisor)** — **on-site strongly recommended** (Veldhoven campus, includes campus tour)
   - Project deep dive (resume / thesis / past projects)
   - Technical questions: C/C++ basics, linked list, OOP, concurrency, occasional systems concepts
   - For CompLitho / ML roles: numpy / pytorch + lithography fundamentals + paper discussion
   - EE: circuit analysis + signal integrity + lab experience
   - Occasional "whiteboard coding" (interns usually easy, FT usually medium)

### By role differences
- **SW (general)**: leans C++/Python, OOP, multithreading, debugging; system software background a plus
- **Embedded SW**: C/C++ pointers, memory layout, RTOS, ISR, occasional hardware/software interface questions
- **EE**: circuits / FPGA / signal integrity; lab experience weighs heavily
- **Computational Lithography (CompLitho)**:
  - **Strongly recommend PhD / Master + strong physics or math background**
  - Topics: leans lithography (OPC, source mask optimization), numerical methods (FFT, FDTD), ML for physics (NN as surrogate)
  - Programming: Python + numpy/scipy, occasional C++ performance optimization discussion
- **Physics/Optics**: classical wave optics, beam propagation, electron optics, Monte Carlo

## 6. Application Channels and Tips

- **Official internship page**: https://www.asml.com/en/careers/students-new-graduates/netherlands/internships
- **Students & New Graduates entry**: https://www.asml.com/en/careers/students-new-graduates

  - https://www.asml.com/en/careers/find-your-job
- **TU Delft / TU/e / Twente / WUR / RWTH / KU Leuven** strong campus recruiting presence; TU/e is virtually direct supply to ASML
- **PhD events**: ASML has dedicated PhD workshops in NL and US, free to register and frequent on-site referral opportunities

### Recommended strategies
1. **Motivation letter is the most critical document in ASML's process** — must be tailored to the project (not just changing the company name); emphasize "why semiconductors / why ASML / why this specific project"
2. CV: highlight hands-on projects (hardware / embedded / numerical simulation / lithography physics) > LeetCode count
3. **C++ basics required**: embedded role pointer/memory layout always tested; SW role OOP/multithreading always tested
4. CompLitho: pre-read several ASML / OPC classic papers (Yu Cao series / GANs for OPC), able to discuss in interview = major plus
5. Referral channels: TU/e and ASML are virtually one ecosystem, referral via TU/e professors and PhD seniors has high success rates
6. HireVue psychology: look at the camera, strict timing, practice with the same tool in advance

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

### Watch out
- **Low stipend**: this is ASML internship's most criticized point, 1-star (on JoinTaro)
- **Veldhoven location**: not Amsterdam! Hard to survive without a car (public transport reaches but slow, 30-60 min daily commute is routine); Eindhoven city is small, nightlife / internationalization far below Amsterdam
- **Monolithic codebase**: intern feedback "code base huge and complex, steep learning curve for first few months"
- **Long onboarding**: 4-week pre-onboarding, including security training, some interns don't do real work for the first month
- **Confidentiality**: EUV / High-NA is national-level tech, strict NDA, limited what can be put on resume
- Some intern feedback "too hierarchical / old-school"; ASML has European manufacturing big-company vibe, not Silicon Valley fast pace

### Pros
- **Role diversity**: SW/EE/Phys/Mech/Optics/ML fully covered, almost any STEM background can find a match
- **Mature mentorship system**: intern feedback "extremely responsive, lots of help from colleagues"
- **Stable conversion**: intern → FT conversion rate high, FT package + 30% ruling makes the post-ruling package very competitive
- **Top resume credential**: ASML is absolute top tier in semiconductor industry, jumping to Intel / TSMC / Nvidia / Samsung / Synopsys is easy
- **Long internship**: 6-9 months enough to produce meaningful thesis / project

### Trade-offs
- (+) Most stable internship path to enter semiconductor industry
- (+) Mature visa support, 9-month length makes GVVA process worthwhile
- (-) Stipend nearly the lowest in the list
- (-) Non-Amsterdam location, weak city life experience
- (-) Traditional manufacturing culture, not suitable for those seeking "Silicon Valley startup vibe"
