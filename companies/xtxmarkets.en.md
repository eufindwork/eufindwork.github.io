## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

**Core conclusion: XTX Markets Limited (Companies House 09415174) holds Skilled Worker license, London FT sponsor active; intern uses Tier 5 GAE / student work**.

## 3. Interview Process

| Round | Content | Duration |
|------|------|------|
| 0. Application | xtxmarkets.com/careers/ → Greenhouse entry; doesn't use referrals | - |
| 1. OA on TestGorilla | coding (C++/Python choose) + time/space complexity analysis; not HackerRank | 60-90 min |
| 2. Technical phone 1 (senior engineer/researcher) | algorithms + DS medium depth + domain (low-latency concepts / kdb knowledge not required) | 45-60 min |
| 3. Technical phone 2 | deeper algorithms + behavioral; sometimes changed to take-home (by team) | 60 min |
| 4. **Panel 3-5 senior**: research + engineering + ops joint | multi-person panel, cross-functional questions; coding + system design + culture | 2-3 hr |
| 5. Offer | 41-day average (Glassdoor) |

**XTX specialty**:
- **Emphasizes "dare to say I don't know"**: official culture bonus, don't bullshit
- **Collaboration > individual hero**: cross-functional panel design is to test collaboration
- **Doesn't require finance background**: academic atmosphere, ML/math/CS papers actually bonus
- **SWE relatively easier than quant researcher**: Glassdoor rates SWE process "easier than Compliance" (relative to other roles at this company, doesn't mean easier than other companies)
- Some teams use take-home rather than onsite whiteboard — can ask recruiter before applying

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **No named grad programme — don't wait for "XTX Graduate Scheme 2026"**: directly apply SWE FT open roles; application windows scattered year-round, but fall recruiting (Sep-Nov) is main battleground
- **AI Research $420K annualized ≠ all interns**: XTY Labs is PhD-only NY-only special program, London Core SE intern stipend significantly lower (but still industry top-tier ~£4-5k/week)
- **TestGorilla OA is key gate**: not like HackerRank LeetCode routine, includes time/space complexity analysis + biased to academic question types; prep suggestion is EPI + time complexity deep dive
- **Slow process (41-day average)**: slower than Citadel (28 days), close to Jane Street (4-6 weeks); but SWE relative to Compliance/Software Developer path "easier"
- **International student friendliness (UK visa)**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Skilled Worker license + relocation support; post-Brexit EU and non-EU same
- **Work-life one of industry's best**: 45-55 hr/week, onsite gym + sleep pods + counselling + education reimbursement + enhanced parental leave; vs Citadel 60-70 hr/week
- **Company small, individual visibility high**: 290 people means seniors can remember every intern's name; but also means limited internal mobility choices
- **Gerko charity culture**: £100M+ personal donations (Brexit Hardship Fund, NHS, Putney/Wandsworth schools) — company not purely profit-driven
- **Glassdoor UK 4.4/5** (24 reviews, small sample but all positive); note: high rating partly due to small company + high salary + good work-life
