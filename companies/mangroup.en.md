## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **UK Skilled Worker 2026 threshold**: base £41,700 / SOC code going rate. Man Group Quant Dev / SWE grad starting salary (£55-78k+) meets threshold.
- **CoS quota**: Man Group historically issues multiple CoS per year, A-rated sponsor licence stable.
- **Internship visa path**: UK university students use Student visa (full-time during holidays / ≤20h term-time); international students need Standard Visitor + Permitted Paid Engagement or company-sponsored Tier 5 GAE.
- **Graduate Route (PSW)**: UK university graduates can use 2-year PSW buffer before being sponsored.
- **PhD friendly**: AHL Quant Talent + Quant Research has many PhD slots, visa usually granted.
- **Sofia office option**: Some Engineering roles in Bulgaria, EU citizens have no visa issues, non-EU need BG work permit (Man Group assists).

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

- **Stable process pace**: Compared to US HFs, timeline is transparent, 4 fixed stages, deadline published (2026-02-27).
- **Tech homework is key**: 2-hour homework + 30 min video is a funnel, sloppy writing directly fails. Value testing / clean code.
- **Sofia office**: Some Engineering roles in Sofia (Bulgaria), confirm location before applying.
- **AHL vs GLG distinction**: AHL = systematic / quant (your SWE target); GLG = discretionary (fundamental / macro), goes through completely different pipeline.
- **Oxford-Man Institute (OMI) ties**: OMI lab in collaboration with Oxford University provides academic cooperation opportunities, PhD students can go OMI → AHL path.
- **Glassdoor**: 4.0/5, reviews lean positive ("good WLB compared to US HF"), complaints concentrated on legacy codebase (.NET old systems) + tooling lagging behind frontline fintech.
- **International student friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — stable sponsor + Graduate Route buffer + multiple entry points.
