## 3. Interview Process

| Round | Content | Duration | Notes |
|------|------|------|------|
| 1. Numerical / Mental Math OA | **75 questions / 10 minutes** (30 easy + 30 decimals + 15 multiple choice) | 10 min | "most candidates run out of time"; Zetamac 50+ as entry |
| 2. Recruiter Interview | business knowledge + motivation + culture fit | 30-45 min | Assess understanding of ETF MM business |
| 3. Trader Interview | business + problem solving + brain teasers | 60 min | ETF brainteaser (simple math + spot opp) |
| 4. Case study | 2 senior traders hosting, trading intuition + decision-making + adaptability | 60-90 min | Decisive round |
| 5. Assessment Day (Onsite Amsterdam) | individual technical (probability / mental math / market scenarios) + **group trading simulation** + behavioral | 1 day | group exercise: candidates trade with each other |
| 6. Offer | feedback within 1-2 weeks | - | |

**Flow Traders-specific notes**:
- **Group trading simulation** is key — not Optiver 1v1 but multi-person simultaneous trade, evaluates interaction + risk management
- SWE track OA more systems engineering (C++/Java + design questions), friendlier than extreme low-latency HFT firms
- Publicly listed company → quarterly PR + media frequency high, not suitable for those who like "stealth" culture

## 6. Application Channels & Tips

- **Official application**: https://www.flowtraders.com/careers/job-search/ — own ATS + greenhouse hybrid; 4 intakes per year (Trader)
- **Greenhouse direct entry** (Trading Intern + Graduate): https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/flowtraders
- **Referral**: important but not killer — Flow Traders business niche (ETF MM), candidate pool small, resume screen relatively loose
- **CV focus**:
  - **ETF knowledge**: unlike Optiver/IMC, Flow Traders emphasizes ETF arbitrage + creation/redemption, candidates need to understand ETF structure (Heard on the Street not enough)
  - **mental math (zetamac 50-60+)**: different 75-in-10 format from Optiver/IMC, practice more
  - C++ / Java both OK (vs Optiver C++ only)
  - Codeforces 1500+ + DevOps / Linux experience plus for Trading Operations Engineering
- **Same-tier alternatives**: Optiver (same city, engineering more HFT extreme) / IMC (same city, options oriented) / Susquehanna ETF desk (Dublin/Philly) / Jane Street ETF desk

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **75 questions / 10 minutes mental math is core elimination**: slightly looser than Optiver (8.3 questions/min vs 10 questions/min), but still extremely fast
- **Glassdoor 4.0/5 — slightly lower than peers**: mainly complaints "corporate-ization high (listed company), more than Optiver's flat" + "bonus transparency insufficient"
- **Intern doesn't get visa — important**: Optiver / IMC is case-by-case, Flow Traders is explicit no — Chinese / Indian students can only apply if already studying undergrad or master in Europe
- **Romania Cluj-Napoca is backdoor**: lower salary but extremely low cost of living, entry path for some Eastern European + non-EU candidates
- **Graduate doesn't get stock**: listed company but grad doesn't get RSU (vs senior); bonus all cash
- **International student friendliness (full-time)**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Amsterdam engineering international, but intern doesn't get visa deducts 1 star
- **International student friendliness (intern)**: ⭐⭐ — must already be in EU
