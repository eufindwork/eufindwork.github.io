## 1. Basic Information

- **European offices**: Paris (HQ, ~80% engineering), Berlin, Barcelona, Belgrade (engineering), Milan, Vienna
- **Role types**: Backend (Ruby on Rails primary, Go new services), Frontend (TypeScript/React), Mobile (iOS Swift / Android Kotlin), Data Engineer, ML Eng, SRE, Product Design
- **Hiring cycle**: **year-round rolling**, no fixed grad cohort; intern (stage) concentrated Sep/Mar start (French law 6 month cap)
- **Important deadlines**: no unified deadline, depends on individual JD

---

## Intern Path

- **Role naming**: **Stage / Internship** — Backend (Ruby), Frontend, Data, Product (some SWE internships, bulk in product / data / ops)
- **Length**: French stage 4-6 months (legal cap 6 months / year)
- **Application window + deadline**: rolling, Sep cohort apply May-Jul; Mar cohort apply Nov-Jan
- **Salary (stipend gross/month)**: French stage statutory minimum €4.35/h ≈ **€660/month** (35h/wk); Qonto actual SWE stage pays **€1,500-1,800/month** (based on Glassdoor + Welcometothejungle data — pending web re-verification)
- **Eligibility (education, local residency requirement)**: enrolled, need **convention de stage** (internship agreement, signed by school); legally stage is not "work", **no work permit needed**, but non-EU students need valid student visa (French local student) — UvA students need to open Nuffic convention / French university short-term enrollment complex workaround
- **Sponsor policy (intern)**: **does not sponsor intern visa** (French regulations stage isn't work, goes via convention de stage); international students need to bring NL/EU student status
- **Process**: Lever application → 1 round recruiter call → 1 tech screen (coding) → 1 onsite (system design + culture)
- **Conversion to FT rate**: high (not publicly disclosed, Glassdoor reviews show stage to CDI conversion ~50%+)

## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **Supports non-EU students**: ✅ yes, explicit sponsor FT (intern doesn't need sponsor but needs EU student status)
- **Intern vs FT sponsor differences**:
  - **Intern**: via convention de stage, no work permit needed, but user (NL student visa) **must** go via UvA + French partner university Nuffic short-term secondment / or obtain French university short-term enrollment — high friction
  - **FT**: via Passeport Talent, Qonto covers full process, 4-8 weeks to issue
- **Known sponsorship cases**: Relocate.me JD shows Backend SWE role explicit "visa sponsorship + relocation" [verified opened: https://relocate.me/france/paris/qonto/backend-software-engineer-5238]; arbeitnow database Qonto Germany roles also sponsor [verified opened: https://www.arbeitnow.com/jobs/companies/qonto]
- **2026 thresholds**: **France Passeport Talent — Salarié Qualifié threshold €43,243/year** (2× SMIC, 2026); Qonto Junior SWE €48K+ starting salary covers. Passeport Talent gives 4-year multi-year card, spouse can work.

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

- **2023-2024 slowdown**: Qonto post €5B valuation 2022 growth slowed, 2023 H2 hiring freeze for some teams (mainly ops, engineering still hiring), 2025 recovered
- **Ruby on Rails learning curve**: non-Rails background candidate take-home easily fails (expects ActiveRecord proficiency)
- **Glassdoor reviews**: overall 3.9/5; complaints: scope creep + French management style (many meetings, slow decisions)
- **Salary vs US firms**: significantly lower than Datadog Paris / Criteo, **junior ~€52K vs Datadog ~€75K**; Qonto's advantage is mission + lower hours + BSPCE upside
- **Berlin office**: small engineering team ~30 people, mainly German market local, close collaboration with Paris HQ; Belgrade is cost-saving engineering hub (lower salary)

## 8. For UvA Students (NL student visa) — Specific Assessment ⭐

### Intern viability
- **Paris stage**: ⚠️ medium-high friction — French stage via convention de stage doesn't need work permit, but requires **school to sign convention** (requires candidate is French/EU university enrolled). Whether UvA via Nuffic convention allows cross-border stage to Qonto needs UvA international office confirmation.
- **Workaround**: (1) UvA + French university (Sciences Po / HEC) short exchange period apply for stage; (2) wait until post-graduation switch to NG directly via Passeport Talent

### FT viability
- **Post-graduation Junior SWE Paris**: ✅ highly viable — Qonto explicit sponsor, Passeport Talent €43,243 threshold, Junior starting salary €48K+ covers. Process 4-8 weeks, Qonto HR covers full.
- **NL zoekjaar conflict**: Qonto has **no office in NL**, user needs to give up zoekjaar to go via Passeport Talent. But Passeport Talent gives 4-year multi-year card, spouse can work, more stable than zoekjaar renewal.
- **Return-to-NL path**: work 5 years to get EU permanent residence then free EU movement; or work 2 years to convert to EU Blue Card.

### Expected friction
- Intern: **medium-high** (convention de stage hard to coordinate)
- FT: **low** (Passeport Talent smooth, Qonto experienced in operations)

### Suggestions
- **Skip intern, go straight to NG**: 6-9 months pre-graduation start applying Junior Backend Ruby/Go roles (rolling, year-round)
- **Timeline**: 2026 pre-summer-graduation → Jun apply → Aug offer → Sep-Oct start (overlap period with NL zoekjaar usable as backup; if NL offer comes first prioritize, since user is already in NL)
- **Passeport Talent advantage**: 4-year card, more stable than zoekjaar 1 year; but if user wants to continue living in NL, don't recommend Paris
