## 3. Interview Process

- **Total rounds**: 4-5 rounds, about 2 weeks complete (2026 feedback) [Source (verified opened): https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Doctolib-Interview-Questions-E1018530.htm]
- **Each round format**:
  1. Recruiter screen (30 min)
  2. **Take-home assignment** (based on Doctolib real problem, 4-6 hours) — Doctolib in 2024 cut from 3 live rounds to 1 live + take-home [Source (verified opened): https://coderpad.io/case-study/how-doctolib-streamlined-its-hiring-process-to-save-hours-per-hire-and-keep-25-more-candidates-in-the-funnel/]
  3. Feature Building Interview (Doctolib signature, on-site extend take-home / modify spec) [Source (verified opened): https://careers.doctolib.com/blog/hiring-advice/all-about-our-feature-building-interview/]
  4. System Design (Senior only, Junior occasional)
  5. Culture fit / Values (Doctolib values emphasize patient impact)
- **Question type distribution**: Take-home + feature building (60%) > System design (20%) > BQ (20%); almost no algorithm LeetCode hard
- **Difficulty assessment**: Medium. Engineering-practice oriented (Rails / TS), not algorithm competition style
- **OA exists**: usually no independent OA, take-home replaces

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- Process is **fast and transparent** (2 weeks vs industry 4-6 weeks), failures get feedback is highlight
- Feature building round is the killer — being unprepared will get crushed on extending requirements on-site
- Paris and Berlin cultural difference: Paris more hierarchical, Berlin more flat
- Healthcare is regulated → release cadence slower than pure internet, suitable for long-term stable + impact seekers
- International student friendliness: engineering team 25%+ non-local, internship needs own visa, full-time sponsor smooth
