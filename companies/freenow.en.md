## Intern Path

- **Role naming**: occasional "Working Student" (Werkstudent, DE mode) / "Internship" by department, no unified cohort
- **Duration**: Working Student long-term part-time (≤ 20h/week during semester); Internship 3-6 mo
- **Application window + deadline**: rolling, no fixed window
- **Salary (stipend gross/month)**:
  - Hamburg Working Student €15-20/hr (about €1,200-1,600/month part-time, matches DE Werkstudent legal cap 20h/wk, Stepstone mytaxi/FreeNow historical posting [Source (verified opened): https://www.stepstone.de/stellenangebote--Werkstudent-Accounting-Finance-m-w-Hamburg-mytaxi--5152045-inline.html])
  - Full-time intern €1,800-2,500/month (Glassdoor; FreeNow Hamburg SWE FT median €90.9K → intern 25-30% prorate)
- **Eligibility (degree, local residence requirement)**:
  - Working Student mode requires **registered DE university student + holding DE residence**
  - Internship usually requires DE/EU residence, cross-Europe intern rare
- **Sponsor policy (intern)**:
  - Official FAQ doesn't explicitly distinguish intern sponsor policy (only general "individual support during relocation")
  - In practice Working Student mode requires user already in DE enrollment, doesn't apply to NL students
  - **Recommendation: NL students skip intern, go FT**
- **Process**: simplified FT (1-2 rounds)
- **Conversion to FT rate**: Working Student → FT conversion high (DE general mode)

> Intern stipend public data still sparse (FreeNow official careers has no independent intern salary page, mytaxi.com legacy portal lists Werkstudent role without price); 2026-06 retry only found FT median €90.9K (Levels.fyi) and Werkstudent hourly market. Numbers stable in €1,200-1,600/month (WS) / €1,800-2,500/month (intern) range.

## 8. Specific Assessment for UvA Students (NL student visa) ⭐

- **Core trade-off**: already in NL holding student visa → choosing Free Now means **giving up NL zoekjaar (€2,801 reduced HSM)**, switch to DE Blue Card / Skilled Worker
- **Intern feasibility**: ❌ **low-zero** — Working Student mode must DE enrollment, cross-Europe intern rare (sponsor not explicit)
- **FT feasibility**:
  - **Hamburg / Berlin SWE → high** — sponsor explicit + DE Blue Card shortage €45,934 cover far exceeds (Free Now median €90K)
  - **Lyft integration post-period: short-term need to observe layoff risk**, don't get laid off within 6 mo of sponsor + relocation
- **Expected friction**:
  - Intern → high (infeasible)
  - FT → medium (sponsor simple, but industry integration period + cross-Europe relocation)
- **UvA advantage lost**: this firm not in NL, no zoekjaar benefit
- **Recommendation**:
  - **If want mobility + want DE → Free Now is stable, sponsor explicit + €90K starting salary strong**
  - **Integration period suggests waiting until after 2026 Q3 to apply** — wait for Lyft integration layoff cycle to clear
  - **If don't want to move to DE → Voi / Bolt not in this tier; staying NL going Uber EU HQ AMS more aligned**
