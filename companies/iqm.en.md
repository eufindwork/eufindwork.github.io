## 3. Interview Process

> Glassdoor: difficulty 2.6/5, 50% positive, Summer Research Intern process about 2 months

- **Total rounds**: full-time 4-5 rounds / Intern 2-3 rounds
- **Each round format**:

### Summer Research Intern (Glassdoor 2023 verified + inferred):
  1. **Online Application** → HR email
  2. **HR Round** (non-technical, 30 min): school / research interest / timetable / motivation
  3. **Technical Round** (60-90 min, with 2 team members): physics (quantum mechanics basics + superconducting/transmon qubit / control theory / quantum algorithm) + programming (Python + Qiskit/Qrisp/PennyLane)
  4. **(Optional) Research Talk** (30-60 min): candidate presents thesis/research project

### Full-time SWE / QEC Engineer:
  1. **Recruiter Call** (30 min): role + location + visa fit
  2. **Hiring Manager** (60 min): past research/eng experience + quantum direction alignment
  3. **Technical Coding** (60-90 min): Python algorithm + (for QEC) surface code / Pauli operators / stabilizer measurement concept questions
  4. **Research / Domain Deep Dive** (60-90 min): paper presentation or IQM-provided case study analysis
  5. **Culture / Team Fit** (45 min): cross-national team work / mission alignment

- **Question distribution**: quantum physics + control theory (30%) / Python + quantum SDK (Qrisp/Qiskit) (30%) / systems/algorithms (20%) / Research / publications (10%) / Culture (10%)
- **Difficulty assessment**: Medium (Glassdoor 2.6/5); physics / QEC concept questions are hard for non-physics background candidates, algorithm questions relatively standard
- **Whether OA exists**: no standardized OA; but take-home (QEC simulation / Qrisp circuit) occasional

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

### Process layer
- **2-month intern process** — much slower than FAANG intern process, expectation management needed
- **Physics + programming double threshold**: pure CS background (no quantum physics) difficult; conversely pure physics (no Python/engineering) also difficult
- **Junior visa policy unclear**: official only commits to senior roles, junior cases few, must disclose by email before application
- **Quantum direction extremely niche**: company business understanding threshold high (transmon / QEC / quantum advantage / NISQ era limitations), non-domain people hard to self-learn to interview level

### Culture / business risks
- **Quantum computing commercialization time window undetermined**: industry overall NISQ-era, fault-tolerant quantum computer still needs 5-10 years; IQM commercial revenue mainly from hardware sales + cloud (Resonance), growing but long path
- **Europe vs USA vs China competition**: IQM is European player representative (vs IBM/Google/Microsoft USA, vs Pasqal/Quantinuum same Europe, vs Origin/Baidu Quantum China)
- **Espoo HQ cost of living**: not low (FI overall high taxes), but engineer net actual quality of life good

### Known "pitfalls"
1. **Junior visa not guaranteed**: don't assume company will handle, confirm before accepting offer
2. **Multi-site collaboration**: Espoo + Munich + Paris cross-timezone, async + travel many
3. **Quantum algorithm SWE role requires physics / math hard background**: pure Python SWE hard to match
4. **Public salary data extremely few**: bargaining lacks benchmark, industry networking is only info source
5. **Company careers domain change**: meetiqm.com → iqm.tech (2025-2026), note bookmark / referral link invalidation

### International student friendliness
- **EU / FI / DE / FR nationals**: extremely friendly
- **Quantum physics PhD (any nationality)**: friendly, official dozens of relocations verified
- **Chinese national master / new grad**: medium friendly — already in EU studying preferred, outside EU difficult
- **Pure CS background**: unfriendly — company main force is quantum professional talent, ordinary SWE low value
