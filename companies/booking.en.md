## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

- **Key restriction**: 2025/2026 Amsterdam SWE Internship **explicitly requires "enrolled in a Dutch University" + "right to work in EEA"**
  - This means non-EU students who are already studying Bachelor/Master at a Dutch institution and hold a study residence permit can usually apply (study residence has built-in 16h/week + summer full-time work right)
  - Non-EU students not at Dutch institutions (e.g. UK/Germany/China schools) **fundamentally cannot pursue 9-week summer SWE internship path**
- **Alternative path**: PhD/Research Intern usually more willing to use GVVA (combined permit residence+work) or Nuffic agreement; when project length 3–6 months IND process is worthwhile
- **Recognised sponsor status**: Booking.com is a **key sponsor** registered with NL IND, experienced in handling HSM/Kennismigrant, full-time conversion goes through Highly Skilled Migrant path
- **30% ruling**: Intern **not eligible**; full-time conversion depends on salary threshold (2024 rules were tightened, 5-year period reduced to 5 years but ratio reduced)
- **Relocation benefits**: 2025 intern officially provides one-way ticket + 30-day managed apartment, then own housing

## 3. Interview Process

Booking.com internship interview takes **HackerRank OA + behavioral/case + technical + culture** as main thread, more focused on **case study / A/B testing thinking** than peer companies.

### SWE Internship (9-week, Amsterdam)
1. **CV screening** (conversion rate < 10%)
2. **HackerRank OA**
   - 90 minutes / 3–5 questions (mainstream reports 4 questions, 75–90 minutes)
   - Question types: LeetCode easy/medium + occasionally SQL + multiple choice (CS basics)
   - **Strong "business domain question" characteristic** — question background mostly hotel review / hotel ID / booking data
   - Not just pass test cases, **time/space complexity also counted**
3. **Recruiter Call** (English, 30 minutes, motivation/experience/expectations)
4. **Hiring Manager / Technical 1v1** (45–60 minutes, pair programming on collaborative IDE, array / hashmap basic questions)
5. **Assessment Day (merged starting 2025)** — Zoom/Teams, half-day continuous:
   - 1× technical coding (medium LeetCode difficulty)
   - 1× **Case Study / Business Case** (Booking specialty, details below)
   - 1× **Culture fit / behavioral** (around Booking values: "Learn Forever", "Own It", "Succeed Together", etc.)

### ML Internship / ML Scientist Process
1. HackerRank: 1 coding + 9 ML multiple choice (probability/statistics/classic models)
2. Recruiter screen
3. Coding interview (Python, LeetCode graphs/trees)
4. System Design / ML System (recommendation / ranking system design)
5. Case study (common question: "How to identify good hotel deals?", focus on A/B testing and metric design)
6. HR culture round

### Booking Specialty: Case Study
- Some processes require **preparing a case presentation within 48 hours**, then presenting to multi-person judges
- Case commonly involves: pricing experiment, ranking algorithm trade-off, fraud detection metric, conversion funnel
- **A/B testing almost mandatory**: control vs treatment, statistical significance, sample size, novelty effect, network effect

### Acceptance Rate
- Publicly reported internship acceptance rate **< 8%**, intern applicant pool thousands per year

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

### Warnings (from 2023–2026 community feedback)
- **2025-07 major layoff (major update)**: Booking.com officially announced global layoff of **~1,000 people** in July 2025, with **Amsterdam HQ impact 200-1,000 people**, 2.8%-14% of Amsterdam 7K headcount; parent 2024 net profit €5B+ (+37% YoY) but officially framed as "**too many layers, too bureaucratic**" flattening; **some 2025-2026 cycle applicants report being told "unable to continue due to internal changes"** [Source (verified opened): https://nltimes.nl/2025/07/16/bookingcom-cut-hundreds-jobs-amsterdam-part-global-restructure] [Source (verified opened): https://www.dutchnews.nl/2025/07/booking-to-cut-some-1000-jobs-significant-impact-in-amsterdam/]
- **Case study highly subjective**: Multiple Glassdoor reports case study scoring depends on interviewer personal preference, same answer different results
- **HackerRank time pressure**: 4 questions / 15 minutes per question, complained "reading question/reading stdin takes more time than writing algorithm"
- **A/B testing mandatory**: ML/DS candidates who ignore statistics review almost certainly fail

### Advantages
- Project quality high, mentorship system mature, real production tasks within 9 weeks
- Amsterdam campus good location (Oosterdokseiland), canteen/welfare/remote work policy friendly
- Compass framework has clear path design for intern→grad→junior→senior

### Pros and Cons
- (+) Big traffic platform + tech investment heavy + ML/personalization team world-class
- (+) NL visa sponsor experience rich, one of the most stable NL big companies for senior conversion
- (-) Business highly concentrated in OTA, not as diverse as FAANG
- (-) **2025-07 major layoff (~1K global, Amsterdam 200-1K)** caused some teams to freeze hiring, intern → return offer rate suppressed; all 2026 Compass cycles are closed
