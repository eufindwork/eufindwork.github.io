## 3. Interview Process

- **Total rounds**: 4-5 rounds (intern 3-4 rounds, full-time 5-6 rounds)
- **Per-round format**:
  1. **Recruiter Screen** (30 min, **aggressive filter — surface answers directly fail**)
  2. **OA / HackerRank** (30 min, intern; full-time may have take-home)
  3. **Decomposition Interview** — given ambiguous business problem, break down + push architecture
  4. **Coding** — LeetCode Medium tier
  5. **Learning Interview** — learn new API/concept on the spot, evaluates learn-on-the-fly ability
  6. (full-time) **System Design + Hiring Manager Final**
- **Question distribution**: Coding 30% / Decomposition 30% / Learning 20% / Re-engineering 10% / BQ 10%
- **Difficulty assessment**: FDSE Intern 3.1/5 (Glassdoor); overall ~28 days
- **Whether OA exists**: **Yes**, HackerRank 30 min for intern

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

- **Recruiter screen killer round**: Palantir filters harder than peers at recruiter stage, "surface motivation = out" — prep to explain clearly why Palantir not Amazon/Google
- **Decomposition / Re-engineering question type unique** — not LC, can't rely on grinding; check [hellointerview / prepfully](https://prepfully.com/interview-guides/palantir-software-engineer) Palantir-specific
- **FDSE not pure coder** — 50% writing code + 50% client onsite (defence / banks / pharma), avoid FDSE if you mind frequent client travel, take pure SWE
- **UK Gov clearance**: some UK Government roles require SC / DV clearance — international students can't get them; check Lever JD for explicit mention
- **High international student friendliness**: Palantir is one of the most stable UK Skilled Worker sponsors within frontier (Juno 8/10)
