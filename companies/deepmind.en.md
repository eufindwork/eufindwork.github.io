## 1. Basic Info

- **European offices (in scope)**:
  - **London (main HQ, King's Cross)**: Almost all RS/RE interns here
  - Note: Paris also has small DeepMind team (Gemini multilingual / some robotics), but intern slots extremely few
  - Globally also Mountain View / Zürich, but within EU scope London is main
- **Internship role types**:
  - **Research Scientist Intern (RS Intern)**:
    - Focus on paper output, work with host researcher on publishable work
    - Strongly prefers PhD (CS / Math / Physics / Neuroscience / EE), Master/Bachelor cases **extremely rare**
  - **Research Engineer Intern (RE Intern)**:
    - Leans engineering, collaborates with RS to build experimental frameworks, scaling experiments, infra
    - PhD not mandatory, strong MSc + top SWE background can get in
  - **Student Researcher Program** (independent project):
    - **12-24 weeks**, minimum 4 days/week, in Google office
    - Any degree (Bachelor/Master/PhD), is DeepMind/Google Research "gray area" internship
  - **Research Ready Programme** (limited project for **UK domestic undergrads**):
    - 8-week summer, joint with Cambridge / York / Imperial / AIchemy etc. universities funded by DeepMind
    - **Not directly DeepMind hire**, is university project, but with DeepMind brand
- **Internship length**:
  - RS/RE intern: **3-6 months** (mostly 6 months, synced with PhD semester gap)
  - Student Researcher: **12-24 weeks**
  - Research Ready: 6-8 weeks (fixed summer)
- **Hiring cycle**:
  - **Sep-Nov open** Summer 2025/2026 RS intern
  - **Dec-Jan** deadline (some teams close early), strong rolling tendency, **apply early interview early**
  - Student Researcher: rolling year-round, but Q4 most active
  - Research Ready (UK campus): Jan-Feb campus deadline, Feb-Apr notification
  - **2026-09-26 recheck**: Official deepmind.google/careers/ and deepmind.google/student-researcher-program/ pages still live, structure/application entry unchanged (both point to the Google Careers portal for open roles), neither page itself publishes specific 2027-cohort open/close dates [Source (actually opened): https://deepmind.google/careers/] [Source (actually opened): https://deepmind.google/student-researcher-program/]

## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **DeepMind is registered UK Skilled Worker sponsor**, full-time conversion path clear
- **Internship paths**:
  - **Preferred**: Undergrad/master's/PhD studying in UK (Student visa automatically allows full-time within internship duration)
  - **Graduate visa** holders (2-year open period post-graduation): can directly intern without new sponsor
  - **GAE (Government Authorised Exchange)**: DeepMind historically used, intern short-term (≤ 24 months)
  - **Tier 5 GAE** merged into new GAE rules, but essentially same
- **Remote internship**: Post-COVID some RS interns allowed hybrid; but visa angle still requires being in UK
- **Overseas PhD temporary visit for intern**: Current standard is to issue GAE certificate of sponsorship, DeepMind submits for you
- **Known sponsorship cases**:
  - Many PhD interns (from European/North American/Asian schools) went through GAE for 6 months — public Quora / LinkedIn cases
  - Full-time conversion almost all uses Skilled Worker
- **International student friendliness**: **⭐⭐⭐⭐** (friendly among AI labs, but **only friendly to those who actually get to interview** — resume screening extremely strict)

## 3. Interview Process

### 3.1 Research Scientist Intern (RS Intern) — Hardest

**Total rounds: 5-6 main stages, 4-6 week cycle**

1. **HR / Recruiter screen** (30-45 min): background, research interests, **host match preference**
2. **Hiring manager / Host chat** (30-45 min): paper discussion, interest direction match
3. **Coding Round** (60 min × 1-2): LeetCode **Medium** dominant, occasional Hard; **PyTorch tensor shape questions** are signature questions
4. **ML / Math Round** (60-90 min):
   - Math fundamentals (linear algebra, eigenvalue/eigenvector, calc, prob)
   - ML theory (loss functions, regularization, optimization, bias-variance)
   - LLM frontier (Transformer from scratch, attention implementation, finetuning methods, Gemini/DeepSeek training stack details)
5. **Research Interview** (60-90 min × 1-3 scientists):
   - **PhD work deep dive** — methodology, limitations, follow-up
   - Hypothetical research question — "if given 100 GPUs, how would you attack X problem?"
6. **Culture / Team Fit** (45 min)

Question type breakdown (RS Intern):
- Research / paper deep dive: **45%**
- ML basic math/theory: **25%**
- Coding (LeetCode + PyTorch impl): **20%**
- BQ / culture: **10%**

Difficulty rating: **3.2 / 5** (Glassdoor RS), 62% positive, average **45 days**

### 3.2 Research Engineer Intern (RE Intern) — Engineering-leaning, more accessible

**Total rounds: 4-5 stages, 4-6 week cycle**

1. **Recruiter call**
2. **Coding interviews × 2**: LeetCode Medium primarily, occasional system questions
3. **ML Fundamentals interview**: optimization, regularization, loss, transformers, **training + inference practical questions** (e.g., "how do you debug an exploding gradient?")
4. **ML Design / System Design**: large-scale training pipeline, distributed training (DDP, FSDP), checkpoint, sharding
5. **Lead Researcher interview**: team match + project discussion

Question type breakdown (RE Intern):
- Coding: **30%**
- ML basics: **25%**
- ML / System design: **25%**
- Projects: **15%**
- BQ: **5%**

**Sundeep Teki guide** reports: "DeepMind engineering role acceptance < 1%, like a hybrid of PhD defense + engineering exam"

### 3.3 Student Researcher Program

- Process lighter: 1-2 rounds + direct conversation with host researcher; strongly dependent on host endorsement, referral mechanism is core
- Usually host knows candidate in advance (paper / workshop / Twitter)

### 3.4 Prep path (David Stutz / public blog recommendations)

- 2-3 week sprint: Cracking the Coding Interview + ML paper review + tensor operations
- Re-review textbooks (Bishop PRML, MacKay, Goodfellow Deep Learning Book)
- Focus: **multi-head attention from scratch implementation** + **training debug stories**

## 6. Application Channels and Recommendations

- **Apply via official site**:
  - https://deepmind.google/careers/ (main entry)
  - https://deepmind.google/student-researcher-program/ (Student Researcher separate channel)
  - Historical Greenhouse interface: https://boards.greenhouse.io/deepmind/ (later transitioned to Google careers)
  - **Roles posted on Google careers**, search "DeepMind" keyword
- **Referral paths (DeepMind intern strongest path)**:
  - **Twitter / X**: DeepMind researchers generally active, showing them your paper/notebook is effective outreach
  - **NeurIPS / ICML / ICLR workshop**: DeepMind paper first author spotlight, on-site meeting host
  - **Academic advisor recommendation**: PhD advisor with DeepMind team collaboration, referral very important
- **Resume/background requirements**:
  - **RS Intern**: PhD in progress + **top-venue first-author paper** (NeurIPS / ICML / ICLR / Nature sub-journal) is effective threshold; occasionally accepts top Master but extremely rare
  - **RE Intern**: Master + engineering capability (Kaggle top / GitHub strong project / previous FAANG SWE intern) can compete; PhD also very common
  - **Student Researcher**: Education flexible, but host endorsement decides everything
  - **Strong baseline**: At least 3 arXiv preprints + 1-2 top-venue papers, or 1 spotlight/oral
- **Strongly recommended**:
  - On deepmind.google look at each hiring manager's publication list, before applying **specifically** describe why fits their research
  - DeepMind hiring portal allows "preferred host", make sure to fill in

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Feedback

- **publication is almost hard requirement for RS intern**: Without top-venue first author hard to pass HR screen (Hacker News also explicitly big-firm PhD interns mostly have NeurIPS/ICML first author)
- **acceptance < 1%** (RE intern, Sundeep Teki data), more stringent than Apple / Nvidia intern
- **Long process + little feedback**: Glassdoor multiple mentions "lack of feedback" is normal; rejection round often not informed
- **Host match risk**: Even if all tech rounds pass, no host researcher willing to take you means rejection; **multiple host preferences + early communication** key
- **Conversion rate (Intern → Full-time)**:
  - Public data **none**, but community estimates: **PhD RS intern → stays as RS** ratio **far below** 20-30% (most still need PhD graduation + re-doing full-time process)
  - **RE intern → RE full-time** conversion higher than RS, but still not automatic — needs separate hiring committee review
  - Quora has claims that "DeepMind on CV is equivalent to six-figure offer directly grabbed" is **exaggeration** (but brand effect indeed strong)
- **PhD intern long cycle**: 6 months almost sacrificing one PhD program semester, needs advisor approval
- **Disaster case**: Public blog "My DeepMind Interview was a Disaster" describes even with paper background got rejected — interviewer rigor extremely high
- **International student friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (overseas PhD entering via GAE is standard path)
- **DEI tilt**: DeepMind has dedicated programs for underrepresented group promotion (Research Ready being one example), but **does not relax technical threshold**
