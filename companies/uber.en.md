## 1. Basic Info

- **Full name**: Uber Technologies, Inc.
- **EMEA HQ**: Amsterdam, Mr. Treublaan 7 (near Oosterdokseiland, walking distance from Central Station)
- **EMEA engineering other hubs**: Aarhus (Denmark, data infrastructure + Grail), Sofia (Bulgaria, mobile/web), Lisbon (newly added, Marketplace)
- **Business**: Mobility (ride-hailing), Delivery (Uber Eats), Freight, Ads
- **Stock**: UBER (NYSE)
- **Internship role types (EMEA SWE)**:
  - **Software Engineer Intern** (Amsterdam / Aarhus / Sofia choose one, but Amsterdam has the fewest slots)
  - **Machine Learning Intern** (common teams: Marketplace, Search, Fraud)
  - Data Scientist / Analytics Intern
  - Strategic Finance / Operations Intern (non-engineering)
- **Internship duration**: **3 or 6 months** (commonly 6 months, some thesis projects 9 months)
- **Application window**: Usually opens Aug-Oct, closes Nov-Dec, starts spring/summer of next year; missing the window usually means waiting until next year

## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

- Uber is an **IND recognised sponsor** in NL, mature support for highly skilled migrants
- **Interns**:
  - Studying at NL institution → study residence permit is sufficient
  - Other European institution (EU citizen) → no visa needed, just register BSN
  - Non-EU + not at NL institution → GVVA needed, Uber supports but **prefers 6-month+ thesis projects** (short summer SWE intern rejection rate higher)
- **Key point**: In Uber EMEA intern blog cases, **Yukie Nomiya is a CS student at University of Rome (Italy, EU)** → EU students most easily get Amsterdam intern; **best path for non-EU students is to get NL institution enrollment first**
- **Relocation**: Interns typically get one-way ticket + 1-month corporate housing; find own housing after
- **30% ruling**: Interns **not applicable**; full-time conversion applicable if meets threshold

## 3. Interview Process

Uber is one of the **highest bar internships** in European mobility/delivery, the process is clearly stricter than ASML / TomTom, close to US FAANG but slightly lighter.

### Standard internship process (5–6 weeks, 5 rounds)
1. **Recruiter Screen** (30 min, HR, motivation + experience + project match)
2. **Online Assessment (CodeSignal)**
   - Usually 70–90 min, 3 questions
   - Topic types: dynamic programming, linked list, number base conversion (typical combination)
   - Platform is CodeSignal, supports running / debugging
3. **Hiring Manager Round** (45 min)
   - Project experience deep dive, culture fit, why Uber, why Amsterdam
   - Provides Q&A opportunity, strongly recommend researching target team in advance
4. **Coding Round** (60 min, virtual, CodeSignal/CoderPad)
   - LeetCode medium (common array / hashmap / graph BFS-DFS / interval / tree)
5. **Low-Level Design (LLD) Round** (60 min, intern occasionally has)
   - Design a small system (e.g., parking lot, ride matching, rate limiter)
6. **Behavioral / Culture Round** (45 min)
   - Uber values: "We build globally, we live locally", "We are customer obsessed", "We see issues, we own them"
   - STAR method answers

### Process characteristics
- **Recruiter actively provides prep material**: usually sends candidates a prep guide (explaining each round's focus), EMEA Uber does this more transparently than other companies
- **Style conversational**: multiple reports praise "interview feels like mutual discussion rather than assessment"
- **Low acceptance rate**: Uber overall SWE intern offer rate < 5%

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

### Watch points
- **Few EMEA slots**: Most global SWE interns are in SF / Bellevue / Sunnyvale; EMEA three hubs (Amsterdam + Aarhus + Sofia) combined hire tens of people annually
- **Amsterdam harder to get than Aarhus / Sofia**: Amsterdam is EMEA HQ, qualifications stricter, recruiter often redirects to Aarhus
- **2022–2023 hiring freeze impact**: 2022 Uber global hiring freeze, 2023 recovery, 2024 tightened again, internship quota fluctuates
- **Long process**: 5–6 rounds 5–6 weeks, not suitable for urgent offers
- **Visa rejection risk (non-EU + non-NL school)**: Some reports recruiter informs in first call "we can only sponsor NL enrolled students"

### Pros
- **Strong brand**: Uber resume backing works for both EU/US
- **Modern tech stack**: Go, Python, Java, Kotlin, Swift, distributed systems
- **Mature mentorship system**: 1 manager + 1 mentor + same cohort, 6 months enough to do meaningful production project
- **Good conversion treatment**: 30% ruling + RSU + Amsterdam cultural atmosphere
- **Company openness**: Uber Engineering Blog is industry top-tier technical blog (Cadence, Schemaless, Peloton, Aresdb etc.)

### Pros and cons
- (+) Amsterdam internship goes directly to EMEA HQ, experience extremely valuable
- (+) intern → grad → SDE I → SDE II pipeline smooth
- (-) Few EU slots, narrow path for non-EU students
- (-) Process long and competition extremely fierce, overall acceptance rate < 5%
