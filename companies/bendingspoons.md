## 1. 基本信息

- **欧洲办公室**: Milan (Italy, HQ 唯一办公地点); 个别 role 全 remote
- **实习岗位类型**: Software Engineer Intern, Cloud Engineer Intern, Recruiter Intern, Marketing Intern, Data Scientist Intern, ML Engineer Intern
- **实习时长**: **3 个月 (full-time only) 或 6 个月 (full-time / part-time)** — FAQ 原话; 部分 cohort (Spain top students summer intern) 是 June–September 4 个月 [来源 (实际打开过): https://jobs.bendingspoons.com/positions/6890dcc0099d1fb8baf95b57]
- **招聘周期**: Rolling, 全年开放, 但 summer (June–September) cohort 一般 1–3 月截止; 推荐 "as early as possible"

## New Grad / Junior 岗位

- **常见岗位名**: Software Engineer (entry-level), Cloud Engineer, Data Scientist, ML Engineer, Recruiter; 没有独立的 "Graduate Programme" 命名 — entry-level full-time 直接发 SWE offer
- **欧洲地点**: Milan (强制 first year 多回办公室, 之后可 remote)
- **是否有独立 Graduate Programme**: **无**, 走标准 SWE 招聘流程, intern → return offer 转 FT 是常规路径
- **非EU签证 (全职)**: **Italy Nulla Osta + Permesso di Soggiorno** (Decreto Flussi 配额制) 或 **UK Skilled Worker** (FAQ 明确 "we can sponsor a visa or residency permit for Italy or the UK"); EU Blue Card Italy 起薪门槛 ~€26k (highly qualified worker)
- **薪资范围**: 公司 FAQ 仅写 "all Spooners get paid competitively", 不公开 base 数字. 第三方 Glassdoor / Joberty 报告 entry SWE base €40k–€55k + 年度调整 (Q4 review)
- **申请窗口**: Rolling
- **面试流程差异 vs 实习**: 与 intern 相同 (take-home + 几轮 HR + 1 个 Hiring Team technical)
- **录取竞争 / 国际生 friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 高 — FAQ 列出 28 个 sponsor-eligible 国家 (含 China / Singapore / 全 EU / UK / US / Turkey / Ukraine 等), case-by-case 给其他国家. 米兰生活成本中等

## 2. 签证与国际学生政策

- **是否支持非EU学生**: **Yes (主动 sponsor)** — FAQ 列明 28 个国家直接支持 sponsorship: 全 EU + Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Bosnia, China, Egypt, Georgia, Hong Kong, Iceland, Kosovo, Lebanon, Liechtenstein, Moldova, Monaco, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Norway, Russian Federation, San Marino, Serbia, Singapore, Switzerland, Turkey, Ukraine, UK, US. 其他国家 case-by-case
- **是否需要提前持有工作许可**: No — 公司处理 Italian / UK residency permit 全流程
- **已知的 sponsorship 案例**:
  - FAQ 原话: "we can sponsor a visa or residency permit for Italy or the UK, as long as you're a resident of [eligible countries]" [来源 (实际打开过): https://jobs.bendingspoons.com/faq]
  - WorkWithVisa 列为活跃 sponsor [workwithvisa.com/companies/bending-spoons/]
  - Relocate.me 多个 Milan intern + FT 岗位标 visa sponsorship [relocate.me/italy/milan/bending-spoons/recruiter-intern-5558]
- **Relocation package** (FAQ 原话): "coverage of all reasonable transportation and temporary accommodation expenses for you, your close family members, and your pets, as well as support with a range of bureaucratic matters". 通常包括: **前 3 个月房租**, plane ticket, 行李运输, visa 费用, dedicated relocation buddy

## 3. 面试流程

- **总轮数**: 2–4 轮 (résumé screening → take-home test → HR 1–2 轮 → Hiring Team technical final)
- **每轮形式**:
  1. Résumé screening
  2. Take-home / 在线 "problem-solving" test — 不一定与岗位强相关, 测**抽象推理能力**, 可能耗几小时
  3. HR interview (culture / motivation, 1–2 轮)
  4. Final technical with Hiring Team (role-specific 深度题)
- **题型分布**: 抽象 problem-solving ~40% + role-specific (coding/system/DS) ~40% + behavioral ~20%
- **难度评估**: Glassdoor 难度 3.55/5 (相当于 LeetCode Medium–Hard 风格但更偏推理), 正面体验率 55.3%
- **OA 是否存在**: **Yes**, take-home 是必经环节
- **时长**: 平均 2 个月 (个别 "1 day" 的 outlier 是 fast track intern)
- **来源**: [Glassdoor Bending Spoons interviews (实际打开过): https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Bending-Spoons-Interview-Questions-E1164562.htm], [Recruiting process PDF (实际打开过): https://jobs.bendingspoons.com/docs/Culture%20&%20Policies%E2%80%94The%20Bending%20Spoons%20Recruiting%20Process.pdf]

## 6. 申请渠道与建议

- **官网投递**: https://jobs.bendingspoons.com (Greenhouse stack, 直接搜 Intern)
- **内推途径**: 现役 Spooner 推荐有效; LinkedIn 找 "Bending Spoons" + role keyword
- **简历/背景要求**: 强调 problem-solving 而非纯 stack; 抽象推理题考察核心, ICPC / Math Olympiad / Putnam 等竞赛背景占优; 不要写空泛 "passionate" 措辞 — Bending Spoons 极重个体能力 over 履历

## 7. 踩坑与社区评价

- **take-home 不要轻视**: 几小时的抽象推理题, 直接决定能否进 HR 轮; 有候选人反映 "和岗位毫不相关" 但其实是测核心能力
- **High-bar culture**: FAQ 原话 "we won't extend an offer until we're confident we've found the right candidate" — pass rate 偏低
- **3 个月 vs 6 个月**: 3 个月仅限 full-time; 在校生想 part-time 必须选 6 个月
- **Milan 第一年常驻**: 即使岗位写 "fully remote", FAQ 强烈建议 first year 多回办公室, 不愿搬家的非EU生需慎重
- **Performance review 严**: Q4 同伴评议决定下一年 comp, low performer 流失率明显
- **国际生 friendliness**: 是 EU 少数 sponsor 国家清单写在 FAQ 里 的公司, 中国 / 香港 / 新加坡均在列, 中国学生友好度 ⭐⭐⭐⭐
