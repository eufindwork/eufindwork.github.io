## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

- **London intern**: Tier 4 Student Visa, vacation full-time OK; Akuna London 2022 hub, intern data publicly scarce
- **Chicago intern**: **Akuna does not sponsor H-1B for intern** (official explicit "Legal authorization required on day 1") → international students need OPT/CPT (i.e. must be studying in US university first); same pattern as Jump $120/hr + Jump not sponsoring H-1B intern
- **London full-time**: Skilled Worker £41,700 (2026); Akuna London base £80-110K → far exceeds compliance; industry estimate has sponsorship license (2022 hub establishment, high international employee ratio)
- **Chicago full-time**: H-1B / O-1 path + Citizenship/PR preferred; industry estimate Akuna US H-1B sponsorship middling (myvisajobs shows data but not as much as DRW/Jump)
- **Advice**: non-EU students who get Akuna offer, **London path far friendlier than Chicago** (Chicago explicit day-1 legal authorization requirement → Chinese/Indian undergrads hard-blocked, unless already on OPT)

## 3. Interview Process

### Akuna Internship (Akunacademy) 4-Stage Standard Process

| Round | Content | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. OA HackerRank | 3 problems LeetCode med + multiple choice (probability/math) | 80 min | "Minimum Swaps" / "Delivery Management" / "Binary Circuit" typical; O(n) or O(n log n) complexity required |
| 2. Tech Phone 1 | resume + Python trivia + brief behavioral | 30 min | 1-2 team members Zoom |
| 3. Behavioral Phone 2 | culture fit + team intro | 15 min | Middle filter layer |
| 4. Super Day Chicago | 4-5 × 45 min back-to-back + lunch + (trader path) trading game | Full day onsite | Full travel covered, decision within 1 week |

### Akuna Trader Path (additional breakdown)

| Module | Content | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Mental math | Speed calculation (3-digit × 3-digit / fractions / percentages) | 35% |
| Probability | Card decks / ball urns / optimal stopping | 30% |
| Trading game | dice/median pricing | 25% |
| Behavioral | culture fit | 10% |

### Akuna SWE Path

- Algorithmic problems + low-latency emphasis
- C++ is the "hot path" standard, Python + Go for platform
- Not heavy on system design (vs Jump/HRT)

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **"Top preference" restriction**: Akunacademy listing explicit "Applicants acknowledge this is their top preference and won't be considered for other Quant/Tech roles that season" → once you apply Akuna, can't simultaneously receive Jump/DRW/Citadel etc. same cohort offers (industry-disputed clause, consistent with Akuna's all-in culture)
- **Chicago intern stipend 2026 substantial drop**: $96/hr (2024) → $62.50/hr (2026), reflecting prop firm industry general drop; but $130K annualized base still exceeds FAANG entry SWE
- **2-day Super Day Chicago**: full travel covered, but pass rate 30-40% (industry estimate), time + emotional investment large
- **OA 3 problems 80 min is industry benchmark**: nearly all prop firm OAs copy Akuna template; unfamiliar with graph BFS/DFS + probability cycle detection → directly fail
- **London 2022 establishment, scarce data**: Akuna London is relatively new hub, intern/FT public data scarce, mainly via LinkedIn DM referral; **but for non-EU students entering EU is a strong choice (relatively visa-friendly among Akuna locations)**
- **International student friendliness (Chicago)**: ⭐⭐ (explicit day-1 legal authorization requirement → international undergrads hard-blocked, unless already on OPT)
- **International student friendliness (London)**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (industry estimate has sponsorship, but need to confirm directly with recruiter)
- **vs Jump Trading**: Akuna specializes in options MM (narrow business), Jump cross-asset diversified + crypto/AI heavy bet; Akuna bar slightly lower + more standardized process + GPA hard threshold vs Jump looks at competitions/PhD more
- **vs DRW**: Akuna single product focused (options), DRW multi-product including crypto/RE; Akuna intern stipend 2026 substantial drop, conversely DRW with sign+completion bonus absolutely higher
