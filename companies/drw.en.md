## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

- **London intern**: Tier 4 Student Visa, vacation full-time OK; DRW doesn't get independent work permit (10-week summer uses student status)
- **Amsterdam intern**: non-EU students need to apply for GVVA / internship permit through Dutch university, DRW Cumberland Amsterdam intern history is sparse (actually no Amsterdam intern public on Levels.fyi)
- **London full-time**: Skilled Worker £41,700 (2026), DRW L2 SWE £148K base → far exceeds; DRW UK has sponsorship license
- **Amsterdam full-time (Cumberland)**:
  - Kennismigrant 2026: €4,357/month (<30) or €5,688/month (30+) gross base (excluding 8% holiday allowance) [Source: verified EU visa thresholds reference]
  - Cumberland Amsterdam Crypto MO Engineer / Relationship Manager industry base €70-90K → far exceeds <30 threshold, 30+ also basically compliant
  - Netherlands 30% ruling (first 5 years 30% tax-free) is Europe's strongest expat tax benefit
- **Suggestion**: non-EU student with simultaneous London + Amsterdam DRW offer, **Amsterdam often wins net income due to 30% ruling + longer multi-year permit** (20-30% advantage); but Cumberland is crypto subunit, career path differs from DRW main TradFi line

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **Chicago intern benefits extremely strong**: $25K sign + $15K completion + corporate housing + relocation, top-tier prop firm second-tier stipend; but need to stay in Chicago for 10 weeks (winter outdoor -20°C)
- **2-day final on-site**: all-inclusive flights + hotel, but pass rate ~30-40% (industry estimate)
- **London base slightly lower than Chicago**: $148K base vs $168K base (by exchange rate), but London cost of living higher → larger net income difference
- **Amsterdam is Cumberland-dominated**: main TradFi roles few, crypto roles many, note career path different
- **International student friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Chicago + London both visas processed; Amsterdam goes through Kennismigrant also processed)
- **Culture**: "trader-led" + lots of ex-CME culture, more macho fraternity-style than Jane Street/HRT; Don Wilson still CEO, strong founder culture
- **vs Jump Trading**: DRW broader business coverage (incl. crypto / RE / VC) + benefits comparable, Jump purer low-latency + higher bar + higher stipend ($120/hr vs $96/hr)
