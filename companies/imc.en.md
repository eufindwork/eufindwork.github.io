## 3. Interview Process

| Round | Content | Duration | Notes |
|------|------|------|------|
| 1. Application review | resume screen | - | "review submissions promptly", IMC faster than Optiver |
| 2. OA (HackerRank, SWE) | **2 coding questions (both hard), C++/Java/Python** | 120 min | no camera; "harder versions of what can be found online" |
| 2. OA (Trader) | IMC numerical test (similar to 80in8) | ~10 min | math heavy, similar to Optiver but slightly less volume |
| 3. First-Round Interview | recruiter / hiring manager phone — intro, motivation, skill walkthrough | 45-60 min | "very kind...generic questions" (2024-09 Amsterdam case) |
| 4. Technical Interview | live coding (HackerRank Codepair), data structure + algorithm | 60-90 min | algorithm + OOP, C++/Java |
| 5. Final-Round / Super Day | Multiple interview stations: tech + culture + future colleagues | 4-6 hours onsite (or virtual) | Amsterdam HQ; flights + hotel paid by IMC |
| 6. Offer | "feedback within a couple of days after reviewing" | - | usually within 1 week |

**IMC-specific notes**:
- OA difficulty: same tier as Optiver but one fewer gate (80in8), candidates feel "Optiver fails so come to IMC" — but IMC also independently eliminates
- "No prior financial markets experience required" — doesn't require finance background, but requires math + algorithm fundamentals
- Trader track independent process, not shared OA with SWE

## 6. Application Channels & Advice

- **Apply via official site**: https://www.imc.com/eu/careers — Greenhouse backend, note EU vs APAC vs US are separate portals (https://job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io/imc)
- **Referral**: important — internal employee referral can skip OA first gate; IMC campus events: TU Delft / Tilburg / TU Eindhoven / ETH / EPFL / Cambridge / Oxford / Imperial / Warwick
- **CV focus**:
  - C++ template metaprogramming + low-latency experience (RDMA / kernel bypass / DPDK / FPGA) directly bonus for engineering
  - Codeforces / IOI / IMO / Putnam direct signal
  - Heard on the Street + Quant Job Interview Q&A (green book) are required for Trader track
  - Simultaneously applying SWE + Trader tracks allowed at IMC (Optiver strictly limits one track)
- **Same-tier alternatives**: Optiver (Amsterdam, same tier) / Flow Traders (Amsterdam, ETF niche-leaning) / Jane Street (London) / Citadel Securities / HRT / DRW (Chicago/London/Amsterdam)

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **OA extremely hard, 2 hard questions**: Blind / Glassdoor users feedback "both far beyond LeetCode hard, time insufficient"
- **Process shorter than Optiver**: no mental math first round + no ZapN reaction test, but HackerRank single round elimination extremely high
- **Trading School intensive training**: 6 weeks concentrated in Amsterdam, international cohort + cultural immersion, heavy family ties may pressure [Source (verified opened): https://www.imc.com/eu/careers/recruitment-process]
- **Glassdoor 4.3/5**: higher than Flow Traders, on par with Optiver; complaints concentrated "OA too hard" / "late process waiting times long (Super Day waits 2-4 weeks)"
- **No equity**: all cash, this means bonus is core — bad year bonus may halve
- **International student friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — same tier as Optiver, abundant Chinese / Indian / overseas master onboarding cases
