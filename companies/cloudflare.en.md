## Intern Path

- **Role naming**: "Software Engineering Intern" / "Systems Engineering Intern" / "Security Engineering Intern" / "Product Manager Intern" — paired with "1.1.1.1 Internship Program" branding
- **Duration**: **12 weeks** (unified, each season)
- **Application window**:
  - **Posting rolling open from 2025-10-15**
  - Spring 2026: mostly closed (1-4 month batch)
  - **Summer 2026 (June-Aug)**: mainstream, most slots, application window 10 month - 1 month
  - **Fall 2026 (Sep-Dec)**: still open, applications to 5-6 month
- **Salary (stipend gross/month GBP / EUR)**:
  - **London intern**: ~£4,200-£5,000/month ("competitive prorated to entry-level" — Cloudflare L2 base ~£90K, prorated ~£7,500/month but intern usually 60-70%) [Levels.fyi intern data]
  - **Lisbon intern**: ~€2,800-€3,500/month (per entry-level Lisbon base ~€45-55K prorated)
  - **US intern (comparison)**: $25.32/hr = ~$4,400/month [Levels.fyi actual: https://www.levels.fyi/internships/Cloudflare/Software-Engineer-Intern/]
- **Eligibility**: In-school bachelor / master / PhD, **must be on-site 3-4 days/week** in hub office [official blog verified opened]
- **Sponsor policy (intern)**:
  - **UK (London)**: Cloudflare UK does not hold Skilled Worker, only holds GBM — intern **nearly impossible** to sponsor. Must already have UK Tier 4 / Student visa
  - **Lisbon**: Portugal Tech Visa path fast (1-3 months), Cloudflare is IAPMEI accredited employer [verified opened: https://www.eurotoptech.com/blog/visa-sponsorship-portugal-software-engineers-2026] — but intern goes via student exchange / D3 short-term, still relatively few
  - **NL student (UvA)**: Lisbon intern smoother than London
- **Process**: Recruiter screen → 1-2 tech rounds (Go / Rust language + system) → behavioral → offer; total 3-4 weeks
- **FT conversion rate**: 60-70% (Cloudflare internal funnel design goal high, 2026 1111 experiment likely to push higher)

## NG (New Grad / Junior Full-time) Path

- **Role naming**:
  - "Software Engineer L1" / "Systems Engineer L1" — all hubs
  - "Engineer, New Grad" — periodic posting
  - Lisbon: "Software Engineer L1, Lisbon"
- **Standalone cohort program**: No explicit grad scheme; new hire follows standard onboarding bootcamp (2 weeks)
- **Starting salary (base + bonus + RSU)**:
  - **London L1 SWE base**: £62-75K [Source (verified opened): https://www.levels.fyi/companies/cloudflare/salaries/software-engineer/locations/london-metro-area]
  - **London L2 median TC**: £88,122 (P50)
  - **London L3 median TC**: £107,914
  - **London full range**: £62K-£230K (L1 → L6)
  - **Lisbon L1**: €45-55K base, TC ~€55-70K (incl. RSU)
  - **Lisbon senior (L5)**: clear €100K TC [eurotoptech 2026 data]
- **Sponsor policy (FT)**:
  - **UK (London)**:
    - Cloudflare Limited holds **GBM Senior/Specialist Worker** A-rated license, **not** Skilled Worker [Source (verified opened): https://sponsorlist.co.uk/visa-sponsorship/cloudflare-limited/]
    - GBM requires: salary ≥ **£48,500** (or going rate, take higher), **12 months+ overseas Cloudflare work experience** (unless salary >£73,900)
    - **Result**: New grad **nearly cannot directly** get UK Cloudflare offer + visa — must first work at Cloudflare US / other office for 12 months, then internal transfer to London
  - **Lisbon**: Portugal Tech Visa, IAPMEI accredited, 1-3 months processing; also enjoys IFICI 20% flat tax 10 years [eurotoptech verified opened]
- **Application window**: Rolling, 10-2 month intensive
- **Process difference vs intern**: Adds one round system design + cross-team with staff/principal eng, total 5 rounds

## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

- **2026 UK thresholds**:
  - Skilled Worker £41,700 (Cloudflare doesn't hold license, not applicable)
  - **GBM Senior/Specialist Worker £48,500** (Cloudflare holds license, actual path used)
  - SOC 2134 Software Developer going rate ~£49,400
- **Cloudflare Limited key restriction** [sponsorlist.co.uk verified opened]:
  - Only holds GBM, doesn't hold Skilled Worker
  - This is the **only** GBM-only company among 6 on this list
  - Practical: HR receiving external grad résumé needing visa, replies "we can only sponsor via intra-company transfer, please apply to US / other geo first"
- **Portugal Tech Visa (Lisbon path)**:
  - IAPMEI accredited, Cloudflare on list
  - Processing 1-3 months (vs UK SW 8-12 weeks)
  - **IFICI benefit**: high-skill activity 20% flat tax 10 years
  - Suitable for non-EU passport holder (China / India / US / others)
- **GBM path**: Is Cloudflare UK's only channel, requires first 12 months at other office

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **GBM restriction is surprise trap**: Blind / Reddit many complaints — get UK offer then told "need 12 months Cloudflare overseas experience" then offer invalid. Must ask before applying
- **Lisbon office culture**: WLB excellent, 4 days remote OK (although official 3-4 in-office, actually flexible), looser than London
- **Lisbon equity conversion**: Public stock (NYSE: NET) ~$200, strong liquidity — more solid than Snyk pre-IPO
- **1111 intern plan**: Announced 2025-09-22, is 2.5x expansion of 2025 (actual ~400). **Don't believe all 1111 will offer** — is aspirational target, actual may be 700-900
- **Pros**: Tech depth (Workers runtime, R2, AI Gateway all industry-leading), public stock + bonus, dog-friendly office (Lisbon)
- **Cons**: London is sales-heavy, eng headcount much smaller than Lisbon — UK path difficult plus few roles, NL student should hardly choose London

## 8. Specific Assessment for UvA Students (NL student visa) ⭐

- **Core trade-off**: Cloudflare is **the company most should NOT choose UK / most should choose Lisbon** in this list
- **Intern feasibility**:
  - **London**: **Extremely low** (GBM doesn't open intern sponsor, must already be in UK)
  - **Lisbon**: **Medium** (12 weeks D3 / student exchange visa path, IAPMEI accredited accelerated)
  - **Recommendation**: Lisbon summer/fall intern apply 3 seasons
- **FT feasibility**:
  - **London**: **Zero** (GBM requires 12 months overseas Cloudflare work experience, grad doesn't qualify)
  - **Lisbon**: **High** ⭐⭐⭐ (Portugal Tech Visa + IFICI 20% flat tax)
  - Alternative path: First Cloudflare US / SG intern → get FT offer → after 12 months GBM transfer to London (detour 2-3 years, high cost)
- **NL path**: Cloudflare **no NL office**, not available
- **DE path**: Munich mainly sales, no eng
- **Estimated friction**:
  - London: **Extremely high** (actually blocked)
  - Lisbon: **Low-medium**
- **Suggestion**:
  - **Completely skip London**, directly choose Lisbon office when applying
  - Lisbon 2026 1111 program is historic window — apply Spring/Summer/Fall 3 seasons
  - Focus on Lisbon SWE L1 / Systems Eng L1 FT roles
  - Backup: Snyk London (most stable UK choice on this list), or detour Lisbon other IAPMEI accredited (OutSystems, Feedzai, Unbabel)
  - If got Lisbon offer, ask IFICI applicability (20% flat tax 10 years, huge value for non-EU passport)
