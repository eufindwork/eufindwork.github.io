## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

- **UK Skilled Worker 2026 threshold**: £41,700 / SOC 2134 going rate ~£49,400. G-Research SWE Grad / Quant Research Postgrad starting salary far exceeds.
- **CoS**: A-rated sponsor licence, historically sponsors hundreds of international candidates stably per year.
- **Internship visa**: UK school Student visa full set (vacation full-time / term ≤20h); international student ML Research Internship usually company supports Standard Visitor PPE or Tier 5 GAE — but SWE Internship more inclined toward UK school Student visa candidates (process simplified).
- **Graduate Route (PSW)**: UK school graduate 2-year PSW, G-Research accepts PSW candidates, subsequent transfer to Skilled Worker.
- **Placement Year**: 10-12 month industrial placement, suitable for UK school sandwich course students; international students need to meet visa work limit during enrollment.
- **ML College access**: post-hire internal training, no visa status limit.

## 6. Application Channels & Tips

- **Official application**: [gresearch.com/vacancies/](https://www.gresearch.com/vacancies/) — all London roles (45+ real-time vacancies) including Graduate / Intern. **Graduate Programme opens August**, earlier the better.
- **ML College**: post-hire free in-house ML training, biggest G-Research differentiator vs Two Sigma / Citadel — suitable for math/CS background but weaker ML experience candidates.
- **Referral**: G-Research referral effective but not as decisive as US HF, cold apply + strong CV pass rate not low.
- **CV focus**:
  - **SWE Internship**: strong CS fundamentals (DS/algo), Python + C++, any systems / distributed project
  - **Quant Research Postgrad**: Master's/PhD in math/stats/CS/physics, Kaggle / papers plus
  - **ML Research Intern**: ML/DL projects (PyTorch / TF), simple alpha modelling experience
  - **Placement Year**: suitable for UK school sandwich course students, 2 years + industrial year
- **Same-tier alternatives**: Man Group/AHL, Citadel, Squarepoint, Marshall Wace, Qube Research & Technologies — UK quant same tier. Top competition: Jane Street, HRT London, Two Sigma London.

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **Long process**: 4 stage, actual timeline 4-8 weeks, slower than Jane Street. Quant Research 4× 1hr panel is endurance test.
- **2 cohort intern**: SWE Internship has spring (Mar-Sep, 6 months) + summer (Jun-Sep, 10 weeks) two cohorts, choose time at application.
- **Top-tier benefits**: 35 days leave (30 + 5 office-based) + 9% pension + free lunch + central Soho Place office + on-site barista — Glassdoor London 4.3/5 comp&benefits rating.
- **Glassdoor summary**:
  - **Positive**: "smart and friendly colleagues" / "healthy work-life balance" / central office location good
  - **Negative**: "micromanagement" / "blame shifting" / "engineers used like pawns" / "too many managers" / some senior complain internal politics
  - **London rating**: 4.1/5 overall, 76% recommend (global 60% recommend, i.e., London office notably better)
- **International student friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — stable sponsor + multiple entries (intern / placement / grad / ML College) + transparent process. UK school advantage more obvious.
- **Conversion strong**: Intern → FT ratio per community ~50-70% (top interns almost stable to FT), one of strongest conversion pipelines in UK quant.
