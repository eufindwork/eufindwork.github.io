## 1. Basic Information

- **European offices**: Amsterdam (HQ) / Sofia (Bulgaria, tech hub) / Istanbul (tech hub)
- **Role types**: iOS / Android / Backend / AI Engineer / Security Engineer (strong mobile-first inclination)
- **Hiring cycle**: rolling, **no cohort program**
- **Important deadlines**: No fixed deadline, year-round rolling
- **IND Recognised Sponsor**: Yes (Bunq official Bunq Together self-described as "primary choice for new skilled migrants to NL" [Source: https://careers.bunq.com/])

## NG (New Grad / Junior Full-time) Path

> ⚠️ **NG channel also scarce** — Bunq historically extremely biased toward Mid / Senior, careers page 4 Amsterdam roles 0 entry-level. Junior usually enters via spontaneous CV submission

- **Role naming**: No standalone NG cohort; occasional "Software Engineer" (no level indication, actually leans mid)
- **Standalone cohort program**: **No** — Bunq style is more like fast-paced startup, no graduate program
- **Starting salary (base + bonus + equity)** (Levels.fyi / TechPays):
  - **Entry-level total comp**: **€39,338** (TechPays 8 data points) [Source (verified opened): https://techpays.com/europe/netherlands/bunq/entry-level] — **extremely low**, 50% lower than Adyen / Booking entry
  - Software Engineer (median Greater Amsterdam): **€56.2K** [Source (verified opened): https://www.levels.fyi/companies/bunq/salaries/software-engineer/locations/greater-amsterdam-area]
  - Bunq global SWE range: **€38.7K – €55.9K+** (Levels.fyi global range)
  - **Equity**: private company (CEO Ali Niknam holds majority), equity allocation opaque
- **Sponsor policy (FT)**: NL Kennismigrant. 2026 threshold < 30 €4,357/month ≈ €52.3K/year. **Bunq entry €39K significantly below standard HSM threshold**, must go via **reduced HSM for recent NL master grads (€2,801/month ≈ €33.6K)** to comply. This is the key risk for applying to Bunq.
- **Application window**: Year-round rolling
- **Process difference vs intern**: N/A (intern almost non-existent)

## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

- **Supports non-EU students**: Yes (IND Recognised Sponsor) — but **note salary matching risk**
- **Intern vs FT sponsor difference**:
  - Intern: Doesn't process (requires NL enrollment)
  - FT: Processes Kennismigrant, but entry-level salary may hit threshold
- **Known sponsorship cases**:
  - Bunq Together (official community) self-described as "primary choice for new skilled migrants to NL" [Source: https://careers.bunq.com/]
  - Dwayne Gefferie LinkedIn: NL fintechs Adyen / Mollie / **bunq** sponsor [Source (verified opened): https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dwaynegefferie_why-dutch-fintechs-adyen-mollie-and-bunq-activity-7185920215904309248-s52u]
- **2026 thresholds**: NL Kennismigrant <30 €4,357 / 30+ €5,942 / **NL master grad reduced €2,801**
- **Key risk**: Entry-level base €39K = €3,250/month, **below standard HSM <30 threshold (€4,357)**. UvA master graduate using reduced €2,801/month OK, but if applying directly from China fresh without zoekjaar path, **may not be able to sponsor**

## 6. Application Channels & Suggestions

- **Official site submission**: https://careers.bunq.com/
- **Referral path**: LinkedIn direct contact talent team; bunq doesn't rely on university career service
- **CV/background requirements**:
  - Strong mobile (iOS / Android / Kotlin / Swift) or backend (Java / Kotlin) projects
  - Finance / payments related experience plus
  - **English fluent, Dutch not required**
- **Suggestions**:
  - **Carefully evaluate culture** — Glassdoor global 2.3/5 (Amsterdam 3.6/5), many reviews describe "abusive and disrespectful culture", "late-night messages and weekend tasks as normal" [Source (verified opened): https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/bunq-Amsterdam-Reviews-EI_IE1007259.0,4_IL.5,14_IM1112.htm]
  - Suitable for those pursuing ownership / fast ship style, not suitable for work-life balance priority
  - Focus on **whether salary can pass HSM threshold** — must negotiate to base ≥ €4,357/month (< 30) or confirm reduced HSM eligibility

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **Glassdoor global 2.3/5** (Amsterdam 3.6/5) — highly polarized [Source (verified opened): https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/bunq-Amsterdam-Reviews-EI_IE1007259.0,4_IL.5,14_IM1112.htm]
- **Pros**: "great colleagues, fun to hang out, international team"; fast learning; high frequency shipping
- **Cons (serious)**:
  - "flashy on the outside but chaotic inside"
  - "running on fear and guilt with insane workloads"
  - "management uses pressure while bullying people into silence"
  - "abusive and disrespectful" culture
  - Recruiting process inconsistent, communication poor
- **CEO Ali Niknam** strong style, key decisions centralized
- **Salary vs Adyen/Mollie**: Bunq entry-level base significantly lower than peers (€39K vs Mollie €71K vs Adyen €60K+)
- **Equity**: private, illiquid

## 8. Specific Assessment for UvA Students (NL student visa) ⭐

- **Intern feasibility**: **Low** — no public intern channel, even if you can get stage offer, culture risk large. Student visa technically covers (16h semester / 40h vacation), Nuffic process complete, no company additional visa processing required
- **FT feasibility**: **Medium** — apply FT via zoekjaar, but has **2 major risks**:
  1. Entry-level base €39K stuck at HSM threshold edge, **must confirm offer is reduced HSM eligibility (€2,801/month)** or negotiate to base ≥ €4,357
  2. Glassdoor culture rating extremely low, suggest entry-level enter cautiously
- **Estimated friction**:
  - Intern: High (no channel + culture risk)
  - FT: Medium (salary threshold + culture)
- **UvA advantage**:
  - Bunq HQ at Amsterdam Naritaweg (Sloterdijk district), **UvA Roeterseiland campus 20 min metro**
  - UvA alumni at Bunq in some numbers but high turnover
- **Suggested application time window**:
  - Year-round rolling, but suggest **get other offers (Mollie / Adyen / Booking) as backup first** before considering Bunq
  - Only prioritize Bunq in following situations: (a) strong mobile / Kotlin background (b) seeking fast-paced startup experience (c) already Senior+ experience, not fresh
