## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

### Process level
- **Public data extremely sparse**: Expectation management is necessary, don't expect feedback within 1 week; process may take 4-8 weeks
- **"every effort but not guaranteed"**: Visa sponsorship not 100%, actively confirm before applying; after offer, visa process still may delay 1-2 months
- **Mission/culture fit is hard filter**: BFL emphasizes "open source" (FLUX [dev] open-sourced) + "scientific rigor"; pure commercial fine-tune farm background may not match

### Culture / business risk
- **Track competition intense**: Stable Diffusion 3 (Stability AI), DALL-E 3 / Sora (OpenAI), Midjourney, Ideogram, Imagen (Google), Aurora (xAI), Recraft, etc. on same stage; FLUX currently quality SOTA but moat is rapidly eroding
- **Stability AI cautionary tale**: Original Stability AI operations collapsed (core team exited 2024 to found BFL), warning about GenAI startup burn rate risk; BFL team reputation strong but business model (API + enterprise license) still being validated
- **Freiburg geographically remote**: Not Munich/Berlin big city, infrastructure inferior; but university town (University of Freiburg) cost of living low + natural environment good
- **In-person bias**: Official "We prefer to be in-office" — remote nearly unaccepted

### Known "pitfalls"
1. **Levels.fyi has no standalone data**: Negotiating salary lacks baseline, easy to be lowballed
2. **MTS all senior roles**: Don't expect "junior friendly" because it's a startup — company explicitly hires senior PhD
3. **Visa case-by-case**: Not guaranteed, must disclose nationality + visa status before final
4. **Freiburg not Munich/Berlin**: Spouse employment / international schools / metropolitan convenience all inferior; suited for pure research / nature lovers
5. **SF via H-1B**: 2026 H-1B lottery rate ~25%, not guaranteed first try

### International student friendliness
- **EU nationality**: Extremely friendly, Freiburg direct entry
- **Top venue first-author + top PhD**: Friendly, via O-1 (US) or Blue Card (DE)
- **New graduate master / undergrad**: Almost not friendly — don't try, company has no junior pipeline
- **Chinese nationality**: Top venue first-author PhD path feasible (Freiburg Blue Card), otherwise blocked
