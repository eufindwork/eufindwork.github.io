## 3. Interview Process

- **Total rounds**: 4-5 rounds (intern 3-4, full-time RS 5-6)
- **Each round format** (based on ML/Research roles):
  1. **Recruiter sync** (15 min)
  2. **Hiring Manager round** (45-60 min)
  3. **Problem solving no-coding** — 1 hr deep learning case study (e.g. self-driving perception design thinking) [Source (verified opened): https://www.glassdoor.co.uk/Interview/Wayve-Interview-Questions-E2225260.htm]
  4. **Programming** (1 hr DS&A)
  5. **PyTorch debugging** — given Google Colab notebook with CNN bug, you debug
  6. **VP leadership / Panel** (full-time)
- **Question type distribution**: DL case study 25% / Coding 25% / PyTorch debug 20% / ML breadth (RL + GenAI + multimodal) 20% / BQ 10%
- **Difficulty assessment**: difficulty 2.93/5 (overall medium), ML Engineer hardest
- **OA exists**: Some SWE roles have HackerRank-style first filter; ML/Research uses take-home / live

## 6. Application Channels & Advice

- **Official application**: https://wayve.ai/careers/ → FirstStage ATS https://wayve.firststage.co/jobs (real-time)
- **Referral channels**: London King's Cross cluster effect, Science team publicly active at NeurIPS / CoRL; Wayve PI direct cold email may be effective (especially PhD application)
- **Resume/background requirements**:
  - **PhD in robotics / ML / CV / RL** → Research Scientist Intern main path
  - **MSc / strong BSc + PyTorch projects** → SWE Intern / Applied Science
  - Self-driving / multimodal / GenAI / RL projects big bonus; real vehicle experience (ROS / autonomous vehicle stack) rare and highly valued
  - Papers (CVPR / ICRA / CoRL / NeurIPS) is the entrance ticket for Research Scientist

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **No Bachelor direct-entry grad scheme** — new graduates go PhD intern / MSc thesis, very few Bachelor direct-entry SWE (unless top school + strong ML projects)
- **ML Engineer process difficulty highest** — per Glassdoor; suggest applying SWE / Applied Science first to leverage conversion
- **PhD intern process slow (~29 days)** — don't put all eggs in one basket, parallel apply DeepMind / Meta Reality Labs / Toyota Research
- **Company expanded fast after SoftBank/NVIDIA $1B+, but industry risk** — self-driving timeline unpredictable, accept cash-heavy rather than short-term liquidity to be suitable
- **International student friendliness**: medium-high (Skilled Worker license + intern visa relocation both explicitly supported), more from-scratch friendly than Synthesia
