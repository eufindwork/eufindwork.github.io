## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **Supports non-EU students**: Yes (IND Recognised Sponsor)
- **Intern vs FT sponsor difference**:
  - Intern: does not arrange work permit, requires candidate to already have NL student visa / EU work rights (just complete Nuffic convention)
  - FT (Graduate / IC): arranges Kennismigrant
- **Known sponsorship cases**: Dwayne Gefferie (NL fintech recruiter) publicly names Mollie as sponsor [Source (verified opened): https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dwaynegefferie_why-dutch-fintechs-adyen-mollie-and-bunq-activity-7185920215904309248-s52u]
- **2026 thresholds**: NL Kennismigrant <30 €4,357 / 30+ €5,942 / **NL master grad reduced €3,122**
- **30% ruling**: From 2024 the 5-year period is tiered down 30%→27%→20%→15%, further tightening in 2027. Grad base €71.4K meets the criterion + residence ≥150km from NL border eligible

## 6. Application Channels and Tips

- **Official application**: https://jobs.mollie.com/departments/engineering
- **Referral channels**: LinkedIn search Mollie + contact talent acquisition, or via NL universities (UvA / TU Delft / VU) career fair
- **Resume / background requirements**:
  - Graduate: BSc / MSc CS, **fluent English (Dutch not required)**, demonstrate depth in at least 1 of PHP / Java / Python via projects
  - Plus: fintech / payments-related projects, OSS contributions, scalable systems experience
- **Tips**:
  - Note PHP — Mollie's core stack historically is PHP (Symfony), gradually migrating to Java / Python. Grad onboarding rotations may require multi-language adaptation
  - **Lisbon office hires most** (9 positions vs Amsterdam 4); if maximizing Mollie odds consider Lisbon (but lower salary and not NL)

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

- **Glassdoor 4.6/5** (253 reviews, 92% recommend) — extremely high [Source (verified opened): https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Mollie-Reviews-E1366725.htm]
- **Pros**: great culture, friendly colleagues, good work-life balance, growth opportunities
- **Cons**:
  - "heavy on-site expectation for low pay" — more on-site (Amsterdam HQ), less remote flexibility than Adyen
  - "no compelling growth path communicated beyond vague 'maybe after 6 months SME program'" — internal promotion path is vague
  - Hiring process is on the long side
- **Salary vs Adyen/Booking**: same-level base is 15-25% below Adyen, slightly below Booking Compass
- **Equity**: private company, equity is illiquid, can only be liquidated at IPO
