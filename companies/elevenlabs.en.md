## 3. Interview Process

- **Total rounds**: 3-4 rounds (intern), 4-5 rounds (full-time), 2-4 weeks
- **Each round format**:
  1. **Recruiter screen** (30 min): project + skills + role introduction
  2. **Coding screen / Take-home** (CoderPad, **90 min async**, 2-3 unrelated questions) [Source (verified opened): https://www.tryexponent.com/guides/elevenlabs-software-engineer-interview]
  3. **Behavioral round** — deep dive past projects
  4. **Coding round** — practical customer problem
  5. **Product decomposition** — UI + system design for use case (similar to system design)
- **Question type distribution**: Coding 50% / Product decomp 25% / Behavioral 15% / project deep-dive 10%
- **Difficulty assessment**: Difficulty 2.5/5 — relatively friendly among frontier labs; positive 50%
- **OA exists**: **Yes** — 90 min CoderPad take-home (async)

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **Sponsor skews senior** — entry-level new grad overseas candidate hit rate low; coming to UK to study MSc first is most stable path
- **Process fast (2-4 wk)** but coding volume large — 90 min CoderPad take-home stricter than peers
- **Remote-first but visa goes through hub** — even if JD writes remote, wanting sponsor still needs London / NYC / Warsaw
- **Poland Warsaw is cost-effective option** — same salary has higher PLN purchasing power, but sponsor difficulty higher than UK
- **International student friendliness**: London medium (senior-skewed), Warsaw friendlier but EU priority, NYC H1B lottery risk
