## 3. Interview Process

> **2026-06 retry**: Glassdoor has specific Collibra Brussels SWE interview reports — typical 5-stage: 30 min recruiter → 30 min live coding (React todo list) → 60 min tech interview (React hooks) → in-office PM + EM (60 min each) → offer + Sterling background check; total 4 weeks, 45% positive rating, average difficulty 2.9/5 [Source (verified opened): https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Interview/Collibra-Senior-Software-Engineer-Interview-Questions-EI_IE478548.0,8_KO9,33.htm]. Above process inference closely matches actual.

- **Total rounds**: 4–5 rounds (FT) / 2–3 rounds (intern)
- **Each round format**:
  1. Recruiter screening (30 min, sponsorship + motivation)
  2. Tech screen (HackerRank / CoderPad, 1h coding)
  3. System design (B2B SaaS data infra + metadata graph)
  4. Cross-functional (PM / EM)
  5. Values / culture (Collibra "Open / Direct / Kind" framework)
- **Question distribution**: Algorithm ~25% + system design ~30% + data modeling / governance domain ~25% + BQ ~20%
- **Difficulty assessment**: LC Medium mostly, system design leans metadata catalog / lineage graph; AI Governance role asks LLM evaluation / governance framework
- **OA exists**: Yes, HackerRank 60 min

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **Belgium high tax vs nominal salary**: Same dilemma as Showpad, brutto-net gap large
- **AI Governance is buzzword but slow landing**: Customers mostly enterprise compliance, pace not as fast as SF startups; those wanting frontier model shouldn't come
- **Dual HQ NY-BE time difference**: EU team often syncs with NY at night, work-life occasionally suffers
- **MyVisaJobs trend down**: 2022 peak 29 LCA → 2024 14 → 2025 10, US sponsorship no longer aggressive; EU sponsor still stable
- **Glassdoor BE rating**: ~4.0/5, culture flat, but recent 2 years layoff rounds caused employee rating fluctuation
- **International student friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐ Brussels itself highly international (EU institutions + NATO), English fully sufficient; Chinese community small
