- **Common role names (key distinction)**:

  - **Quantitative Researcher**: ML/stats/PhD heavy
  - **Strategy and Product**: business-oriented
  - **Machine Learning**: ML engineering / applications

## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

**Core conclusion: Jane Street London is on the Skilled Worker license list, full-time sponsor is standard operation; intern via Tier 5 GAE or student work path**.

- **Skilled Worker (full-time)**: Jane Street UK Partnership LLP / Jane Street Europe Limited hold sponsor license (worksponsors.co.uk confirmed); L1 base £175K+ far exceeds 2026 national threshold £41,700 and SOC 2136 going rate £55K
- **Intern visa**: UK students directly use Tier 4 visa with built-in work right (full-time vacation work); non-UK students go through Tier 5 GAE or Standard Visitor (depending on school country — US students common)
- **Relocation package**: provides flights + temporary housing + reimbursed moving expenses (intern fully-free corporate housing; FT has relocation package)
- **Nationality restrictions**: no obvious restrictions, but trading/research involving regulated function has stricter background checks (FCA SMCR)
- **EU 27 applicants (post-Brexit)**: same as non-EU via Skilled Worker; no settled status also OK
- **Working from home / remote**: London office mandatory onsite (4-5d/week), doesn't accept cross-national remote
- **Transfer from London to NY/HK**: internal transfer via L-1 (NY) or employment pass (HK), common path but requires 2+ years tenure

## 3. Interview Process

| Round | Content | Duration |
|------|------|------|
| 0. Application | janestreet.com/join-jane-street/ direct application; referral not required (Jane Street no referral culture) | - |
| 1. Online test | **50 questions in 8 minutes** mental math test (probability + mental math, e.g.: probability of 2 dice sum ≥10) — Jane Street's most famous round | 8 min |
| 2. Phone (recruiter) | experience + interest + projects | 30 min |
| 3. Tech phone 1 | algorithm + probability + market making puzzle; **any language allowed (Python/C++/Java/OCaml all OK)**, official says "don't try OCaml for the first time" | 45 min |
| 4. Tech phone 2 | deeper algorithm (DP / recursion / data structures) + probability puzzle upgrade | 45 min |
| 5. Final / onsite (London or NY) | 4-6 rounds: coding (OCaml or own) + probability + game theory + culture/judgment + lunch with engineer; SWE path adds system design (low-latency / message bus) | 5-6 hr |
| 6. Offer | usually 1-2 weeks, overall 4-6 weeks application → offer |

**Jane Street specifics**:
- **Mental math 50/8** is entry-level gate, no pass = direct reject
- Emphasizes **clever insight over rote computation** (interviewers reward "seeing the trick" over brute force)
- Interviewing in OCaml is optional, but functional programming mindset (recursion / immutability / pattern matching) is a plus
- **No LeetCode hard routine**, questions more like Putnam / Project Euler

## 6. Application Channels & Advice

- **Apply via official site**: https://www.janestreet.com/join-jane-street/open-roles/ — Jane Street almost only looks at official site applications
- **Referral**: **doesn't exist** or not popular — Jane Street anti-referral culture, candidates treated fairly; don't spend effort looking for referral
- **CV focus**:
  - **Codeforces / IOI / Putnam / IMO** significant bonus (Jane Street recruiter directly looks at competition ID)
  - GPA + school prestige important (Oxbridge / Imperial / UCL / Cambridge / Edinburgh top targets, overseas Harvard/MIT/CMU/Princeton)
  - Open source contributions (especially OCaml ecosystem: dune, async, core) huge bonus
  - Doesn't need finance background, don't force finance project
- **Same-tier alternatives**: HRT (algo dev / SWE) / Citadel Securities / XTX (Software Dev) / Optiver / IMC / Susquehanna (SIG)

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **50/8 mental math is hard gate**: 90% of unprepared people fail this round; preparation suggestion is to drill Heard on the Street + use Anki to train mental math
- **OCaml fear is overrated**: officially repeatedly emphasized "don't try OCaml first time", but some 1point3acres / Blind users think "knowing OCaml significantly improves impression"; compromise is understand functional concept but interview with Python
- **Slow but thoughtful process**: 4-6 weeks application → offer, slower than Citadel, but rejection letters usually give specific feedback
- **International student friendliness (UK visa)**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Skilled Worker license + active relocation, L1 base far exceeds threshold; **post-Brexit EU students same treatment as non-EU**
- **Compensation satisfaction extremely high, growth medium**: collective bonus = low internal PnL transparency, individuals don't know "what they're worth"; senior engineer hard to switch (no listed equity "golden handcuffs", but few companies in market can match Jane Street pay)
- **OCaml lock-in**: OCaml experience is niche in external market after leaving Jane Street; conversely Jane Street turnover rate extremely low (~3-5%/yr)
- **Glassdoor 4.3/5** (company-wide), but London data points few, mainly US employee feedback
