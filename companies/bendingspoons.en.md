## 1. Basic Information

- **European offices**: Milan (Italy, HQ sole office); a few roles fully remote
- **Internship role types**: Software Engineer Intern, Cloud Engineer Intern, Recruiter Intern, Marketing Intern, Data Scientist Intern, ML Engineer Intern
- **Internship duration**: **3 months (full-time only) or 6 months (full-time / part-time)** — FAQ verbatim; some cohorts (Spain top students summer intern) are June–September 4 months [Source (verified opened): https://jobs.bendingspoons.com/positions/6890dcc0099d1fb8baf95b57]
- **Hiring cycle**: Rolling, open year-round, but summer (June–September) cohort generally closes 1–3 month; recommend "as early as possible"

## New Grad / Junior Roles

- **Common role names**: Software Engineer (entry-level), Cloud Engineer, Data Scientist, ML Engineer, Recruiter; no standalone "Graduate Programme" naming — entry-level full-time directly receives SWE offer
- **European location**: Milan (mandatory first year to return to office often, remote later possible)
- **Standalone Graduate Programme**: **None**, follows standard SWE recruiting process, intern → return offer to FT is the regular path
- **Non-EU visa (full-time)**: **Italy Nulla Osta + Permesso di Soggiorno** (Decreto Flussi quota-based) or **UK Skilled Worker** (FAQ explicitly says "we can sponsor a visa or residency permit for Italy or the UK"); EU Blue Card Italy starting salary threshold ~€26k (highly qualified worker)

- **Application window**: Rolling
- **Interview process difference vs intern**: Same as intern (take-home + a few rounds HR + 1 Hiring Team technical)
- **Acceptance competition / international student friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ High — FAQ lists 28 sponsor-eligible countries (incl. China / Singapore / all EU / UK / US / Turkey / Ukraine etc.), case-by-case for other countries. Milan cost of living moderate

## 3. Interview Process

- **Total rounds**: 2–4 rounds (résumé screening → take-home test → HR 1–2 rounds → Hiring Team technical final)
- **Each round format**:
  1. Résumé screening
  2. Take-home / online "problem-solving" test — not necessarily strongly related to role, tests **abstract reasoning ability**, may take several hours
  3. HR interview (culture / motivation, 1–2 rounds)
  4. Final technical with Hiring Team (role-specific deep questions)
- **Question distribution**: Abstract problem-solving ~40% + role-specific (coding/system/DS) ~40% + behavioral ~20%
- **Difficulty assessment**: Glassdoor difficulty 3.55/5 (equivalent to LeetCode Medium–Hard style but more reasoning-oriented), positive experience rate 55.3%
- **OA exists**: **Yes**, take-home is a required step
- **Duration**: Average 2 months (occasional "1 day" outlier is fast track intern)
- **Sources**: [Glassdoor Bending Spoons interviews (verified opened): https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Bending-Spoons-Interview-Questions-E1164562.htm], [Recruiting process PDF (verified opened): https://jobs.bendingspoons.com/docs/Culture%20&%20Policies%E2%80%94The%20Bending%20Spoons%20Recruiting%20Process.pdf]

## 6. Application Channels & Suggestions

- **Official site submission**: https://jobs.bendingspoons.com (Greenhouse stack, search Intern directly)
- **Referral path**: Active Spooner referrals are effective; LinkedIn search "Bending Spoons" + role keyword
- **CV/background requirements**: Emphasize problem-solving rather than pure stack; abstract reasoning questions test core capability, ICPC / Math Olympiad / Putnam competition background advantageous; don't use vague "passionate" wording — Bending Spoons heavily values individual ability over résumé

---

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **Don't underestimate take-home**: Several hours of abstract reasoning questions directly decide whether you advance to HR round; some candidates report "completely unrelated to the role" but it actually tests core capabilities
- **High-bar culture**: FAQ verbatim "we won't extend an offer until we're confident we've found the right candidate" — pass rate is low
- **3 months vs 6 months**: 3 months full-time only; students wanting part-time must choose 6 months
- **Milan first-year residence**: Even roles listed as "fully remote", FAQ strongly recommends first year to return to office frequently, non-EU students unwilling to relocate need careful consideration
- **Performance review strict**: Q4 peer review determines next year's comp, low performer attrition is significant
- **International student friendliness**: One of the few EU companies with sponsor country list written in FAQ, China / Hong Kong / Singapore all included, Chinese student friendliness ⭐⭐⭐⭐
