## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

- **London internship**: Tier 4 Student Visa, full-time vacation OK; 10 weeks summer via student status
- **Amsterdam internship**: Netherlands university for GVVA / intern permit, Jump historical intern data few (Levels.fyi no Amsterdam intern public)
- **London full-time**: Skilled Worker active sponsor (license #5976015 verified 2026-05-27), £41,700 (2026), Jump London SWE base £150K+ → far exceeds compliance
- **Amsterdam full-time**: Kennismigrant 2026 = €4,357/month <30 or €5,688/month 30+; Jump Amsterdam SWE / Quant Trader base far exceeds threshold; 30% ruling bonus 5 years tax-free
- **Recommendations**: non-European students:
  - If pure SWE → London more direct (Skilled Worker 5 years ILR endpoint close)
  - If Quant Research/Trader + PhD → Amsterdam because 30% ruling + small startup feel + European main, actual net income + career growth better
- **French/Dutch requirements**: neither required (Jump culture is English-first)

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **Top stipend ($120/hr Chicago intern)**: industry top 3 alongside HRT/Citadel Securities; but bar also top 3, acceptance rate industry estimate < 1%
- **Extremely low-profile culture**: almost no PR, Glassdoor reviews fewer than DRW/Citadel; engineers > traders headcount ratio reversed (opposite of DRW/Jane Street)
- **Amsterdam = European center**: 2018 established, "small startup feel", but leads entire European trading desk → full-time visa path stable; intern data few
- **London slightly lower stipend than Chicago intern**: $80/hr vs $120/hr, but housing/transport included, net experience still good (London is European quant center)
- **C++ rabbit hole**: SWE interview C++ deep questions memory order / atomics / ABI / UB; unfamiliar with modern C++ directly fails
- **International student friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (UK Skilled Worker active sponsor verified + Amsterdam Kennismigrant + 30% ruling; intern stipend top-tier)
- **vs DRW**: Jump higher engineer density / higher bar / higher stipend / purer business (DRW includes crypto/RE/VC, Jump almost pure prop trading + Jump Crypto/AI labs)
- **vs HRT**: industry often discussed together, HRT historically older + Citadel lineage, Jump more indie + AI/Crypto heavy bet
