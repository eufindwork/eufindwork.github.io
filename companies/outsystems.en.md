## 3. Interview Process

- **Total rounds**: 3-4 rounds, total ~21 days (Glassdoor avg) [Source (verified opened): https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/OutSystems-Interview-Questions-E642590.htm]
- **Per-round format**:
  1. Screening interview with project manager (~1 hour)
  2. **Take-home challenge** — 1 week to complete exercise, evaluates analytical + technical ability
  3. Challenge presentation (presentation + Q&A with team)
  4. Director interview (1 hour, culture + senior tech)
  5. Team fit talk (1 hour, peer interaction)
- **Question distribution**: Take-home challenge 40% / Tech deep-dive 25% / Algo + DB 20% / Culture 15%
- **Difficulty assessment**: medium. SQL / data structures / OOP / design patterns are baseline; low-code platform knowledge not mandatory (except OutSystems-specific dev roles)
- **Whether OA exists**: usually no standalone OA, take-home challenge substitutes

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

- 1-week take-home is standard — don't rush to the last day
- Lisbon SWE salary is **significantly lower** than Paris / Berlin, but NHR (Non-Habitual Resident) tax benefit is useful for newcomers to Portugal (first 10 years 20% flat rate)
- Careers page **no explicit visa sponsor statement** — is a community pitfall, must confirm via email
- OutSystems is a low-code platform company, R&D genuinely does underlying SWE / platform; not "low-code dev" roles
- International student friendliness: Lisbon tech scene English-first, Tech Visa process completes in 1-3 months, friendly
