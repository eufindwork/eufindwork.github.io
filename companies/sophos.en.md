## Intern Path

- **Role naming**:
  - **Software Engineering Intern - 12 Month Placement** (flagship role, Abingdon, June/July 2026 start) [Source (verified opened): https://startup.jobs/software-engineering-intern-placement-year-sophos-7347991]
  - "Threat Analyst Intern" — Abingdon SophosLabs
  - "Co-op Program (NSG / ESG)" — 4 or 8 months, winter or summer
- **Duration**: **12 months** (placement, strongly promoted) or 4-8 months co-op
- **Application window**:
  - **Placement 2026/27**: opens Oct-Nov, Nov-Dec recruitment days (official 2: Tue Nov 4 + Thu Nov 6), Jan offer, June/July 2026 start
  - Summer intern: Oct-Jan window
- **Salary (stipend gross/month GBP)**:
  - **Placement**: **£27,000 p.a.** (= £2,250/month gross) [Source (verified opened): startup.jobs Sophos placement actual number]
  - Summer intern: pro rata
- **Eligibility**: enrolled undergraduate 2nd year completed placement (UK sandwich year), or master's gap year. **Fully remote by default**, optional Abingdon onsite
- **Sponsor policy (intern)**:
  - UK Tier 4 / Student visa holders OK
  - **NL student visa**: cannot directly take UK intern (company doesn't open intern SW sponsor)
  - DE Karlsruhe: historically no intern posting (from Glassdoor + careers verification)
- **Process**: Online application → recruitment day (group task + interview, same day) → offer
- **FT conversion rate**: high (placement designed to funnel grad, ~60%+ convert FT)

## NG (New Grad / Junior full-time) Path

- **Role naming**:
  - "Graduate Software Engineer" — Abingdon [Source (verified opened): https://careers.sophos.com/job/Abingdon-Graduate-Software-Engineer-OX14-3YP/728526502/]
  - "Associate Threat Analyst" — Abingdon SophosLabs
  - "Junior Software Engineer" — Karlsruhe DE (occasional)
- **Independent cohort program**: New Grad portal exists [sophos.com/en-us/careers/new-graduates verified opened] — graduate hire concentrated onboarding (September), paired mentor + 6 months ramp
- **Starting salary (base + bonus + RSU)**:
  - **Abingdon Grad SWE base**: £32-38K (Glassdoor + Indeed extrapolation, placement £27K → Grad +20-30%)
  - **Karlsruhe Junior SWE base**: €45-55K (DE standard entry-level + cybersec premium)
  - After privatization **no public stock**, RSU = phantom equity (Thoma Bravo vesting, 5+ yr), TC numbers nominally lower than listed peers
  - **TC Abingdon Grad**: ~£32-38K + 5-10% bonus = ~£35-42K
  - **TC Karlsruhe Junior**: ~€48-58K
- **Sponsor policy (FT)**:
  - **UK**: Sophos Limited holds Skilled Worker license (pending specific huntukvisasponsors verification, historically licensed); but grad base £32-38K **far below £41,700 SW threshold** — needs NEC (£33,400) + 70% new entrant discount
  - Going rate SOC 2134 ~£49,400, new entrant 70% = ~£34,580 — **most grad roles on borderline**, company case-by-case decides sponsor (historically conservative)
  - **DE (Karlsruhe)**: Sophos Technology GmbH can sponsor EU Blue Card (€45,934 shortage threshold 2026) — Junior SWE €45-55K can pass Blue Card, **DE path actually more stable**
- **Application window**: opens Sep, Dec-Feb concentrated
- **Process differences vs intern**: 1 more hiring manager + technical deep dive, total 3-4 rounds

## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

- **2026 UK thresholds**: Skilled Worker £41,700, NEC £33,400, SOC 2134 Software Developer going rate ~£49,400
- **2026 DE thresholds**: EU Blue Card shortage occupation (IT) **€45,934**, normal €48,300
- **UK side**:
  - Sophos Limited historically holds Tier 2 / Skilled Worker — but **placement intern doesn't open sponsor**, grad SWE £32-38K on SW threshold borderline
  - Most international grads actually don't get sponsor, company leans toward hiring home / settled status
- **DE side ⭐**:
  - **Karlsruhe Junior SWE €45-55K can take EU Blue Card** (shortage occupation €45,934)
  - Karlsruhe Sophos is the old Astaro company, acquired by Sophos in 2011, has licensed sponsor experience
  - DE path friendly to NL students (cross-border from NL to DE via Blue Card, process 4-8 weeks)
- **GBM path**: not applicable (Sophos UK is legal entity, no intra-company transfer)

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **Impact after Thoma Bravo privatization**: similar to Darktrace, RSU path terminated, changed to phantom equity (5+ yr vest). Old employee attrition increased (2022-2024 multiple layoff rounds)
- **2024 Secureworks acquisition**: completed 2025-02, integration period chaotic, duplicate role layoffs (mainly US side). UK / DE side relatively stable
- **Placement review**: RateMyPlacement score ~4.5/5 — UK sandwich year, Sophos is popular, learn a lot (real project, not used as intern)
- **Karlsruhe office culture**: leans traditional DE, WLB excellent (35h/week), but slow promotion, salary lower than Munich Big Tech
- **Abingdon disadvantage**: near Oxford, commute inconvenient (weak public transport), most employees drive; remote-friendly somewhat eased
- **Advantages**: cybersec old brand, codebase stable, learn deep (kernel-mode driver, antivirus engine, real stuff)

## 8. For UvA Students (NL Student Visa) — Specific Assessment ⭐

- **Core trade-off**: Sophos is the **only UK + DE dual-line** of the 6 companies, DE Karlsruhe path actually most suitable for NL students
- **Intern feasibility**:
  - **Abingdon placement**: **zero** — placement designed for UK student sandwich year, no international sponsor
  - **Karlsruhe**: **low** — no clear intern posting, occasional working student (Werkstudent) but needs EU work right
  - **Advice**: skip intern, apply grad directly
- **FT feasibility**:
  - **Abingdon Grad SWE**: **low** — £32-38K base on SW threshold borderline, historically conservative sponsor, mostly to settled status
  - **Karlsruhe Junior SWE**: **medium-high** ⭐ — €45-55K passes Blue Card €45,934 shortage threshold, DE company sponsor process smooth
- **NL path**: Sophos **no NL office**, not applicable
- **DE path** ⭐: Karlsruhe is best path
  - From NL student status post-graduation (zoekjaar) → transition to DE Blue Card is conventional migration
  - Blue Card processing 4-8 weeks, faster than UK SW
  - 21 months later can get DE permanent residency (Niederlassungserlaubnis), much faster than UK ILR (5 yr)
- **Expected friction**:
  - Abingdon UK: **high**
  - Karlsruhe DE: **medium-low** ⭐
- **Advice**:
  - **Strongly recommend Karlsruhe** (bypass UK)
  - Karlsruhe roles few, focus on: Junior SWE / Cybersec Engineer / Cloud Security
  - Parallel apply: Tier 1 list ARM (Cambridge, UK high sponsor), Sophos DE
  - Don't put hope on Abingdon placement / grad
