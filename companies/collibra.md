## 2. 签证与国际学生政策

- **是否支持非EU学生**: **Yes (有历史)** — US H-1B 持续 sponsor (2025 财年 10 LCA + 12 approvals / 1 denial), EU Brussels 端通过 Single Permit / Highly Skilled
- **是否需要提前持有工作许可**: No
- **已知的 sponsorship 案例**:
  - MyVisaJobs 数据: 2025 财年 Collibra H-1B 平均 base $180,670, Green Card avg $192,017, peak 2022 共 29 certified LCA [来源 (实际打开过): https://www.myvisajobs.com/employer/collibra/]
  - 历史 sponsored 岗位 top: Software Engineer (5), Senior SWE (4), Senior Enterprise Architect (2)
  - Brussels intern → FT pipeline 多见 KU Leuven / ULB 校招通道
- **EU Brussels-Capital Region highly skilled 门槛**: ~€51k brutto/年 (2026), Flemish 区 ~€48k. Engineering FT base 满足无压力

## 3. 面试流程

> **2026-06 retry**: Glassdoor 已有具体 Collibra Brussels SWE 面经 — typical 5-stage: 30 min recruiter → 30 min live coding (React todo list) → 60 min tech interview (React hooks) → in-office PM + EM (60 min each) → offer + Sterling background check; 总时长 4 周, 45% positive rating, 平均难度 2.9/5 [来源 (实际打开过): https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Interview/Collibra-Senior-Software-Engineer-Interview-Questions-EI_IE478548.0,8_KO9,33.htm]. 上述流程推断与实际接近.

- **总轮数**: 4–5 轮 (FT) / 2–3 轮 (intern)
- **每轮形式**:
  1. Recruiter screening (30 min, sponsorship + motivation)
  2. Tech screen (HackerRank / CoderPad, 1h coding)
  3. System design (B2B SaaS data infra + metadata graph)
  4. Cross-functional (PM / EM)
  5. Values / culture (Collibra "Open / Direct / Kind" framework)
- **题型分布**: 算法 ~25% + system design ~30% + data modeling / governance domain ~25% + BQ ~20%
- **难度评估**: LC Medium 居多, system design 偏 metadata catalog / lineage 图; AI Governance 岗会问 LLM 评估 / 治理 framework
- **OA 是否存在**: Yes, HackerRank 60 min

## 7. 踩坑与社区评价

- **比利时税重 vs 名义薪**: 跟 Showpad 同样困境, brutto–net 落差大
- **AI Governance 是 buzzword 但落地慢**: 客户多 enterprise compliance, 节奏不如 SF 初创快; 想做 frontier model 的别来
- **双总部 NY-BE 时差**: EU 团队晚上经常跟 NY sync, work-life 偶尔吃亏
- **MyVisaJobs trend down**: 2022 peak 29 LCA → 2024 14 → 2025 10, US sponsorship 不再激进; EU sponsor 仍稳
- **Glassdoor BE 评价**: 4.0/5 左右, 文化扁平, 但近 2 年 layoff round 让员工评分波动
- **国际生 friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐ Brussels 本身国际化高 (EU 机构 + NATO), 英语完全够用; 中文社区小
