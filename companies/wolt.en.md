## 3. Interview Process

1. **Take-home assignment** (Applied Science 2026 process explicitly requires, big weight)
2. Recruiter screen (30 min)
3. Hiring manager (45 min)
4. Technical interview (live coding + system design, 60-90 min)
5. Team fit + Wolt values (Entrepreneurial / Caring etc.)
6. Offer

> Multi-source Glassdoor / Reddit r/Helsinki: take-home is the heaviest component of Wolt hiring, determining 80% of admission results.

## 8. For UvA Students (NL student visa) — Specific Assessment ⭐

- **Core trade-off**: Already in NL holding student visa → choosing Wolt means **giving up NL zoekjaar (€2,801 reduced HSM)**, exchanging for Finland Blue Card / specialist permit
- **Intern feasibility**: ❌ **Zero** — Wolt explicitly does not sponsor intern + needs FI already having work visa; **skip intern, go directly to FT**
- **FT feasibility**:
  - **Helsinki SWE entry → high** — Wolt €99K far exceeds Finland Blue Card €47,244 threshold; FT sponsor explicit + DoorDash mobility process
  - **Stockholm / Berlin office → don't apply R&D** (these two offices mainly ops, tech heavy in Helsinki)
- **Estimated friction**:
  - Intern → high to infeasible
  - FT → low (sponsor explicit + starting salary loosely covers threshold)
- **UvA advantage invalidated**: this company not in NL, no zoekjaar benefit
- **Advice**:
  - **If you don't plan to move to Helsinki — Wolt not worth applying (intern infeasible, FT requires on-site)**
  - **If you're willing to go to Helsinki — Wolt is Finland tech first tier, starting €99K is Helsinki top, FT sponsor policy clear, recommended to apply**
  - Skip intern path completely, directly apply entry-level SWE / Applied Scientist FT roles
