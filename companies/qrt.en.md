> Information richness: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Levels.fyi Paris L3 €112K total + London Quant Researcher £118K actual + Built In London 2026 internship confirmed + 5-6 round interview process clear; stock breakdown missing — QRT is private with no RSU, all deferred bonus)

- **Common role names**:
  - **Quantitative Researcher** (Paris/London primary, heavy ML/Stats)
  - **Quantitative Trader** (systematic strategy build + monitor)
  - **Software Engineer** (Trading Tech / systems / low-latency)
  - **Research Engineer** (Quant + SWE hybrid)
  - **Quantitative Developer** (Paris has a dedicated students/new grads section)
- **European locations**: London (HQ, most) / Paris (Quant Dev + Quant Research stronghold, French not required) / also Zurich / Dubai / Hong Kong / Singapore
- **Independent Graduate Programme**: **Semi-structured**. No traditional "Graduate Scheme" brand, but (1) official Paris site has dedicated "Students and New Graduates" entry (2) 2026 Early Access Internship Programme 5-6 months, year before final / penultimate / final year + Master/PhD; (3) general application email `campus@qube-rt.com` [Source (verified opened): https://www.qube-rt.com/careers; https://openquant.co/job/2026-early-access-internship-research-qube-research-and-technologies/2756]
- **Non-EU visa (full-time)**:
  - **London**: Provides Skilled Worker visa sponsorship (£41,700 2026 general threshold, QRT London Quant Researcher £112K base far exceeds)
  - **Paris**: Passeport Talent Salarié Qualifié, 2026 threshold €39,582 gross/year (= €2,707/month, i.e. 1.5 SMIC), QRT Paris SWE L3 base ~$119K (~€110K) far exceeds [Source (verified opened): https://www.testciviquefrance.fr/articles/passeport-talent-salarie-qualifie-2026-salaire-conditions-procedure]
  - QRT is a French-system fund, officially relatively skilled at processing Passeport Talent for non-EU students (especially Polytechnique / ENS international students)
- **Salary structure (quant-specific, key)**:
  - **Intern (Paris/London)**: QRT does not publish intern stipend, but efinancialcareers lists QRT as "elite niche quant internship", industry benchmark internship €4-6k/month (London £5-7k/month) — not as outlier as Jump Trading, but far above European SWE average
  - **Full-time New Grad (London Quant Researcher)**: median **£118K total / £112K base / £6K bonus / £0 stock** (Levels.fyi L1, latest 2026-05-28) — note bonus number is low because of deferred comp [Source (verified opened): https://www.levels.fyi/companies/qube-research-and-technologies/salaries/data-scientist/title/quantitative-researcher]

## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

**Intern perspective (Paris + London dual entry):**

- **Paris internship**: Goes via **French intern convention de stage** (school + company + student tripartite agreement), non-EU students need French/EU school enrollment first, QRT does not handle separate work permits; intern stipend (gratification) legal minimum €4.35/hour × 35h × 4 weeks ≈ €659/month, but quant interns offer far more (industry €3-5k/month)
- **London internship**: Goes via **Student Visa (Tier 4)** + Tier 4 students can work 20 hours/week (term-time) / 40 hours (vacation); QRT internship counts as vacation full-time; no separate work permit needed
- **Full-time non-EU/UK students**:
  - **Paris full-time**: Passeport Talent Salarié Qualifié 2026 = €39,582/year gross threshold (changed from SMIC multiplier to fixed amount); QRT Paris L3 SWE €110K+ base far exceeds, directly compliant [Source: https://www.testciviquefrance.fr/articles/passeport-talent-salarie-qualifie-2026-salaire-conditions-procedure]
  - **London full-time**: Skilled Worker visa 2026 = £41,700/year, QRT London Quant Researcher £112K base directly compliant; QRT has sponsorship license
- **French requirement**: Paris office working language is English, French not required (many international employees); but customer-facing or local admin gives bonus
- **Recommended path**: Non-EU students going **Paris internship → Paris full-time Passeport Talent** is the smoothest; UK via Graduate Visa 2-year buffer then switching to Skilled Worker also works

## 3. Interview Process

| Round | Content | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. CV screen | recruiter call, team intro | 30 min | Chinese / French / English all OK, default English |
| 1. OA | 2 LeetCode easy/medium, Python | 90 min | HackerRank platform, auto-graded |
| 2. Tech Phone 1 | Python data structures + team fit | 60 min | Includes behavioral, intros to each team scope |
| 3. Tech Phone 2 / Quant questions | probability + statistics + ML (Quant Res) / LeetCode medium-hard (SWE) | 60-75 min | Quant Researcher emphasizes ML/regression/time series |
| 4. Final onsite (London/Paris) | team mixed interviews, 3-4 employees back-to-back + lunch | half day | Meet with future collaborating team |
| 5. Offer | within 1-2 weeks | — | Includes deferred comp ratio choice |

**Overall time**: 4-8 weeks; SWE track "easiest" (Glassdoor users), Quant Researcher track hardest

## 6. Application Channels & Advice

- **Official submission**: https://www.qube-rt.com/careers (note the main page often shows "no matching jobs", but careers/job listings are actually applicable)
- **Campus email**: `campus@qube-rt.com` (one of the only intern + grad channels, recommend direct CV PDF attachment)
- **Referrals**: École Polytechnique / ENS / CentraleSupélec / ENSAE alumni network is dense; search "QRT" + school on LinkedIn
- **CV focus**:
  - French-system funds look at **grandes écoles** background (Polytechnique / ENS / CentraleSupélec / Mines / ENSAE), MFE or PhD gives big bonus
  - Quant competition / Kaggle / OS projects (C++/Python) > generic internships
  - Recommended books: *Heard on the Street* (Crack) / *Quant Job Interview Questions and Answers* (Mark Joshi) / *A Practical Guide to Quantitative Finance Interviews* (Xinfeng Zhou)
- **Same-tier alternatives**: Two Sigma, Citadel, Squarepoint (French-system peer, see squarepoint.md), DRW (US-system but London has roles), G-Research

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **Deferred comp double-edged sword**: 40%+ employees participate in deferred plan, senior 75% bonus locked 3 years → "golden handcuffs"; but deferred invested in QRT-managed fund returned +30% in 2024, effectively a stealth raise
- **Some employees only get 3% profit share**: efinancialcareers reports some employees dissatisfied with individual P&L allocation, but QRT emphasizes "firm-wide collaborative" culture, i.e. collective P&L > individual
- **Final round process slow**: WSO users report individual finals with 2-3 weeks no feedback, need active follow-up
- **International student friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Paris/London dual visa paths; QRT recruits many international students from grandes écoles = MFE/MSE PhD application channel smooth)
- **French not required**: Paris office working language is English (95%+ internal communication in English), but work life still needs basic French
- **Glassdoor difficulty 2.89/5**: Friendlier than Squarepoint (3.21) / Jump Trading (≥3.5)
