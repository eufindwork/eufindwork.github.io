## 1. Basic Info

Google's core EMEA internship programs are divided into three types with different positioning, must distinguish clearly:

| Program | Target | Duration | Cities (2025) | Notes |
|------|---------|------|------------|------|
| **STEP Intern (Student Training in Engineering Program)** | first-year undergrad (3-year program) / second-year (4-year program), i.e., lower-year with ~2 years remaining | 10-12 weeks (2025 is 6/30-9/26) | London / Zurich / Munich / Dublin | Historically biased toward Underrepresented Groups, but actually any background can apply. Return offer rate high, can directly continue to next year SWE intern |
| **Software Engineering Intern** | undergrad third year and above / master / PhD in-school | 10-14 weeks | Zurich / London / Munich / Warsaw / Dublin / Paris, etc. | Standard SWE internship, process similar to New Grad |
| **Research Intern (PhD)** | PhD in-school, with publications | 12-14 weeks | Zurich (incl. DeepMind) / London / Paris / Munich | Host match needed, depends on research direction fit |
| **Student Researcher** | Bachelor/Master/PhD all OK | flexible (part-time/full-time) | EMEA university locations | 2025 cycle deadline 2/26/2025, rolling review |

**Key layoff/hiring background (2024-2025):**
- Google experienced 2023 mass layoffs (12,000 people), but 2024-2025 STEP/SWE Intern programs in EMEA **not cancelled**, hiring pace normal but headcount notably tightened from 2021-2022 peak.
- Zurich office historically plays role of "engineering powerhouse" in Europe, internship slots slightly fewer than London but level more technical.
- Warsaw mainly hosts Google Cloud, Chrome, YouTube infra teams.
- Munich biased Chrome / Privacy / Hardware.

Source: [internshipp.com STEP Guide](https://internshipp.com/blog/google-step-internship-guide), [buildyourfuture.withgoogle.com](https://buildyourfuture.withgoogle.com/programs/step/)

## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

### 2.1 UK (London)
- **Tier 5 Government Authorised Exchange (GAE) visa** is the main path for interns, Google provides through authorized sponsor (like BUNAC) for international students.
- Internship to FT requires **Skilled Worker visa** (formerly Tier 2), Google is registered sponsor.
- International students (Student visa holders) can full-time intern during summer, no additional visa needed.
- Source: [DavidsonMorris UK Internship Visa Guide](https://www.davidsonmorris.com/internship-visa-uk/)

### 2.2 Switzerland (Zurich) - Highest Difficulty
- **EU/EFTA students**: can work upon enrollment (Permit B for students), employer simple notification.
- **Third-country students (China/India/US, etc.)**: studying in Switzerland **must wait 6 months** to start any paid internship, unless the internship is required by course (ECTS credits attached).
- **Direct application from overseas to Zurich Intern**: Google must apply for work permit through **Office of Economy (AWI)**, process relatively slow, usually needs IAESTE assistance (simplified visa application for third-country nationals).
- Practice: Zurich office has slot limits on international students (Swiss labour market test), European local applicants prioritized.
- Source: [Kanton Zurich Students & Interns](https://www.zh.ch/en/wirtschaft-arbeit/erwerbstaetigkeit-auslaender/studierende-und-praktikanten.html), [ETH Zurich Non-EU Employment](https://ethz.ch/en/studies/international/after-arrival/employment/non-eu.html)

### 2.3 Germany (Munich)
- **Required internship (Pflichtpraktikum)**: student visa doesn't need additional application, can directly do.
- **Voluntary internship (freiwilliges Praktikum)**: student visa holders can full-time work 120 days per year (or half-time 240 days).
- Non-EU master graduates can apply for **EU Blue Card** to convert to FT.
- Google Munich accepts international students but prioritizes EU/local.

### 2.4 Poland (Warsaw)
- Non-EU students with D-type student visa can intern.
- Warsaw international student ratio lower, salary relatively lowest but cost of living also low (see Section 5).

## 3. Interview Process

### 3.1 STEP Process (Lower-Year Undergrad)
1. **Resume screening** (open Sep-Oct, EMEA 2025 cycle 10/25/2024 deadline)
2. **(Some cases) Online OA** — 3 programming questions, some years skip directly to interviews
3. **2 rounds 45-minute technical interview** (remote, Google Meet)
   - Round 1: easy-medium, String/Array/HashMap main
   - Round 2: medium-hard, DP/Graph/Tree
4. **Host Matching (informal chat)** — EMEA specific, team and candidate two-way selection
5. Offer decision usually 2-4 weeks post-interview

### 3.2 SWE Intern Process (Upper-Year/Master/PhD)
1. Resume screening
2. Recruiter call (verify info, visa, availability)
3. **2 rounds technical interview**, each 45 min, medium to medium-hard, often involves graph, tree, DP, edge cases enumeration
4. Host matching
5. Offer

### 3.3 Research Intern Process
1. Resume + research statement
2. Host (potential supervisor) leads technical/research talk
3. 1-2 rounds additional algorithmic interview (some directions skip)
4. Two-way matching

Source: [GeeksforGeeks Google SWE Intern 2025](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/interview-experiences/google-swe-intern-2025-selected/), [Medium STEP 2024 Experience](https://medium.com/@ma2889/google-step-internship-2024-interview-experience-9000c74594b1)

### 6.3 Application Tips
- **Earlier application better**: STEP/SWE intern both rolling review, early Oct submission pass rate several times higher than late Oct.
- **Resume**: highlight competitions (ACM/Codeforces), open source contributions, challenging personal projects. Google recruiters explicitly say "personal projects outside class" big plus.
- **Referral useful but not necessary**: Internal referral helps resume screen but doesn't affect subsequent technical interviews.
- **Multi-location application**: same cycle can apply multiple locations, but each location independent requisition, may cause process confusion, suggest picking 2-3 most desired.
- **STEP priority**: if you meet STEP eligibility (first/second-year undergrad), **must apply STEP not SWE Intern** — competition much smaller, and return rate high.

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

### 7.1 Common Pitfalls
1. **Zurich visa delay**: third-country students after getting Zurich offer, Permit B processing slow, may affect internship start date (some delayed onboarding 2-4 weeks). Suggest urging Google internal immigration team immediately after getting offer.
2. **Host Matching fail**: technical interview passed but host matching didn't match team, offer void. EMEA more serious than US, because each EU office team count originally less. Reddit / Blind 2024 multiple feedback "interview passed but 8 weeks didn't match team finally rejected".
3. **STEP and SWE Intern duplicate application**: same year can only choose one, mixing up will auto-reject second.
4. **Conversion (to NG) not guaranteed**: STEP/SWE Intern gives return offer but not necessarily NG offer. NG conversion needs to go through host matching again, 2023-2024 some interns reported NG conversion rate notably dropped.
5. **Munich working language**: although working language is English, some teams internal meetings mix German, some international students report integration not as good as Zurich/London.

### 7.2 Community Reviews (Blind / 1point3acres / Reddit)
- **Zurich**: "salary ceiling but cost of living too", post-tax single can save 4-5k CHF/month, internship experience extremely helpful for finding FT. 1point3acres multiple 2-year review threads rate 4.5/5.
- **London**: Tech hub, team diversity high (DeepMind / Cloud / Search / Ads), strong social life. But London rent + tax make interns take-home actually not as generous as imagined.
- **Munich**: good work-life balance, team biased hardware / Chrome, limited options if want Web infra.
- **Warsaw**: high cost-effectiveness, extremely low cost of living, young work atmosphere. But career mobility limited, most teams are secondary site.
- **STEP general reputation**: "highest ROI European intern" — being able to get this caliber in first/second year is already top tier. Return offer almost automatic.
