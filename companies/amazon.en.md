### 1.2 Main Internship Product Lines

1. **SDE Intern (Software Development Engineer Intern)**: length 3–6 months (full-time) / 6–12 months (part-time); Bachelor/Master/PhD in progress
2. **Applied Scientist Intern**: 6 months; Master/PhD in progress, lean toward NLP/Vision/RecSys/LLM
3. **AWS Intern**: same recruiting process as SDE Intern, but allocated to AWS BU (Dublin / London / Berlin)
4. **MBA Launch Program (Madrid etc.)**: MBA summer internship + three-year rotation
5. **PhD Graduate Research Program**: hundreds of research internships

### 1.3 Length / Internship Season

- **Summer**: usually 12 weeks, Jun–Sep
- **Winter / off-cycle**: some roles winter intake (e.g. Madrid Winter 2025 data point see §5)
- **European long placement**: some UK roles have 12-month placement (sandwich year), coexisting with summer 12-week

## 3. Interview Process

### 3.1 SDE Intern Standard Process

```
Apply (referral accelerates)
  → OA1 (Debugging, 7 questions / 20 min, across C/C++/Java)
  → OA2 (2 DSA, ~30 min each; total 70–90 min; may add Work Simulation)
  → Work Simulation + Workstyle Assessment (90–180 min; email/IM/video simulation + LP MCQ)
  → 2–3 technical rounds (60 min each; first 15 min LP behavioral, last 45 min Coding LC Medium)
  → Bar Raiser (may merge with technical round; veto power)
  → Offer
```

### 3.2 Applied Scientist Intern Process

```
Apply / ML Challenge / paper referral
  → OA (2 LC Medium DSA + 20 min / 45 LP workstyle)
  → Round 1: DSA & Problem Solving 60 min (with 2 SDE interviewers)
  → Round 2: ML Deep Dive 75 min (with 2 Applied Scientists; includes peer coding)
  → Offer (typically notified within a week)
```

### 3.3 Leadership Principles (LP) Behavioral Interview

**The soul of Amazon recruiting**. 16 LPs (added #15 and #16 in 2021):

1. Customer Obsession
2. Ownership
3. Invent and Simplify
4. Are Right, A Lot
5. Learn and Be Curious
6. Hire and Develop the Best
7. Insist on the Highest Standards
8. Think Big
9. Bias for Action
10. Frugality
11. Earn Trust
12. Dive Deep
13. Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit
14. Deliver Results
15. Strive to be Earth's Best Employer
16. Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility

**Top 6 most frequent in interviews**: Customer Obsession, Ownership, Dive Deep, Deliver Results, Bias for Action, Earn Trust. **Prepare at least 2 STAR stories for each**.

**Intern-level LP high-frequency questions**:
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate and how you resolved it
- Describe the most challenging technical problem you've worked on
- When did you use data to make a decision?
- Tell me about a quick important call you had to make
- Tell me about a mistake you made
- Tell me about a time you used Gen AI (added post-2024, related to Amazon LLM business)
- Describe a challenging project and what made it difficult

### 3.4 Bar Raiser

- Held by trained senior employees not on the hiring team
- **Veto power**: even if hiring manager wants you, Bar Raiser veto = no offer
- Mainly examines whether LP answers are "above current team level", preventing bar drop under hiring pressure
- May not be explicitly labeled in intern interviews, but usually present

### 3.5 OA Details (2024–2025)

- **OA1 — Debugging**: 7 questions × 3 min; locate bug in given erroneous code and fix; languages C/C++/Java
- **OA2 — Coding**: 2 questions LC Easy–Medium; common topics sorting, sliding window, BFS/DFS, hashmap
- **Work Simulation**: simulates Amazon work day (email/IM/video), scores response plans; intern version may include "manager asks you to debug a piece of code + log" task
- **Workstyle Assessment**: single-choice LP situational questions, tests fit with Amazon culture

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

### 7.1 Common Pitfalls
- **OA1 Debugging time pressure**: 7 questions 20 min = avg 3 min/question, unfamiliar with C/C++ syntax = easy to fail
- **LP story reuse**: using same story for different LPs gets identified; Bar Raiser especially sensitive
- **Madrid salary on low end**: $1,668/month tolerable under Spanish prices, but 3x difference from London/Luxembourg, consider net of living cost when choosing location
- **Dublin AWS process long**: experience posts mention full process 4 months; some 8 rounds (3 online + 5 onsite)
- **Bar Raiser culture**: even if all hiring managers like you, Bar Raiser veto = no offer

### 7.2 Community Reviews
- Glassdoor Amazon SDE Intern: difficulty 3/5, 69% positive, avg 58 days hire (US sample 437)
- Glassdoor Amazon SDE Intern UK: avg 48 days (sample 13)
- Glassdoor Amazon Applied Scientist Intern: difficulty 3/5, 71% positive, avg 25 days
- Consensus: **OA difficulty ≤ interview difficulty** (OA is LC Easy–Med, onsite is Medium–Hard); thorough LP prep can compensate for slightly weak technical
- Luxembourg SDE: relocation friendly + international students concentrated, but working language all English, Luxembourg local French/German not mandatory
- Madrid: strong MBA Launch channel, vendor management/business internships more (rather than pure SDE)

### 7.3 Conversion Rate
- Historical data points: high-performing interns usually get graduate offer or second internship; conversion rate no official number, community estimate 60–70%
- Luxembourg data point mentions "if internship performance good, can directly go graduate role after graduation"
