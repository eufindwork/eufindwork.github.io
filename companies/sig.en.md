## 3. Interview Process

| Round | Content | Duration | Notes |
|------|------|------|------|
| 1. Online Assessment | **SIG Quantitative Evaluation (SQE)**: 17 math + probability questions | ~60 min | Includes linear system, expected value, grid path-finding; "20 min" short version for prescreen |
| 2. HR Phone Interview | vibe check + 3 probability / EV questions | 45 min | Not pure BQ, has quant screen |
| 3. Trader Interview (Round 1) | 1 trader, analytical + behavioral + brain teaser (decision-making) | 60 min | poker / cards / dice / coins; heavy EV calculation |
| 4. Take-Home Project | 48h, any language, analyze trade sequence + propose strategy | 48 hours | Unique to SIG, vs Optiver/IMC don't have |
| 5. Final Onsite (Dublin) | trader + HR "good cop bad cop", heavy EV + MM game based on estimated fair value | half to full day | Flight + hotel paid by SIG |
| Total process | **2-3 months, 5 rounds** | very slow process | WSO + Tradermath unanimous evaluation |

**SIG-specific notes**:
- **Decision theory + Poker**: SIG's most famous interview "assume you're playing poker, ..." series — candidates need to understand expected value + Kelly criterion + Bayesian update
- **48h take-home** is rare in quant circle: a historical trade dataset + you write a strategy + report; SIG uses this to assess "learn while doing" ability
- **SWE track** OA leans algorithm (HackerRank / Codility) + system design, vs Trader track SQE leans brain teaser
- **Slow process is public pain point**: Glassdoor + WSO multiple posts complaining "2-3 month wait", recommend parallel application Optiver + IMC

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **Slow process (2-3 months)**: one of the longest in industry; WSO + Glassdoor extensive "wait time too long" complaints; recommend parallel application Optiver + IMC
- **48h take-home is entry pain point**: 48 hours high intensity, difficult during enrolled student + full-time interview overlap
- **Decision theory heavy**: not reading Thinking in Bets / Kelly criterion / Bayesian directly hurts
- **Dublin cost of living extremely high**: city center 1-bed €2,000+/month, grad base €70-100K actually less disposable than Amsterdam Optiver (even nominally higher TC)
- **No 30% ruling equivalent**: Ireland personal tax equivalent to NL without ruling → actual after-tax gap Amsterdam Optiver/IMC + 30% ruling may overtake Dublin SIG
- **Private company no RSU**: all cash + discretionary bonus; bonus volatility PnL sensitive (2022 reduction 42% per head public report) [Source (search result): https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/a-hft-firm-that-paid-1-1m-per-head-in-2022-saw-pay-fall-42]
- **Dublin Chinese / Indian student friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — CSEP process slightly more lenient than NL Kennismigrant, but Dublin culture "East Coast US-ification" is high
- **Glassdoor 4.1/5**: Same as Flow Traders; complaints focused on "high work intensity (60-70h/week common)" + "slow interview process"
