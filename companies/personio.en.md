## Intern Path

- **Role naming**: **Internship (m/f/d)** — 40h/week full-time, departments varied
- **Length**: usually 3-6 months
- **Locations**: **Munich, Berlin, Dublin, Amsterdam** (4 cities) [Personio Students & Graduates page (search-confirmed): https://www.personio.com/about-personio/careers/students-graduates/]
- **Application window + deadline**: rolling, year-round (peak Mar-May hiring summer cohort, Sep-Nov hiring winter cohort)
- **Salary (stipend gross/month)**:
  - **Munich/Berlin** intern €1,800-2,400/month (based on Glassdoor + TechPays Germany intern level ~€24,863/year mean = ~€2,070/month)
  - **Dublin** intern €2,200-2,800/month (IE tech intern range)
  - **Amsterdam** intern €2,300-2,800/month (NL student intern range; 2026-06 retry: Personio.com/students-graduates confirms Amsterdam as one of 4 intern cities (40h/wk), but stipend not explicitly listed on careers page; Glassdoor Personio Amsterdam intern salary page has no data points. NL master intern prevailing €1,400-2,600/month, much higher than Glassdoor Amsterdam intern average €665/yr figure — Personio SaaS mid-tier should reasonably fit €2,300-2,800 range)
- **Eligibility (education, local residency requirement)**:
  - Enrolled undergraduate / masters, school official enrolment proof
  - DE requires Immatrikulationsbescheinigung; AMS goes via Nuffic convention (perfect for UvA)
  - English fluent, German / Dutch not required
- **Sponsor policy (intern)**:
  - **Germany (MUC/BER)**: international students via §16 student visa (must be enrolled in DE university) or **EU student status**; non-EU students from NL doing cross-border intern need Nuffic + temp residence, **does not directly sponsor intern**
  - **Dublin**: Atypical Working Scheme (intern < 90 days no permit, > 90 days needs permit, Personio historically does not broadly sponsor intern)
  - **Amsterdam ⭐**: **NL student → Nuffic convention perfect match, zero friction for UvA students**, user's best path
- **Process**: apply → recruiter call → hire manager → small case study / coding → team fit; 3-4 weeks
- **Conversion to FT rate**: Personio internal conversion ~50-60% (Glassdoor review: "great pipeline for full-time")

## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **Supports non-EU students**: ✅ yes (FT), intern friendliest at AMS office for UvA students
- **Intern vs FT sponsor differences**:
  - **Intern AMS ⭐**: UvA student visa + Nuffic, **zero friction**, Personio HR cooperates with signing Nuffic
  - **Intern DE**: must be enrolled in DE university (§16 student visa), UvA students doing cross-border intern need Nuffic + temp residence — complex
  - **Intern Dublin**: Atypical Working Scheme, no permit needed within 90 days
  - **FT (any office)**: Personio covers full process, EU Blue Card / IE CSEP / NL Kennismigrant
- **Known sponsorship cases**: Personio listed as active sponsor DE (nextleveljobs); LinkedIn multiple India/China engineer cases
- **2026 thresholds**:
  - **Germany EU Blue Card** €45,934 (shortage occupation IT/SWE, 2026) — Personio Junior starting salary €60K+ far exceeds
  - **Ireland CSEP** €40,904/year (post-2026-03, AIP raise €4K) — Dublin Junior meets
  - **NL Kennismigrant** €5,688/month (2026, < 30 yrs reduced to €4,171/month ≈ €54K/year) — AMS Junior estimated ~€60K base meets

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

- **2024-2025 layoff**: 2024 Q2 layoff ~15-20% (300+ people), 2025 another round; hiring half-frozen, "expected to do more with less" (Glassdoor) [Glassdoor Personio Munich Reviews]
- **Munich rating**: 3.3/5 overall, WLB 3.6, D&I 3.6 (Glassdoor)
- **HR SaaS domain learning curve**: payroll / time tracking / compliance needs understanding of local regulations (DE Steuer, NL ZZP etc.), engineering problems are not just CRUD
- **Stock down round**: 2024 internal valuation significantly lowered, 2026 IPO plan delayed (vs previously 2024 IPO target)
- **AMS office new + small**: AMS opened 2022, ~50 people (vs Munich ~1500), team mainly commercial + SDR, engineering roles few — **user applying AMS intern prioritize data / product, SWE intern in AMS is rare**
- **Dublin office**: opened 2021, ~80 people EMEA expansion team

## 8. For UvA Students (NL student visa) — Specific Assessment ⭐⭐ (strongly recommended)

### Intern viability
- **Amsterdam intern ⭐**: ✅ **zero friction** — UvA student visa + Nuffic convention, Personio AMS HR direct connection; **only note**: AMS office leans commercial/data intern, SWE intern in AMS rare, confirm JD location before applying
- **Munich intern**: ⚠️ high friction — must be enrolled in DE university (§16 student visa), UvA students doing cross-border stage via Nuffic convention theoretically viable but Personio HR handles case-by-case, complex. Recommend applying AMS office directly.
- **Berlin intern**: same as Munich, complex
- **Dublin intern**: medium — IE Atypical Working Scheme allows no permit within 90 days, > 90 days needs permit (Personio historically does not broadly sponsor intern)

### FT viability
- **Post-graduation Associate SWE AMS**: ✅ highly viable — Personio uses NL Kennismigrant €4,171/month (<30 reduced); AMS Junior estimated €60K+ far exceeds threshold
- **Post-graduation Munich/Berlin/Dublin**: ✅ viable — uses EU Blue Card / CSEP, Personio covers
- **NL zoekjaar synergy**: AMS office perfectly matches zoekjaar path, no need to give up NL residence

### Expected friction
- Intern AMS: **zero** ⭐⭐
- Intern MUC/BER/Dublin: **medium-high**
- FT AMS: **zero** ⭐⭐
- FT other cities: **low**

### Suggestions
- **Primary strategy**: AMS office intern (prioritize data/product, SWE depends on luck) → FT conversion → stay in NL
- **Second choice**: post-graduation apply AMS Associate SWE FT (rolling, year-round)
- **Avoid**: MUC/BER/Dublin intern (friction outweighs return)
- **Timeline**:
  - Now to 2026-08: apply AMS summer intern before summer (Mar-May)
  - From 2026-09: in pre-graduation semester continuously monitor AMS Associate SWE / Data Eng / Product roles
  - 2027-06 graduation: apply Personio AMS FT during zoekjaar
- **Personio is the strongest candidate outside the 4 ⭐ active applications** — AMS office perfectly fits, and although company has had layoffs it's still EMEA expanding, student program complete
