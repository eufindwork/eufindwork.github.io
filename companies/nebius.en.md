## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

**Core conclusion (official wording): "Not at this stage" — Nebius Early Talent Program currently does NOT provide visa / relocation support**

| Dimension | Policy (from official FAQ wording) |
|------|------------------------|
| Supports intern visa | **No.** "Not at this stage. However, if there is sufficient demand, we may consider offering visa or relocation support in future Early Talent programs." |
| Requires work authorization | Yes — "International candidates must be legally authorized to work in their role's country" |
| Full-time sponsorship after conversion | Nebius is IND recognised sponsor (Nebius B.V., registered in NL), full-time SWE can use Kennismigrant (Highly Skilled Migrant) + 30% ruling; but intern stipend usually doesn't meet kennismigrant threshold, uses internship work permit |
| Degree / age requirements | No formal requirements, but program mainly targets "final-year students and recent graduates" |

**Viable paths for Chinese students**:
1. **Already studying in EU (with student visa)**: can do Amsterdam / Berlin internship via school internship agreement (tripartite), no separate work permit needed
2. **Already studying in Finland**: can apply for Mäntsälä Data Center Technician (hardware, not SWE-fit)
3. **Not in EU**: current cycle nearly closed; only path is to apply for "Remote EU" / "Remote US" tagged AI Researcher roles — but need target country work authorization first, Nebius doesn't arrange
4. **Alternative paths**: apply to Toloka / TripleTen / Avride (same group but independent recruiting) — different BUs may have different policies
5. **Play Codeforces / Kaggle**: Nebius 2025-2026 recruits via Codeforces "Early Talents" collaboration round + token credits, one of the few "passive exposure" channels for non-EU students

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

### Process layer
- **Extremely low offer rate**: Taro shows Amsterdam SWE 0% pass (n=3), Glassdoor reviews also confirm "extremely selective"
- **75-day hire cycle** (Glassdoor company average) — slow tier in Europe
- **Algorithm standard benchmarked to ICPC**: not "LC 200 problems can solve"; topics lean ad-hoc / math structure / optimization / edge cases (typical Yandex style)
- **Feedback not transparent**: multiple interview reports say "all solved but still rejected, no specific feedback"

### Culture layer (Glassdoor 46 reviews composite)
| Review direction | Content |
|---------|------|
| Positive | "Great team with experienced developers"; "Good salary and interesting projects"; AI infra is truly cutting-edge |
| Negative | "Toxic culture" / "Managers are bosses not leaders"; "Horrible internal processes"; **"Very Russian centric, limited English proficiency among most employees"** (this repeatedly appears, real friction point for non-Russian candidates) |
| Overall rating | **4.2 / 5** (46 reviews) |

### Known "traps"
1. **Official explicitly does not arrange intern visa** → don't hope for exception, even Codeforces red name won't get one
2. **Yandex heritage double-edged**: engineering culture strong but language / communication culture still in transition; manager English fluency varies
3. **Early Talent Program 2026 is "first edition"**: process / mentorship / conversion paths have no historical data to check
4. **Office mandatory in-person**: 2-3 months entire program office-based, must physically live in Amsterdam / Berlin / Paris / Helsinki
5. **Affiliate companies (Toloka / TripleTen / Avride) recruit separately**, can't cross-apply; getting into Nebius doesn't easily let you transfer to affiliates
6. **Stipend specific number still a black box**: official only says "junior FTE comparable", must ask gross / net / housing / 30% ruling applicability clearly in final round before applying

### International student friendliness
- **Policy-wise unfriendly**: doesn't arrange visa
- **Practically**: already studying in EU (especially NL/DE/FI) + strong algorithm background + fluent English = friendly
- **Russian / Russian heritage background**: historically friendly (Yandex era continued)
- **Chinese students**: algorithm competition background (ICPC/Codeforces) is the most effective "language-independent" signal
