## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **Paris internship**: French convention de stage tripartite agreement, school + company + student; non-EU students need French/EU enrollment; intern stipend (gratification) statutory €659/mo minimum, Squarepoint gives €3-4k/mo
- **London internship**: Tier 4 Student Visa, vacation full-time OK; no separate work permit needed
- **Paris full-time**: Passeport Talent Salarié Qualifié = €39,582/year (2026, new fixed amount, no longer SMIC multiple); Squarepoint Paris QD base €70K+ far exceeds → compliant
- **London full-time**: Skilled Worker £41,700 (2026); Squarepoint UK QD £85.5K+ → compliant; Squarepoint UK Ltd has Tier 2 sponsor license
- **French requirement**: Paris office working language is English + French mix (technical internal mostly English, admin / HR has French); French B2 is a plus but not a gate
- **Path recommendation**: For non-EU students with both Paris + London offers, Paris with Passeport Talent 4-year multi-year permit is the longest in Europe, UK Skilled Worker 5 years to ILR is a nearer endpoint — choose based on long-term plans

## 3. Interview Process

| Round | Content | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. CV screen | recruiter call introducing role | 30 min | Very fast, reply in 1-3 days |
| 1. OA | Python-heavy: data structures + probability + stats | 90 min | HackerRank, all Python (even QD doesn't force C++) |
| 2. Tech Phone 1 | algorithm + math/finance + deep Python | 60 min | LeetCode med, about 50% pure algo |
| 3. Tech Phone 2 | system design lite + KDB+/q (if QD) / stats + signal design (if QR) | 60-75 min | KDB+/q knowledge is a big plus but not required |
| 4. Senior Quant/Dev chat | problem-solving depth + cultural fit | 60 min | "conversational with senior" |
| 5. Final / Onsite | 4-5 employees mix, including head-of-team / hiring manager | half day | London or Paris, depending on base location |
| 6. Offer | within 1-2 weeks | — | Average 31 days end-to-end |

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

- **Relatively fast process**: Average 31 days (faster than QRT 4-8 weeks / Akuna months)
- **Python-heavy OA**: Even QD/QR paths use Python OA, not C++; but full-time QD writes a lot of KDB+/q + C++
- **Glassdoor 39.8% positive (medium-low)**: Main complaints are (1) slow recruiter follow up (2) opaque final round decisions (3) opaque bonus — common French-rooted fund issues
- **French not mandatory**: Paris team working language is primarily English, French B2 is nice-to-have
- **International student friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Paris + London + Madrid + Zug + Geneva multi-site → many visa choices)
- **vs QRT**: Squarepoint older (2014 vs QRT 2018) + slightly smaller scale but similar AUM + culture more "low-key academic"; QRT expands hiring more / lower engineer ratio / heavier deferred comp
