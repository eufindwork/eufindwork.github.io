## 3. Interview Process

- **Total rounds**: 4 rounds, ~4 weeks (some candidates up to 2 months) [Source (verified opened): https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Unbabel-Lisbon-Interview-Questions-EI_IE868834.0,7_IL.8,14_IM1121.htm]
- **Each round format**:
  1. HR screen (recruiter knowledgeable about engineering team, English)
  2. **Take-home option A**: 3-hour onsite with 3 interviewers
     **Take-home option B**: 1-week programming challenge + two 45-min onsite interviews (recommend B, more prep room)
  3. Technical interview with engineer (60 min, discuss challenge + experience)
  4. Final / team fit
- **Question type distribution**: Take-home / coding 50% / Technical discussion 25% / BQ 15% / Domain (NLP / translation) 10% (NLP/ML roles add)
- **Difficulty assessment**: **2.6/5 (Glassdoor)** — simpler than GAFAM/Datadog, practical-oriented
- **OA exists**: Yes (take-home challenge) or onsite either-or

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

- **Take-home 1 week** vs 3-hr onsite — choosing 1-week take-home gives more prep room but time-consuming, choosing onsite is high-pressure but quick
- Unbabel **business pivot risk**: experienced layoffs 2024 (focus customer service AI vs general translation), company trajectory still rebuilding
- Lisbon salary far below France / Germany, but NHR tax + low cost of living + climate compensate
- Engineering Medium / blog inactive (vs OutSystems engineering), tech brand on the weaker side
- International student friendliness **extremely high** — explicit visa sponsor + relocation, English-only, diverse nationalities
