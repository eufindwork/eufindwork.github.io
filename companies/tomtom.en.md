  - **Software Engineer Intern** (Amsterdam / Berlin / Łódź)

  - Marketing / Communications / Finance / Web Designer Intern etc.

## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **Key limitation (multiple Glassdoor reports)**: **"TomTom is currently only able to interview candidates who hold a visa that doesn't require sponsorship"**
  - This means TomTom's **intern visa support is very limited**, more conservative under recent layoff pressure
  - Actually feasible paths:
    - EU/EEA students → apply directly
    - Non-EU students already studying at NL/DE/PL institutions → apply via study residence + Nuffic internship agreement
    - **Non-EU students not at EU institutions → almost cannot apply for internships directly**
- **Erasmus+ / school exchange agreements**: TomTom historically accepted Erasmus students (e.g. Spanish exchange interning in Netherlands), but relies on school-signed internship agreements rather than TomTom directly sponsoring visa
- **Full-time conversion**: TomTom is still IND registered sponsor, but recent senior hiring freeze magnitude is large, 30% ruling applies to full-time meeting threshold

## 3. Interview Process

TomTom process relatively **lightweight**, total 4–6 weeks, shorter than Booking / Adyen / Uber.

### Standard process (6 steps, official how-we-hire page)
1. **Application Review** — resume screening
2. **Initial Recruiter Call** — 30 min, experience + motivation + expectations
3. **Optional Technical Assessment** — short online coding task (not all roles mandatory)
4. **Technical Interview** — **1 hour**, typically includes:
   - 30 min behavioral (based on TomTom leadership behaviors)
   - 30 min live coding (problem solving, screen share)
5. **Interview Loop** — 3–4 rounds, different interviewers, involving system design + leadership evaluation
6. **Final Decision & Offer**

### Technical examination focus
- **Software Engineer**: C++ / Python / Scala / Java; **data-oriented** (Big Data, distributed systems); map/navigation related algorithms (Dijkstra, A*, R-tree, graph) frequently appear
- **Data Engineer**: Python + Spark + SQL; AWS / Azure experience a plus; map ETL pipeline thinking
- **Map Data Sourcing**: GIS-leaning, QGIS / shapefile / OSM experience
- **Data Scientist**: Python, numpy/sklearn/pytorch, occasionally CV (satellite image segmentation is a real TomTom intern project)

### Common behavioral questions
- "Tell me about a time you debugged a complex issue"
- "How do you handle disagreement with a teammate"
- "Why TomTom / why maps"
- TomTom leadership behaviors (Customer obsession, Take ownership, Be a team player, Grow yourself)

### Recommended strategy
1. **Visa status self-check**: confirm before applying that you don't need TomTom sponsor (have Netherlands study residence OR EU passport); otherwise wasted application
2. **Emphasize map/geospatial experience**: GIS, OSM, GeoJSON, R-tree, Spatial SQL — these are more worth highlighting at TomTom than general algorithms
3. **C++ / Spark / Python** multi-language a plus; TomTom is one of the few NL companies still emphasizing C++ (Map SDK, embedded navigation)
4. **Berlin / Łódź / Madrid are easier offer alternatives** (Amsterdam HQ has highest hiring bar, other hubs have more slots)
5. **Erasmus+ / school agreements**: if European in-school student, applying through school partnership has higher success rate than direct application

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

### Warnings (important)
- **2024 large-scale layoffs**: 300 people (one source 500), mainly affecting application layer, sales, customer support; company calls "AI pivot", essentially contraction
- **Strategy uncertain**: device business receding, now 90%+ revenue from B2B map licensing (Volkswagen, Renault, Apple); future fierce competition with Google/HERE
- **Stock weak**: 1 year -26.7%, investor confidence weak
- **Strict visa sponsor**: this is the biggest difference from Booking / Adyen / ASML — TomTom **explicitly unwilling to sponsor intern visa**
- **Internship project quality varies**: some interns reported "projects on the edge, not necessarily shipping"
- **Internationalization level**: Amsterdam HQ international but Łódź/Pune more local

### Advantages
- **Map/geospatial** domain is niche experience, jumping to Google Maps / HERE / Apple Maps / Mapbox is valued
- **Amsterdam HQ location**: next to Central Station, top-tier commute experience
- **Lightweight process**: 4–6 weeks vs Adyen 6–8 weeks, suits time-pressed candidates
- **wellbeing culture**: working hours more friendly than Adyen / Uber, work-life balance reviews above average (Glassdoor median rating)

### Pros and cons
- (+) Short process, fast pace
- (+) Map / autonomous driving niche experience
- (-) Visa policy nearly dealbreaker for non-EU students
- (-) Company financial and strategic downtrend, intern long-term value uncertain
- (-) Low stipend
- (-) Chinese community interview experiences nearly zero, information asymmetry
