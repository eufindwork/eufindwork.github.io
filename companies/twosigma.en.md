## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **UK Skilled Worker 2026 threshold**: base £41,700 or SOC code going rate. Two Sigma SWE / Quant starting salary (£105k+) far exceeds threshold, no base-line issue.
- **CoS (Certificate of Sponsorship)**: Two Sigma UK entity is sponsor licence holder, historically stable in issuing CoS for graduates + interns.
- **Internship visa path**: Student identity holding **Tier 4 / Student visa** can do dissertation placement / summer internship while studying at UK school (≤20h/week during term / full-time vacation); non-UK international students need to go through **Standard Visitor + Permitted Paid Engagement** or **Tier 5 GAE** — Two Sigma historically sponsors short-term visa for NYC HQ students to intern in London.
- **Graduate Route (PSW)**: UK school graduates have 2-year PSW, during which Two Sigma can defer sponsorship, transitioning to Skilled Worker afterwards.
- **PhD students**: Quant Researcher intern has many PhD slots, visa generally not an issue.
- **Limitations**: Two Sigma doesn't discriminate based on visa status during interview / resume review, but OA + technical interview bar higher than most Big Tech.

## 3. Interview Process

| Round | Content | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0. OA (Online Assessment) | HackerRank, 2-3 LeetCode medium-hard + probability/statistics concept questions; Quant Research intern seen linear interpolation + daily temperature prediction | 90-120 min |
| 1. Recruiter Screen | Logistics + CV walk-through | 20-30 min |
| 2. Technical Phone Screen | 60 min codepair (Python/C++/Java) + data structures/algorithms (big O, hash, recursion, tree/graph) | 60 min |
| 3. Onsite / Virtual Superday | 3× 60-min technical (2 coding + 1 system design occasionally) + behavioral; Quant Research more probability/statistics/ML model evaluation | half day |
| 4. Final / Leadership | Manager / senior chat on fit + project experience | 45-60 min |

Notes:
- **Forbidden ChatGPT / any AI tool** — interviewer explicitly states, if deemed used directly DQ [Source (verified opened): https://www.twosigma.com/careers/interviewing-at-two-sigma/interviewing-for-software-engineering/]
- System design occasionally appears (focus on scalability + fault tolerance), but SWE intern usually doesn't test
- Signature brain teaser / probability questions mainly appear in Quant Researcher track, SWE track leans LeetCode + CS fundamentals

## 6. Application Channels and Advice

- **Official submission**: [careers.twosigma.com](https://careers.twosigma.com/) — London roles use SearchJobs filter, intern usually explicitly labeled "Internship".
- **Referral**: connect LinkedIn London office Modeling Engineer / Quant Researcher; Two Sigma referrals carry significant weight (few people, strong internal discussion culture), pass rate clearly higher than pure cold apply.
- **CV focus**:
  - Math/stats/CS strong academic record (top school + high GPA is hard currency)
  - Python + numerical experience (NumPy/pandas/scikit-learn)
  - Any ML / system layer (Spark / Kafka / k8s) experience
  - Quant Research track: papers / competitions (Kaggle / Putnam) > internships
- **Tier-equivalent alternatives**: Jane Street, Citadel, Citadel Securities, DRW, Jump Trading, Hudson River Trading (HRT) — these are common cross-interview set; G-Research / Man Group / Millennium / D.E. Shaw also commonly cross-applied.

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

- **Relatively slow process**: 4-8 weeks from OA to final offer, slower than Jane Street / HRT (~2-3 weeks fast track), but faster than D.E. Shaw.
- **Zero tolerance for AI tools**: multiple candidates reported DQ for suspected Copilot / ChatGPT use. Codepair is real-person monitored environment, don't try to cut corners.
- **London office info sparse**: most interview experiences from NYC, London-specific data sparse; actively ask recruiter about "London team structure + bonus variation".
- **Glassdoor summary**: global 4.2/5, engineering culture positive ("smart colleagues, low ego"), complaints concentrated on NYC HQ-centric decisions + steep tooling learning curve.
- **International student friendliness**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — UK entity stable sponsor, but bar extremely high; PhD students London Quant Research admission rate slightly higher than NYC (smaller application pool).
