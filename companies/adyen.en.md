## 2. Visa & International Student Policy

- **Adyen's strength**: is a key Dutch IND **recognised sponsor**, handles 150–200+ visa applications annually, high approval rate
- **Graduate Program supports** H-1B (US) / Skilled Worker (UK) / Kennismigrant (NL) globally
- **Interns**:
  - Non-EU students already enrolled in Dutch institutions → student residence permit suffices, Adyen only needs to sign tri-party internship agreement
  - Non-EU students at institutions in other countries → length > 90 days requires GVVA (combined permit), Adyen usually accommodates, but master thesis interns more welcomed
- **Relocation**: official provides free visa + first 3 months housing for graduates; intern relocation support **varies by role**, confirm at offer stage
- **30% ruling**: interns **do not qualify**; full-time graduates may qualify depending on salary threshold

## 3. Interview Process

Adyen is **one of the European fintech firms with the longest interview process and heaviest cultural fit weighting**. SWE path uses **take-home engineering assignment** as the core differentiator (vs. pure LeetCode companies).

### Standard Process (4–6 weeks, 5–6 rounds)
1. **Recruiter Call** (30 min, cultural fit + motivation + Adyen Formula intro)
2. **Hiring Manager Chat** (30–45 min, resume deep dive, why Adyen)
3. **Technical Assessment** (one of two, depending on team/seniority):
   - **HackerRank online test**: 1–2 medium difficulty algorithm questions (sliding window, hashmap, tree traversal)
   - **Take-home Assignment**: build a small payment-related service, usually 5–7 day submission window; graded on quality (code style / tests / API design / error handling) rather than LeetCode optimization
4. **Take-home Review / Pair Programming**: discuss take-home design choices with 2 engineers, extend functionality or refactor live
5. **Adyen Formula Interview** (**core**, 45–60 min): behavioral interview around Adyen's eight principles (detailed below)
6. **Final Leadership Round**: VP or above, examining culture fit and long-term alignment

### Adyen Formula (8 Principles) — Behavioral Interview Backbone
1. **Winning is more fun**
2. **Building a great team**
3. **The Adyen Formula** (unified approach)
4. **Choose direct over indirect** (direct communication culture)
5. **Launch fast and iterate** (typical question: "Tell me about a time you shipped an imperfect MVP to gather data")
6. **Including different perspectives**
7. **Pick the company over the team**
8. **Ethical business** (typical question: "Describe a situation where you chose an ethical path over a more profitable one")

**STAR method is standard**, but Adyen interviewers dislike "canned answers", expecting **authentic + self-reflective**.

### Internship Path Details
- Thesis intern: process compressed to 2–3 rounds (recruiter + hiring manager + culture round), take-home usually omitted because thesis proposal substitutes
- NextGen part-time: lighter process, usually 1–2 rounds suffice, focus on on-the-spot reasoning

## 7. Pitfalls & Community Reviews

### Warning Points
- **Long process**: 4–6 weeks, occasionally stretches to 8 weeks, not suitable for those urgently needing offers
- **Cultural fit weight extremely high**: same technical level, slightly weaker culture round = direct reject
- **Stipend on the low end**: vs. Amsterdam cost of living (€1,800/month one-bedroom shared is average), living on stipend alone is tight
- **Office-only trend**: post-2024 Adyen explicitly requires office attendance, some interns report it's very intense, "9-to-7 is the norm"
- **Not a leetcode company**: grinding 1000 problems but unable to write production-quality code → easy to fail on take-home
- Feedback: some teams' "too direct" culture makes international newcomers uncomfortable, mental preparation needed

### Advantages
- **Strong visa support**: one of the most stable paths for non-EU students into the Dutch tech scene
- **Fintech depth**: payment system underlying experience, strong resume value (jumping to Stripe / Block / Klarna all advantageous)
- **No internal leveling competition**: no American leveling (L3/L4/L5), more egalitarian culture
- **Amsterdam HQ location unbeatable**: Rokin 154, walking distance to Dam Square, top-tier commuting experience

### Pros & Cons
- (+) Stable industry position, top client list, strong resume endorsement
- (+) Not a leetcode heavy hitter, engineering-savvy people have advantage
- (-) Stipend not competitive, choose Booking / Databricks for stipend
- (-) Long process, culture fit subjective
