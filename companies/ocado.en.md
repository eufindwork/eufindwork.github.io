## Intern Path

- **Role naming**: "Software Engineering Internship" / "Technology Summer Internship" — Hatfield (12 weeks, summer)
- **Length**: 10-12 weeks summer (June-Aug)
- **Application window**: opens Oct-Dec, offers Jan-Feb
- **Salary (stipend gross/month GBP)**: ~£2,500-£3,000/month (= ~£30K p.a. prorated)
- **Eligibility**: enrolled undergraduate (penultimate year) / Masters CS / Robotics / Math / Physics
- **Sponsor policy (intern)**:
  - UK Tier 4 / Student visa OK
  - **NL student visa**: cannot directly do UK intern (company does not open intern SW sponsorship, no historical case)
  - Athens / Barcelona / Sofia / Krakow office: no public intern posting historically
- **Process**: CV → video interview (6 questions, company values) → online tests (situational judgment + numerical / multi-tasking) → assessment centre (group task + 2 technical tasks including coding, **no interview/presentation** for SWE candidates)
- **Conversion rate to FT**: medium-high (~50%), designed to funnel to grad scheme

## 2. Visa and International Student Policy

- **2026 UK thresholds**: Skilled Worker £41,700, NEC £33,400, SOC 2134 Software Developer going rate ~£49,400
- **Ocado license status** [worksponsors.co.uk + huntukvisasponsors verified opened]:
  - Ocado Innovation Limited, Ocado Operating Ltd, Ocado Retail Limited all hold Skilled Worker license, A-rated
- **Key issues**:
  - **Software Engineering Grad** (Tech): possibly sponsor (base at £41,700 threshold, NEC + new entrant discount can pass)
  - **Commercial Solutions Grad**: **explicitly does not sponsor** (huntukvisasponsors marked) — base low + SOC not eligible
  - **Engineering Grad** (hardware): some SOC codes (mechanical / automation engineer) viable, case-by-case
  - **Finance Grad / UX Grad**: typically not sponsored (ACA path / UX SOC code limitations)
- **Policy hard edge**: company only covers "company-specific costs" — does not pay visa fee + IHS (£1,035/yr * 5 = ~£5K out-of-pocket for grad)
- **GBM path**: not applicable (Ocado is UK legal entity)
- **NL student visa**: UK intern does not open sponsorship, NL/EU office has no intern posting

## 7. Pitfalls and Community Reviews

- **Stock price long depressed**: 2021 peak £29, 2026 ~£3, sharesave limited value
- **Commercial Solutions Grad is a trap** for international student: does not sponsor + includes night shifts (CFC operation rotation)
- **Tech vs Retail culture difference**: Ocado Technology (Hatfield main building) leans traditional UK eng culture, WLB OK (~40h), but slow promotion. Ocado Retail (M&S JV) more commercial
- **OSP customer attrition risk**: Norwegian Kroger delayed OSP deployment, Casino (France) withdrew, 2023-2024 multiple customers publicly complained — affects long-term tech roadmap
- **Hatfield commute**: 20-25 min train from London Kings Cross, staff shuttle bus from station. But Hatfield itself boring, most employees hybrid living in London
- **Pros**: genuinely doing large-scale robotics + warehouse automation, learn deep tech (1000+ bot coordination, real-time bin picking), not typical CRUD app

## 8. For UvA Students (NL student visa) — Specific Assessment ⭐

- **Core trade-off**: Ocado is the **most traditional UK grad scheme style** on this list — clear structure but conservative sponsor policy + grad base at SW threshold
- **Intern viability**: **zero**
  - UK intern does not open sponsorship, no historical international case
  - EU offices (Athens, Barcelona, Sofia, Krakow) no public intern postings
- **FT viability**:
  - **Software Engineering Grad ⭐ viable**: 
    - Hatfield base £38-42K at £41,700 SW threshold (need NEC + new entrant discount)
    - Ocado historically sponsors international grads ~10-15% cohort
    - Glassdoor + GradSignal list as active sponsor
    - Assessment: **medium** viability
  - **Commercial Solutions Grad**: **don't pick** — huntukvisasponsors explicitly marks no sponsor
  - **Engineering Grad (hardware)**: case-by-case, not recommended to gamble
  - **UX / Finance Grad**: no sponsor
- **EU office path**: 
  - Athens / Barcelona / Sofia / Krakow have SWE FT roles — friendly to EU passport holders
  - NL student post-graduation using zoekjaar to switch to EU path viable (Spain / Greece / Poland SWE paths)
- **Expected friction**: 
  - Hatfield SWE Grad: **medium** (possible sponsor + base at threshold)
  - Other grad roles: **very high** (blocked)
- **Suggestions**: 
  - Only apply **Software Engineering Graduate Programme** (don't touch other grads)
  - In application directly write visa status in cover letter to let HR decide sponsor pipeline
  - Backup EU offices (Athens SWE / Barcelona SWE) — NL student post-graduation path
  - **Important**: Ocado does not pay visa fee + IHS, need to reserve ~£5K cash for visa
  - Backup: Snyk London (most stable on this list), Datadog London (sponsor friendly)
  - **Realistic assessment**: Ocado SWE Grad fits as **medium-priority backup**, don't treat as main pipeline (sponsor probability ~30-40%)
